ABOUT DETAILERFIT

Software research for operators who would rather finish cars than compare pricing pages.

DetailerFit is an independent research project focused first on solo mobile detailers and small detailing crews in the United States.

Why this site exists

Detailing software is split between detailing-specific platforms and broad field-service products. The useful question is rarely “which product has the most features?” It is “which product removes the most friction from my actual week at a price I can justify?”

What we publish

  • Current pricing and plan-limit research
  • Workflow-based comparisons
  • Head-to-head decision guides
  • A rules-based software finder
  • Clear separation between research and hands-on testing

Editorial promise

We will not invent firsthand experience, customer results, or product testing. When we rely on a vendor’s official information, we say so. When we later test a product directly, we will label the test and describe the workflow used.
