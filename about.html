ABOUT DETAILERFIT

Software research for operators who would rather finish cars than compare pricing pages.

DetailerFit is an independent research project focused first on solo mobile detailers and small detailing crews in the United States.

Why this site exists

Detailing software is split between automotive-specialist platforms and broad field-service products. The useful question is rarely “which product has the most features?” It is “which plan removes the most friction from my actual week at a cost I can justify?”

What makes DetailerFit different

  • Plan-level comparisons: we look at the first tier that contains the workflow you need, not only the advertised starting price.
  • Bottleneck-first decisions: booking, routing, team size, device fit and job volume can change the recommendation.
  • Research labels: we do not present vendor-document research as hands-on testing.
  • Commercial separation: affiliate commissions do not buy ranking positions.
  • Corrections are welcome: vendors and readers can send current official documentation when something changes.

What we publish

Current pricing and plan-limit research, workflow comparisons, head-to-head decision guides and a rules-based Software Finder.

What we do not claim

We do not invent customer results, personal product usage or “tested” labels. When hands-on testing is added later, the test workflow and date will be stated explicitly.
