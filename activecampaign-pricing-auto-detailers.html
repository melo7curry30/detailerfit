How ActiveCampaign pricing works

ActiveCampaign does not have one fixed monthly price for every customer. The live pricing experience varies with contact count, currency, billing cadence and the products you select. Its FAQ currently states that pricing starts at $15 for accounts with fewer than 1,000 contacts.

For a detailing business, the practical pricing question is not only “what is the cheapest plan?” It is “which plan supports the automation depth, segmentation and integrations needed for the workflow I actually intend to run?”

What changes your real monthly cost?

  • Contact count: the number of contacts is a major pricing input, so cleaning stale or duplicate records matters.
  • Billing cadence: annual and monthly pricing can differ.
  • Plan tier: Starter's five-action automation limit may push a real lifecycle workflow toward Plus or higher.
  • Add-ons: CRM, SMS, reporting, transactional email or other optional capabilities can increase spend.
  • Integration costs: connecting your booking, forms or operational CRM may involve another paid tool or middleware.

Starter vs Plus for a small detailing business

Starter is reasonable when the goal is to validate email capture, a basic welcome sequence or a simple follow-up. The five-action limit per automation is the key constraint to test.

Plus becomes more relevant when you want a multi-stage lead journey, service-specific reminders, branching rules or reactivation sequences. Do not upgrade because “more automation” sounds better; map the exact workflow first and count the required steps.

Is Pro worth it?

Pro is easier to justify when advanced segmentation, predictive/conditional content or attribution/conversion tracking will materially change how you operate. A small shop that does not review campaign data regularly may pay for capabilities it does not use.

Trial and buying checklist

ActiveCampaign currently advertises a 14-day trial with no credit card required. Use that period to connect one real lead source and test one complete automation.

  • Import a small clean contact sample.
  • Connect or simulate the booking-completed event that should stop sales follow-up.
  • Check unsubscribe and suppression behavior.
  • Test the automation on mobile and desktop email clients.
  • Confirm the live price for your actual contact count before the trial ends.
