Affiliate disclosure

How commercial relationships work on DetailerFit.

Current MVP status: At launch, product links are direct, untracked vendor links unless explicitly marked otherwise.

If affiliate links are added

DetailerFit may earn a commission when a reader signs up or purchases through a clearly marked affiliate link, at no extra cost to the reader.

What commissions do not buy

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee a #1 ranking, positive conclusion, or inclusion in a shortlist. Products without affiliate programs can still rank above products that pay commissions.

How we label links

Pages containing active affiliate links will include a disclosure near the recommendation or comparison—not only in the footer.

Corrections

If a vendor believes a published price, feature, or plan limit is outdated, the correction process is described on our Contact page.
