How affiliate links work

Some software vendors pay DetailerFit when a reader becomes a qualifying lead, trial user, subscriber or customer through a tracked link. Commission structures vary by partner and can change. A commercial relationship is disclosed near relevant calls to action and does not increase the price paid by the reader.

What commissions do not influence

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee a #1 ranking, a positive verdict, inclusion in a shortlist or a higher Match Score. Products without affiliate programs can rank above products that pay commissions.

Affiliate payout, commission rate and partner status are not inputs to DetailerFit's evidence-weighted Match Score. Match Score is intended to describe fit for the reader's stated requirements, while Evidence Confidence describes the strength of the supporting evidence.

How affiliate links are labeled

Pages containing active affiliate links include a disclosure near the relevant recommendation or comparison. Affiliate links are marked with appropriate sponsored-link attributes where applicable. Official-source links used as research citations remain separate from tracked commercial links.

Research standard

DetailerFit relies primarily on current vendor pricing, product documentation and help-center material unless a page explicitly states that hands-on testing was performed. Unknown or unverified capabilities are not treated as confirmed merely because a vendor is an affiliate partner.

See How We Review and the Match Score methodology for more detail.

Sponsored content

If DetailerFit publishes paid sponsored content, it will be clearly identified as sponsored. Sponsorship does not purchase a ranking position, Match Score advantage or guaranteed positive recommendation.

Corrections

If a vendor believes a published price, feature or plan limit is outdated, it can use the Contact page to provide a correction and supporting source. Corrections are evaluated on evidence, not affiliate status.
