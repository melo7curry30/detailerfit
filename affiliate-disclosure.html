How affiliate links work

DetailerFit may earn a commission when a reader signs up or purchases through a clearly marked affiliate link, at no extra cost to the reader.

What commissions do not buy

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee a #1 ranking, positive conclusion or inclusion in a shortlist. Products without affiliate programs can still rank above products that pay commissions.

How we label affiliate relationships

Pages containing active affiliate links include a disclosure near the relevant recommendation or comparison. Affiliate links are also marked for search engines using appropriate sponsored-link attributes where applicable.

Our research standard

Current research pages rely primarily on official vendor pricing, feature documentation and help-center material unless a page is explicitly labeled hands-on tested. An affiliate relationship does not change the evidence standard.

Sponsored content

If DetailerFit publishes paid sponsored content, it will be clearly identified as sponsored. Sponsorship does not purchase a ranking position or a guaranteed positive recommendation.

Corrections

If a vendor believes a published price, feature or plan limit is outdated, the correction process is described on our Contact page.
