What AI Receptionist does

The published feature set centers on 24/7 call answering, spam filtering, transfers, voicemail, call transcripts and a knowledge base. Higher plans add more capacity and business features.

This is a phone/front-desk product, not a complete detailing operating system. If you need estimates, invoices, vehicle history and job workflow in one database, compare a full CRM alongside it.

Appointment scheduling

Google Calendar integration is listed on Starter, Solopreneur and Business. For a mobile detailer, that makes Starter the first plan to inspect if the goal is for the receptionist to move beyond message-taking into appointment scheduling.

Call handling and follow-up

The current pricing page lists call transfers, voicemail, text and audio transcripts, real-time notifications and multilingual support across the product. It also documents post-call actions on eligible plans, which can send summaries or alerts to registered contacts after a call.

Best fit for auto detailers

  • You miss calls while working on vehicles.
  • You want a dedicated phone layer without replacing your current CRM.
  • You want calls transferred or summarized when human attention is needed.
  • You want Google Calendar scheduling on an eligible plan.

Potential limitations

  • It is not a detailing-specific CRM or shop-management system.
  • Appointment scheduling is not listed on the Essential plan.
  • Included call minutes vary by plan, so real usage matters when comparing total cost.
  • Complex ceramic, correction, tint or PPF inquiries still need safe pricing and escalation rules.

DetailerFit assessment: AI Receptionist is worth testing as a low-friction phone layer when missed calls are the problem and your existing CRM already handles operations. We would not replace a strong detailing CRM solely to gain phone coverage.

Final check before subscribing

Forward a real business line or test number during the trial and run the calls that matter: a basic detail, a high-ticket coating inquiry, a reschedule, a service-area question and a caller who needs a human. Judge the product on how safely it handles those scenarios, not just on whether it answers the phone.
