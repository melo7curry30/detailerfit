What the headline price hides

A software product can look cheap until the workflow you actually need sits on a higher tier. The biggest recurring pricing traps for detailers are customer self-booking, additional users, job-volume limits, texting, route optimization and automotive-specific vehicle records.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest auto detailing software in this index?

Several products publish $0 entry plans, including DetailPilot, ServiceM8, Reservio and Sparkyl. The practical cheapest option depends on booking volume, users, device workflow and whether you need detailing-specific vehicle records.

Which tools include online booking without a premium upgrade?

At this check, AutoHustl, DetailPilot, Jobber, JobField, Housecall Pro, ServiceM8, Reservio and OrbisX publish customer booking at or from their entry plan or single-plan offering. QuoteIQ and Urable require higher plans for customer-facing online booking.

Why are some cells marked “verify”?

Because a missing public feature gate is not evidence that a feature exists or does not exist. When current official documentation does not establish an equivalent workflow clearly enough, DetailerFit marks it for verification instead of filling the gap with an assumption.
