Automotive-specific vs general field-service software

Automotive-specialist products can be more natural when vehicle records, VIN data, inspections, coatings, tint, PPF or vehicle condition documentation are central to the business. General field-service platforms can be stronger when the operation looks more like a mobile service fleet: customer booking, dispatch, routes, employee scheduling and broader service-business administration.

That trade-off is why this page does not force one universal winner. A one-person detailing startup and a multi-van team can enter the same market with completely different requirements.
