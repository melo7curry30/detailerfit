What Should a Mobile Detailing CRM Actually Do?

Traditional CRM software focuses on contacts and sales pipelines. A mobile detailer needs more. Your CRM is usually part customer database, part scheduler, part quoting tool, part payment system, and part retention engine.

1. Keep the full customer history

You should be able to see contact information, previous jobs, estimates, invoices, payment history, notes and communications without searching through text threads or spreadsheets.

2. Capture new leads

Website forms, booking links and request forms should move leads into the system before they disappear. This matters especially when you are detailing a vehicle and cannot answer every call immediately.

3. Store useful vehicle information

Automotive-specific CRMs can add VIN data, vehicle profiles, service history, damage intake, inspections and other vehicle-oriented context that generic CRMs do not prioritize.

4. Turn leads into approved jobs

Professional estimates, optional upgrades, e-signatures, deposits and customer portals can reduce friction between “How much?” and a confirmed appointment.

5. Bring customers back

Review requests, maintenance reminders, rebooking campaigns and long-term follow-up matter because repeat customers can be much cheaper to acquire than entirely new leads.

1. Jobber — Best Overall CRM for Many Solo Mobile Detailers

Best for: a solo mobile detailer who wants a mature customer-management system, online booking, professional quotes, invoices, payments and a customer portal without immediately paying for an automotive-specialist platform.

Jobber is a general field-service platform rather than a detailing-specific CRM, but its client management is one of the strongest fits for this particular keyword. It connects client details, job history and communication history, and it can separately tag and manage leads before they become active customers.

Its Client Hub gives customers a self-service place to request work, approve quotes, check appointments and pay invoices. Jobber's online-booking system can also let customers select services and available times based on rules you configure.

Jobber pricing

For one user, Jobber Core is $49/month with no commitment or $29/month effective with annual billing. Connect is $139/month with no commitment or $99/month effective annually; Grow is $199/month or $149/month effective annually. Higher plans add deeper automation, time/expense tracking, richer quoting and job costing.

Why Jobber ranks #1 here

  • Strong lead and client management
  • Client history and communication history in one profile
  • Customer-facing Client Hub
  • Online requests and booking
  • Professional quotes, invoices and payments
  • Low annual-billing entry price for a solo user
  • Easy path into more advanced automation as the business grows

Where Jobber falls short

  • Not built around vehicles, VINs or detailing-specific inspections
  • Advanced automation and profitability tools require higher plans
  • Team pricing increases as you scale

DetailerFit verdict: If your biggest problem is managing leads and customers—not vehicle-specific workflow—Jobber is a very rational CRM starting point.

2. QuoteIQ — Best Affordable AI-Focused CRM for Solo Operators

Best for: a solo operator who wants estimating, invoicing, payments, internal scheduling and a rapidly expanding set of AI tools at a low monthly entry price.

QuoteIQ Essentials costs $29.99/month for one user and includes estimates, invoices, recurring service plans, payments, e-signatures, an internal job calendar, customer contact management, lead-source tracking, analytics and its AI feature set through monthly IQ Credits.

That is a lot of CRM functionality for the price. QuoteIQ also publishes AI tools for natural-language CRM control, estimating assistance, reporting and virtual call workflows.

The important catch: QuoteIQ's internal scheduling should not be confused with customer self-booking. Current 2026 documentation says InstaSchedule customer self-booking begins on Elite at $299/month. If a public booking link is essential, compare the price of Elite—not Essentials—with competing products.

Why consider QuoteIQ

  • $29.99/month entry price
  • Estimates, invoices, payments and e-signatures
  • Internal scheduling and Google Calendar sync
  • Customer contact management and lead-source tracking
  • AI tools included across all plans through IQ Credits
  • Web, iOS and Android access
  • No long-term contract

Potential drawbacks

  • Customer self-booking currently requires the much more expensive Elite tier
  • Entry plan is limited to one user
  • Not fundamentally an automotive-specific vehicle CRM

DetailerFit verdict: QuoteIQ is one of the strongest values if you are solo, handle scheduling yourself and want a lot of CRM and AI functionality without a large software bill.

3. Urable — Best Automotive CRM for Detailers Planning to Grow

Best for: a detailing business that wants customers, vehicles, scheduling, inspections, routing, payments and business analytics in an automotive-specific system.

Urable is much more vehicle-aware than a generic CRM. Its current plan matrix includes customer interaction tracking, vehicle-oriented scheduling, employee assignment, VIN functionality, inspection tools, invoicing, payments, inventory and profitability features.

Express currently costs $70/month, Pro $110/month and Enterprise $183/month. The big pricing advantage for a growing shop is that all three plans include unlimited users.

Pro is the important tier for many growth-focused detailers because it adds online booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up messaging, QuickBooks integration and profit analytics.

Why consider Urable

  • Designed specifically for automotive care
  • Unlimited users on every current plan
  • Customer and vehicle records
  • VIN and inspection workflow
  • Online booking on Pro+
  • Route optimization for mobile work
  • Long-term follow-up messaging on Pro+
  • Profit analytics and inventory tools

Potential drawbacks

  • Higher entry price than Jobber, QuoteIQ or ServiceM8
  • Online booking requires Pro at $110/month
  • Some advanced workflows require Enterprise

DetailerFit verdict: A solo beginner may not need Urable immediately, but it becomes one of the best CRM choices when the business starts adding technicians, vehicles, inspections and more complicated operations.

6. Mobile Tech RX — Best for Estimating and Vehicle Documentation

Best for: detailers and auto-reconditioning businesses where vehicle check-in, estimating, VIN data, condition documentation and quality control are more important than a traditional sales CRM.

Mobile Tech RX is different from the other products here. It is built around automotive reconditioning workflows and supports dedicated modules for Detail, PDR, Paint & Body, Interior, PPF/Vinyl, Wheels, Glass and Tint.

The current Getting Started plan is $39/month per admin plus $15 per additional user. It includes one service module, scheduling, customer text alerts, estimating, credit-card processing, and before-and-after photo workflow. Higher plans add more sophisticated operational capabilities.

Automotive CRM vs General Field-Service CRM

This is the core choice.

Automotive-specific CRMs such as Urable and OrbisX understand that the object being serviced is a vehicle. That can mean VIN data, vehicle history, inspections, damage intake, automotive service packages and workflow around the vehicle itself.

General field-service CRMs such as Jobber and ServiceM8 are designed around customers, jobs, schedules and technicians. They may actually be better if your biggest pain point is lead management, booking and communication rather than vehicle data.

QuoteIQ sits closer to the service-business side but differentiates itself heavily through AI, estimating and automation. Mobile Tech RX sits on the other end: less like a classic sales CRM and more like an automotive estimating and operating workflow.

How to Choose the Right CRM

If you mostly lose leads because you cannot answer the phone

Prioritize lead capture, booking, automated communication and customer portals. Jobber, OrbisX and higher-tier QuoteIQ workflows deserve closer attention.

If you forget to follow up with old customers

Prioritize automated follow-up, reminders, review requests and rebooking. Urable Pro, OrbisX and higher QuoteIQ tiers become more relevant.

If quoting takes too long

Mobile Tech RX and QuoteIQ deserve a serious look. The first is deeply automotive-estimating focused; the second is aggressively building AI-assisted estimating and CRM tools.

If your team is growing

Compare user pricing carefully. Urable and OrbisX currently advertise unlimited users, and ServiceM8 includes unlimited users on paid plans. A cheap one-user plan can become expensive once several technicians need access.

If customers must book themselves online

Compare the exact plan that unlocks self-booking. This is one of the biggest pricing traps in CRM comparisons: a cheap starting tier may include a calendar without including a public booking link.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best CRM for a solo mobile detailer?

Jobber is our best overall CRM pick for many solo mobile detailers because its entry plan can combine client management, online booking, quotes, invoices, payments and a customer portal at a relatively low annual-billing price. QuoteIQ is a strong alternative for AI-focused operators who do not need customer self-booking, while Urable is more automotive-specific.

What is the cheapest CRM for a mobile detailing business?

ServiceM8 currently has a $0 Free plan for one user and up to 30 new jobs per month. Jobber Core starts at $29/month with annual billing for one user, and QuoteIQ Essentials starts at $29.99/month.

Which detailing CRM is most automotive-specific?

Urable and OrbisX are the strongest classic CRM choices in this comparison for vehicle-care businesses. Mobile Tech RX is also highly automotive-specific, but its product emphasis is more heavily centered on estimating, vehicle intake and reconditioning workflow.

Does QuoteIQ Essentials include online booking?

No. QuoteIQ Essentials includes an internal calendar and scheduling. Current 2026 documentation says customer self-booking through InstaSchedule starts on Elite at $299/month.

Which CRM is best for a detailing team with several employees?

Urable and OrbisX deserve serious consideration because both currently advertise unlimited users. ServiceM8 also includes unlimited users on its paid plans, although plan pricing is tied to monthly new-job allowances.

Do I need an automotive-specific CRM?

No. If your biggest challenges are leads, booking, quotes and communication, a general field-service CRM such as Jobber can be excellent. Automotive-specific software becomes more valuable when VINs, vehicle records, inspections, damage documentation and vehicle-specific workflow matter every day.
