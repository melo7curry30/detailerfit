What a solo mobile detailing CRM actually needs

  • Customer and vehicle history: enough context to stop searching old text threads.
  • Fast estimates and invoices: usable from a phone between jobs.
  • Scheduling: a clean internal calendar—and, only if you need it, customer self-booking.
  • Payments: a short path from completed job to paid invoice.
  • Follow-up: reminders and repeat-work communication without another disconnected tool.
  • A sane upgrade path: the first employee should not force an emergency migration.

ServiceM8 — strongest $0 and iPhone-first starting point

ServiceM8 publishes a Free plan at $0/month for one user and up to 30 new jobs per month. Starter is $29/month with unlimited users and a 50-job monthly allowance. Scheduling and Online Bookings are published across all plans.

The trade-off is device strategy. ServiceM8 is explicitly Apple-first for the full field experience. That can be a strength if the business already runs from an iPhone or iPad, and a reason to compare alternatives if Android is central to the crew.

QuoteIQ — low-cost internal quote-to-payment workflow

QuoteIQ Essentials costs $29.99/month for one user and publishes estimates, invoicing, internal job scheduling, online payments, contact management and web/iOS/Android access. That makes it a credible low-cost option when the main problem is scattered quoting, scheduling and billing.

The important limitation is customer self-booking. InstaSchedule does not begin until Elite at $299/month. If customers choosing their own appointment time is a must-have, compare the $299 workflow—not the $29.99 headline.

Jobber — strongest fit when self-booking matters immediately

For one user, Jobber Core is published at $49/month with no commitment and $29/month effective when billed annually. Core includes online booking alongside scheduling, quotes, invoices, online payments, reporting and a website.

That means Jobber can be cheaper than a lower-headline-price competitor once customer self-booking becomes a hard requirement. The trade-off is that Jobber is a broad field-service platform rather than an automotive-specific CRM.

Urable — strongest automotive growth path

Urable Express starts at $70/month and publishes unlimited users. Pro is $110/month and adds online booking. The product is explicitly positioned around vehicle-service workflows, which can matter more than winning the lowest solo subscription price.

For a truly solo operator with basic needs, $70 may be more than necessary. For someone expecting to add technicians, the combination of automotive specialization and unlimited users can change the long-term economics.

Housecall Pro — broad booking and dispatch stack

Housecall Pro Basic is published at $79/month on monthly billing or $59/month billed annually for one user. It includes online booking plus scheduling and dispatch, estimates, invoicing and payments.

For a pure solo detailer, that broader stack may be more software than necessary. It becomes more interesting when detailing is part of a wider service business or when general field-service operations matter more than automotive specialization.

A simple buying rule for a one-person business

Buy the cheapest plan that removes your current bottleneck. Do not pay for team GPS, complex pipelines or advanced dispatch because you might need them someday. Upgrade when a real workflow requires the feature.

Final recommendation by scenario

  • iPhone-first + minimum spend: start by examining ServiceM8.
  • Low-cost internal scheduling, quoting and invoicing: examine QuoteIQ Essentials.
  • Customer self-booking immediately: put Jobber Core high on the shortlist.
  • Automotive specialization + planned hiring: examine Urable.
  • Broader service-business operations: examine Housecall Pro.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best CRM for a solo mobile detailer?

There is no universal winner. ServiceM8 is particularly strong for a $0 or iPhone-first start, QuoteIQ for a low-cost internal workflow, Jobber when online booking matters immediately, Urable for automotive-focused growth and Housecall Pro for broader field-service operations.

What is the cheapest option?

ServiceM8 publishes a free plan for one user and up to 30 new jobs per month. Starter is $29/month. QuoteIQ Essentials starts at $29.99/month.

Which option is best for online booking?

Jobber Core and ServiceM8 publish customer online-booking capability at their entry levels. Urable adds online booking on Pro at $110/month. QuoteIQ customer self-booking through InstaSchedule begins on Elite at $299/month.

Do I need CRM software if I only do a few jobs each month?

Not necessarily. A small operator can start with basic tools. CRM software becomes more valuable when quotes, scheduling, follow-up, customer history or invoices repeatedly fall through separate apps and message threads.
