BUYING GUIDE · UPDATED SEP 6, 2026

Best mobile detailing software: start with the operator you are today.

Five tools compared using current public vendor evidence. No vendor paid for placement.

Research label: This guide is based on current official vendor documentation. It is not presented as a hands-on product test.

Quick answer

For a cost-conscious solo detailer, QuoteIQ and ServiceM8 deserve the first comparison. For a more structured field-service operation, Jobber becomes more interesting. Urable deserves a look if vehicle-service specialization matters enough to justify the higher starting price. Housecall Pro is broader and starts higher, but brings a substantial general field-service toolkit.

Our shortlist

1. QuoteIQ — best research fit for a low-cost contractor-focused start

Starts at $29.99/month and includes core estimates, invoices, scheduling and online payments. Higher tiers unlock more communication, job-costing and operations features.

2. ServiceM8 — best research fit for solo / iPhone-first economics

The free plan gives one user up to 30 jobs/month. The $29 Starter plan moves to unlimited users and 50 jobs/month.

3. Jobber — best research fit for a more structured service operation

Core starts at $49/month on flexible monthly billing for one user, with deeper automation and operational features on higher plans.

4. Urable — strongest vehicle-service specialization in this shortlist

Express starts at $70/month and publishes unlimited users, reminders, invoicing, inventory tracking and payment integrations.

5. Housecall Pro — broad field-service alternative

Basic is published at $79/month on monthly billing or $59/month billed annually, with booking, scheduling/dispatch, estimates/invoicing, payments, job costing and review management.

What we would choose by scenario

  • Just starting, under 30 jobs/mo: compare ServiceM8 Free and QuoteIQ Essentials first.
  • Solo but detailing-specific workflow matters: compare QuoteIQ and Urable.
  • 2–5 people and automation matters: compare the actual QuoteIQ / Jobber / ServiceM8 tiers that contain your required automation.
  • General field-service processes matter more than detailing specialization: compare Jobber and Housecall Pro.
