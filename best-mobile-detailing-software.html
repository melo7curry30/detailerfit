Best mobile detailing software: start with the operator you are today.

A research-based shortlist for solo operators and small crews, using current vendor pricing and plan documentation.

Research label: This guide uses official vendor documentation and is not presented as a hands-on product test.

Quick answer

There is no universal winner. For a solo iPhone-first operator, ServiceM8 has unusually strong entry economics. QuoteIQ is attractive when you want a low-cost contractor-oriented quote-to-payment workflow and do not need customer self-booking yet. Urable becomes more compelling when automotive specialization and unlimited users matter. Jobber and Housecall Pro become stronger as you value broader field-service booking, team and dispatch workflows.

Scheduling is the biggest trap: “has a calendar” is not the same as “customers can book themselves.” See our mobile detailing scheduling software comparison before choosing a plan.

What to compare before you buy

  • First plan with your must-have feature: starting price can be misleading when booking, automation or routing is gated higher.
  • Team economics: compare user limits and job-volume limits, not only monthly price.
  • Field device fit: the software has to work while you are at the vehicle, not just behind a laptop.
  • Customer loop: lead → quote → booking → job → payment → review/repeat work should require as little duplicate entry as possible.
  • Migration risk: do not choose a platform you will outgrow after the first employee if moving later would be painful.
