Best CRM & Business Management Software for PPF Shops (2026)

Paint protection film shops need more than a generic calendar and invoice app. High-ticket installs create unique problems around film inventory, vehicle condition documentation, multi-day scheduling, package quoting, deposits, warranties and job profitability. We compared four platforms with published PPF-specific workflows to find the best fit.

Quick answer

OrbisX is our best overall PPF shop software for 2026 because its current PPF feature set combines roll-level film inventory, estimate options, custom warranties, job-level profit/loss and automotive customer records in a single $100/month promotional plan. Urable is a very close second and is the better choice for shops that prioritize structured workflows, multi-day projects, line-item scheduling and video inspections.

Important search-intent note: this guide covers CRM, quoting and business-management software for PPF installers. It does not compare vehicle pattern libraries or plotter/cutting software used to physically cut film.

Research disclosure: DetailerFit has not hands-on tested every platform in this guide. Rankings are based on current vendor documentation, published pricing, plan-level feature availability and fit for a PPF installation workflow, verified September 7, 2026.

1. OrbisX — Best Overall PPF Shop Software

Our pick: OrbisX has the strongest combination of PPF-specific inventory, warranty, estimating and profitability features at its current published price.

OrbisX publishes a dedicated PPF installer workflow rather than simply listing PPF as one of many supported service categories. Its current PPF page specifically documents the ability to create estimate options and templates, collect vehicle color during booking, track film rolls by batch number and quantity, generate job-level profit/loss reports, receive low-inventory warnings and create custom PPF warranties.

Why OrbisX ranks #1

  • Film inventory is genuinely PPF-aware: OrbisX says shops can track film by roll, batch number, yard and other units.
  • Custom warranty records: warranty details and claims can stay associated with the customer rather than living in a spreadsheet or PDF folder.
  • Estimate options and templates: useful for full-front vs track-pack vs full-body packages or multiple film brands/grades.
  • Job-level profitability: published PPF documentation describes automatic profit/loss reporting for each job.
  • All features in one published tier: the current pricing page says there are no traditional feature tiers.

OrbisX pricing for PPF shops

$100/month — currently promotional

OrbisX currently advertises $100/month as a limited-time price, a 30-day free trial, no contracts or commitments, iOS and Android apps, and all features without tier restrictions. The vendor also notes that some pay-per-use credits and third-party services such as Dialpad or Twilio can add cost.

Best fit: dedicated PPF shops that want film inventory, warranties, quoting, profitability and broader CRM/automation in one system.

Main caution: $100/month is explicitly promotional. We would not build a long-term software budget on the assumption that new customers will always get this price.

2. Urable — Best for Structured PPF Operations

Urable is the strongest choice when a PPF shop cares less about having every film-specific function in one base plan and more about building a disciplined process from quote to inspection to installation to delivery.

Its dedicated PPF page emphasizes multi-day jobs, drag-and-drop scheduling, online booking, photo/video inspections, customized workflows, employee compensation and line-item scheduling. Urable also says its inventory tools can track PPF materials, low-stock levels, usage and job-costing/invoicing data.

Why Urable is a very close #2

  • Excellent project control: complex PPF installs can be broken into line items and assigned to employees.
  • Multi-day scheduling: useful for full-body jobs that may include wash, correction, disassembly, installation and cure time.
  • Strong liability documentation: video/photo inspections are built around recording pre-existing vehicle condition.
  • Automated workflows: follow-ups, scheduling, quotes and deposit chasing can be standardized.
  • Unlimited users on every plan: attractive as a shop adds installers or front-desk staff.

3. Tint Wiz — Best for Shops Combining Window Tint + PPF

Tint Wiz is built first around film businesses, which gives it a different feel from a generic field-service CRM. Its strongest PPF advantages are professional proposals, film-roll inventory, project records, deposits and customer communication.

The current product documentation says Tint Wiz can manage film rolls, labels, check-ins/check-outs and remaining material by weight or length. Its proposal workflow supports multiple options, photos, upsells, SMS/email delivery, customer approval and tracking when the proposal is opened or viewed.

Why Tint Wiz is especially strong for tint + PPF

  • Window tint, PPF, ceramic coating and wrap services can live in the same project system.
  • Film inventory is more relevant to a film shop than generic SKU inventory.
  • Good / Better / Best proposals fit premium PPF package selling.
  • Deposits can be collected after proposal approval.
  • Unlimited users and projects remove per-seat pressure as a team grows.
  • Automotive and flat-glass work can coexist for businesses that do both.

Tint Wiz pricing

The current Tint Wiz homepage advertises $150/month flat, unlimited users, unlimited projects, a 30-day free trial and no contract.

Pricing note

Some older Tint Wiz pages still show a $100/month price. We use $150/month in this guide because that is the price on the current main product page and FAQ. Confirm the amount displayed during signup.

Best fit: shops where window tint and PPF are both major revenue categories and film inventory plus proposal presentation matter more than advanced vehicle-reconditioning estimating.

4. Mobile Tech RX — Best for Estimating & Vehicle Documentation

Mobile Tech RX takes a different route. It comes from automotive reconditioning, so its PPF/Vinyl module is particularly strong around estimating the vehicle, documenting damage and building repeatable pricing.

The current PPF/Vinyl page highlights pre-set estimate packages, pricing by panel and material type, interactive vehicle diagrams, photos and notes, scheduling, payments, customer reminders and QuickBooks integration.

Why Mobile Tech RX stands out

  • Panel-level estimating: pricing can be customized by vehicle panel and material type.
  • Interactive vehicle diagrams: useful when the estimate needs to communicate exactly what areas are being covered.
  • Damage documentation: photos and notes can stay attached to the estimate/job record.
  • Low-cost entry: the Getting Started plan can use the PPF/Vinyl service module.
  • Advanced shop workflow exists: Pro adds workflow management, time tracking, checklists, COGS and net-revenue tracking.

Which PPF Software Should You Choose?

Choose OrbisX if your shop is PPF-first

For a dedicated PPF operation, the combination of roll/batch inventory, custom warranties, estimate options and job profitability is difficult to beat at the current published price.

Choose Urable if operations discipline matters most

If the biggest problem is coordinating technicians, documenting vehicles, managing multi-day installations and creating a repeatable process, Urable's Enterprise-level workflow is exceptionally well matched to the job.

Choose Tint Wiz if you are a tint shop expanding into PPF

Tint Wiz is the most natural bridge when tint is already core to the business. Film inventory, proposal presentation and project records translate well to PPF without forcing the shop into a generic CRM model.

Choose Mobile Tech RX if estimating is the bottleneck

For installers who care about panel/material pricing, interactive vehicle diagrams and photo-based documentation, Mobile Tech RX deserves a demo even if another platform ultimately wins on inventory or CRM depth.

Do PPF Shops Need Pattern-Cutting Software Too?

Possibly — but that is a separate software category.

A CRM/business-management platform helps you manage the customer and job: lead capture, quoting, deposits, scheduling, vehicle records, film inventory, workflow, payments, warranties and follow-up.

Pattern-cutting software helps you manage the physical film pattern: vehicle templates, nesting, plotter output and cut files.

Some businesses need both. Do not assume a strong PPF CRM automatically replaces the software connected to your plotter.

New browser-based option: OmniPlot

OmniPlot is a public-beta PPF and tint cutting platform built around a browser-first workflow. It supports pattern selection, auto-nesting, HPGL output and multiple plotter connection methods, with individual plans starting at $0 and paid tiers from $29/month. Its low price and tablet-friendly direction are interesting, but its current pattern-library documentation is still inconsistent enough that we recommend testing it alongside an established cutting system rather than migrating blindly.

Final Verdict

Best overall: OrbisX

OrbisX wins this comparison by a narrow margin. Its published PPF workflow directly addresses film rolls, batch tracking, estimates, warranties, payments and profitability — the problems that make a PPF shop different from a generic service business.

Urable is the strongest alternative for larger or process-heavy shops, particularly when video inspections, multi-day scheduling and structured technician workflows matter more than paying the lowest possible monthly price.

For most buyers, our shortlist would be simple: demo OrbisX and Urable first. Add Tint Wiz if tint is a major part of your revenue, and add Mobile Tech RX if estimating and vehicle documentation are the center of your workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best CRM for a PPF shop in 2026?

Our best overall pick is OrbisX because its published PPF feature set includes film roll/batch tracking, estimate options, custom warranties and job-level profit/loss reporting. Urable is our top alternative for structured workflows, inspections and multi-day team scheduling.

What is the cheapest PPF business software in this comparison?

Mobile Tech RX has the lowest entry price at $39/month for an admin plus $15 per additional user, and its Getting Started plan can use the PPF/Vinyl module. Urable starts at $70/month with unlimited users.

Which PPF CRM is best for film inventory?

OrbisX and Tint Wiz stand out. OrbisX publishes roll, batch-number and yard-level inventory plus low-stock alerts and job profit/loss. Tint Wiz tracks film rolls by weight or length and supports roll check-in/check-out.

Which software is best for PPF vehicle inspections?

Urable is particularly strong when its Enterprise plan is used because it includes video inspections and line-item scheduling. Mobile Tech RX is also strong for damage documentation through photos, notes and vehicle-oriented estimating.

Is Tint Wiz only for window tint?

No. Tint Wiz is window-tint-first, but it explicitly supports PPF, ceramic coatings, wraps, detailing and other automotive appearance services.

Does PPF business software replace cutting software?

No. The products in this guide are CRM and shop-management platforms. Pattern libraries and plotter/cutting software are a separate category and may still be required.
