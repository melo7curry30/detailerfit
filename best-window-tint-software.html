Best Window Tint Software for Tint Shops (2026): 8 Tools Compared

Window tint software can mean two completely different things: software that runs the shop, or software that cuts film. We separated those jobs instead of pretending a CRM and a plotter-pattern library belong in the same ranking. Below are our 2026 picks for tint-shop business software first, followed by the best cutting and pattern tools.

Quick answer

Tint Wiz is our best overall business-management software for a dedicated tint shop in 2026. It is built around film businesses and combines proposals, deposits, appointments, inventory, project records and customer communication for $150/month flat. Service VIN is the strongest alternative if live-margin quoting, film-cost control and AI-assisted lead follow-up matter more. For pattern cutting, DigiCut is the safer established choice, while OmniPlot is the most interesting browser-first budget option—but it is still in public beta.

Search-intent note: this guide covers both window tint shop software and window tint cutting software, but ranks them in separate sections because they solve different problems.

1. Tint Wiz — Best Overall Window Tint Shop Software

Our pick: Tint Wiz is the most straightforward choice for a shop that wants software designed around film work rather than a generic field-service CRM adapted to tinting.

Tint Wiz's current product site positions the platform around automotive and flat-glass tinting, PPF, ceramic coatings and wraps. Its core workflow connects proposals, deposits, appointments, inventory, notes and before-and-after photos. That matters because a tint shop can keep the customer conversation, vehicle/project record and film workflow together instead of stitching together a booking app, spreadsheet and messaging tool.

Why Tint Wiz ranks #1

  • Purpose-built for film businesses: the product is centered on tint, PPF and related installation workflows.
  • Proposal-to-job flow: proposals, deposits and appointments stay connected rather than becoming separate manual steps.
  • Film inventory: inventory is part of the shop workflow rather than a generic add-on.
  • Automotive and flat glass: useful for shops that mix vehicle tint with residential or commercial film work.
  • Simple pricing: Tint Wiz currently publishes $150/month flat with no per-user fee and advertises a 30-day free trial.

Tint Wiz pricing

$150/month flat. The vendor says the price does not change with team size and currently advertises a 30-day free trial. Always verify current terms at signup.

Best fit: dedicated tint shops, especially businesses that also sell PPF, wraps, ceramic coating or flat-glass film.

Main caution: Tint Wiz is shop-management software, not a replacement for a vehicle-pattern cutting library if you pre-cut film on a plotter.

2. Service VIN — Best for Live Margin + AI Lead Follow-Up

Service VIN is the most differentiated option in this list if you care about seeing profit before a quote goes out. Its dedicated window-tint page says shops can quote by glass set and film tier with live margin, track film to the foot, offer 24/7 online booking and use AI tools to answer or follow up with leads.

That combination targets a common tint-shop problem: a busy shop can look booked while still leaking margin through material cost, missed calls, slow follow-up or underpriced packages.

Why Service VIN ranks #2

  • Film-aware inventory: the tint product page specifically describes film tracked to the foot.
  • 24/7 booking: customers can move from interest to a scheduled job without waiting for shop hours.
  • AI layer: higher tiers extend into missed-call recovery, follow-up and AI front-desk workflows.
  • Flat per-shop pricing: current plans start at $79/month rather than charging per installer seat.

3. OrbisX — Best for Multi-Service Film Shops

Best Window Tint Cutting Software

If you landed here searching for window tint cutting software, window tint plotter software or window tint patterns, this is the section you want. These tools are not CRMs. Their job is to supply editable vehicle patterns and send cut files to a plotter.

1. DigiCut — Best Established Tint + PPF Pattern Software

DigiCut is focused specifically on automotive tint and PPF pattern cutting. Its current site describes editable patterns and a professional installer workflow rather than shop CRM functions.

  • Monthly: $199/month.
  • Annual: $1,788/year, equivalent to $149/month.
  • Best for: installers who want a mature, dedicated tint/PPF pattern platform and are comfortable with a higher software budget.

Main caution: this is a cutting tool. You will still need a separate system if you want CRM, deposits, automated follow-up and broader shop management.

2. Film Designs 4 — Best for a Large Offline-Capable Pattern Library

Film Designs 4 is professional window tint and PPF cutting software from Film & Vinyl Designs. The vendor currently advertises 130,000+ window tint and PPF patterns and says the software can operate offline.

  • Tint-only monthly: $125.
  • PPF-only monthly: $125.
  • Tint + PPF monthly: $175.
  • Best for: shops that value a large established pattern library and offline operation.

Film & Vinyl Designs says these prices became effective July 1, 2026.

3. OmniPlot — Best Browser-Based Budget Option

OmniPlot takes a very different approach: the cutting software lives in the browser and works with HPGL-compatible plotters. The current product is in public beta and can be tried without a credit card.

  • Lite: $29/month.
  • Pro: $79/month.
  • Strength: browser-first workflow, low entry cost, PPF + tint support and broad plotter compatibility through HPGL.
  • Best for: installers who want to test a modern, device-flexible cutting workflow without committing to a $100–$200 monthly pattern platform.

Main caution: beta status. We would test known vehicles and familiar panels in parallel before replacing a production-proven cutting system.

Which Window Tint Software Should You Choose?

Choose Tint Wiz if window tint is the center of your business and you want the most direct all-in-one shop workflow.

Choose Service VIN if you care most about margin visibility, film cost, online booking and AI-assisted lead handling.

Choose OrbisX if your shop mixes tint with PPF, wraps, coatings or detailing and you want a broader auto-appearance operating system.

Choose Urable if you prioritize structured CRM, scheduling and repeatable team operations across multiple service lines.

Test FilmIQ if AI lead response is strategically important and you are comfortable evaluating an emerging platform.

Add a cutting tool—DigiCut, Film Designs 4, OmniPlot or another proven pattern system—if you use a plotter. A CRM cannot replace the pattern library, and the pattern library cannot run your front desk.

The practical stack for many tint shops

One shop-management platform + one cutting platform. For example: Tint Wiz for customers, quotes and jobs, plus DigiCut for plotter patterns. Or Service VIN for margin/booking/CRM plus OmniPlot for browser-based cutting. The right pair depends on how much you value pattern maturity, automation and material-cost visibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best software for a window tint shop?

For a dedicated tint shop, our 2026 research pick is Tint Wiz. Its workflow is purpose-built around film businesses and the current $150/month flat price is easy to model. Service VIN is our preferred alternative for shops that prioritize live-margin quoting and AI-assisted lead follow-up.

Is window tint CRM software the same as tint cutting software?

No. Shop software manages leads, quotes, scheduling, payments, customer history and operations. Cutting software supplies vehicle patterns and communicates with a plotter. The categories are complementary, not interchangeable.

What is the cheapest window tint cutting software in this guide?

Among the products in this guide with public pricing, OmniPlot has the lowest paid entry at $29/month. It is also in public beta, so price should not be the only deciding factor for a production shop.

What is the best window tint software for a solo shop?

If you want tint-specific business software, Tint Wiz is simple but costs $150/month. Urable starts lower at $70/month, while Service VIN starts at $79/month. A solo operator should compare the exact features needed at those entry tiers rather than choosing solely by base price.

Can one program handle both CRM and film cutting?

Most leading products specialize in one side. Tint Wiz, Service VIN, OrbisX, Urable and FilmIQ are business-management platforms. DigiCut, Film Designs 4 and OmniPlot are cutting/pattern tools. Using one from each category is normal.

Which software supports flat-glass tint work?

Tint Wiz explicitly positions its system for both automotive and flat-glass window tinting. If flat glass is a major share of your revenue, verify the exact estimating and project workflow during your trial before committing.
