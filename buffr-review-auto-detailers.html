What is Buffr?

Buffr is a business platform built specifically for auto detailers. Its current product positioning combines online booking, customer and vehicle records, payments, route planning, automated communication, review requests, referrals and business reporting in one system.

That detailing-native positioning is the main reason Buffr is interesting. A generic field-service platform can manage appointments and invoices, but Buffr is trying to organize the workflow around detail packages, vehicles, repeat maintenance work and mobile routes from the start.

DetailerFit lens: Buffr's closest strategic comparison is not only another detailing CRM. At $29/month, it also competes with detailers who are currently stitching together Square, Google Calendar, forms and spreadsheets because dedicated software feels too expensive.

The problem is that Buffr's separate pricing page does not tell exactly the same story. It lists Starter with up to 50 bookings per month and assigns route optimization and SMS to Pro, while the homepage describes Starter as including unlimited bookings, automated email/SMS and route planning/optimization.

We would not choose a plan from the comparison table alone. Use the 14-day trial to verify the exact feature set visible in your account, then confirm any must-have limits before moving customer data or embedding the booking widget on a production site.

Online booking and CRM

Buffr's core workflow is booking-first. The vendor advertises a customer-facing booking page/widget, customer and vehicle records, service history, job tracking, reminders and review/referral tools.

For a solo mobile detailer, that can replace several disconnected tools: a form for bookings, a spreadsheet for customers, a calendar for appointments and separate messages for reminders or review requests.

The strongest question to test is not whether those features exist, but how much configuration they require for real-world packages, travel areas, add-ons and repeat customers. DetailerFit has not hands-on tested that setup yet.

Payments and the real cost of the $29 plan

Buffr uses Stripe for card processing. Its current homepage says Stripe's standard card-processing fee is 2.9% + $0.30, then Buffr adds its own platform fee when the payment is processed through Buffr.

On the homepage, that Buffr fee is currently 2% on Starter, 1.5% on Pro and 1% on Business. Cash or payments collected outside Buffr are described as having no Buffr platform fee.

Why this matters more than the subscription price

A $29 monthly subscription looks inexpensive, but a detailer running a high volume of card payments through Buffr can pay substantially more in percentage-based platform fees than in the base subscription. Compare the payment economics using your expected monthly card volume, not only the software sticker price.

This does not automatically make Buffr expensive. It means the right comparison is subscription + payment cost + operational value, especially against tools that monetize payments differently.

Routes and mobile detailing

Buffr's homepage currently markets route planning and optimization as part of its mobile-detailing workflow. That is particularly relevant for operators who lose time driving across a wide service area or manually ordering jobs.

However, Buffr's pricing page assigns route optimization to Pro, while the homepage includes it in the currently available Starter description. This is one of the plan gates that should be checked directly in the trial.

Mobile app and device experience

Buffr says the platform works in a mobile browser and can be added to the iPhone or Android home screen. That makes it usable like a progressive web app without waiting for an app-store install.

Native iOS and Android apps are still listed as coming soon. For a mobile detailer who works almost entirely from a phone, we would test the browser experience carefully before replacing an established native-app workflow.

What is still coming soon?

The current homepage places several higher-end features in future Pro or Business tiers rather than the available Starter plan. These include:

  • AI receptionist / AI assistance on the Business roadmap.
  • Multi-user team access, fleet and multi-location tools on Business.
  • Live GPS tracking and expanded marketing tools on Pro.
  • Memberships and digital gift cards on Pro.
  • Native iOS and Android apps on the roadmap.

Buffr also references QuickBooks Sync with rollout language that appears stale relative to the current September 2026 date. Treat QuickBooks availability as something to verify in-product rather than assuming the roadmap date reflects current production status.

Who should test Buffr?

Buffr is most interesting for a solo or small mobile detailer that has outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected booking/payment tools but does not want to start with an expensive CRM.

A $29 detailing-native platform can be compelling if the current Starter feature set really includes the booking, reminders and route tools your business needs. The trial is especially important here because Buffr's public plan documentation is still catching up with the product.

Established multi-tech shops should be more cautious until Buffr's team, fleet, AI and native-app roadmap matures. They may be better served by comparing established options first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Buffr cost?

Buffr's homepage currently lists Starter at $29/month as available now, Pro at $49/month as coming soon and Business at $99/month as coming soon. Its separate pricing page describes feature gates differently, so confirm the live plan during the trial.

Does Buffr offer a free trial?

Yes. Buffr currently advertises a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Does Buffr charge a fee on payments?

Yes for payments processed through Buffr. Its homepage currently lists a 2% Buffr platform fee on Starter in addition to Stripe's card-processing fee. Buffr says cash or payments collected outside the platform do not receive the Buffr platform fee.

Can Buffr import existing customers?

Buffr says customer data can be imported from a CSV or spreadsheet. Public documentation does not yet clearly explain every type of historical CRM record that can be migrated.

Does Buffr have native mobile apps?

Not yet based on the current roadmap. Buffr works in a mobile browser and can be added to a phone's home screen; native apps are listed as coming soon.

Has DetailerFit hands-on tested Buffr?

No. This review is research-based and uses Buffr's current public product documentation. We do not claim hands-on findings about interface speed, route quality, support, reliability or payment reconciliation.
