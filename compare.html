DEVICE FIT

"Supports Android/iPhone" does not always mean "run everything from the app."

Use the device column as a compatibility screen, then open the review before buying. ServiceM8 is Apple-first and uses ServiceM8 Lite on Android. DetailPilot documents a mobile-web workflow on any phone and a native iOS app. Do not infer full mobile-admin parity from an OS badge alone.
