What the 14-day trial includes

New accounts receive a 14-day Pro trial without a credit card. Growth is the paid entry plan at $29/month; the current pricing page no longer lists Starter as a continuing free plan.

Test a complete booking, deposit and customer reminder during the trial, then choose the plan needed to keep the booking page online.

When Growth makes sense

Growth includes customer booking, deposits, invoices, reminders and Google review requests. The current pricing summary does not state a numeric booking cap; confirm any usage limits with the vendor.

Why Pro is the important website tier

Pro adds website and custom-domain tools, a booking widget, memberships, multi-service and multi-vehicle booking, and customer journey tracking. Verify these workflows during the trial.

Business: team operations

Business is positioned for teams. Public pricing lists team management, individual roles and permissions, advanced reporting, webhooks, automatic local-SEO features, removal of DetailPilot branding and chat support.

Payments, deposits and memberships

DetailPilot explicitly advertises deposits plus online and on-site payment collection. Membership plans are published on Pro and Business. If recurring maintenance details are central to your retention strategy, that is one of the more relevant reasons to compare the Pro tier alongside the entry Growth plan.

Device fit

DetailPilot is web-based and explicitly advertises an iOS app. During this source check, we did not find equally clear public documentation for a dedicated Android app. Android-heavy teams should confirm the current field experience directly with DetailPilot before buying.

Trial and cancellation

The 14-day trial is temporary. Growth, Pro or Business is required afterward. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled; confirm current checkout terms and processor fees.

DetailerFit verdict

DetailPilot is a relevant paid option for new and growing detailers who want booking and payment workflows designed around their services. Growth starts at $29/month, while Pro adds website and membership tools. Budget for the continuing subscription after the trial.

It is not automatically the best fit for every established shop. Larger teams should compare Business against deeper automotive CRMs, and Android-dependent operators should verify device support before migrating.
