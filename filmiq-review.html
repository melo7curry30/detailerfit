What is FilmIQ?

FilmIQ is a shop-management and sales platform operated by SeaWraps LLC and positioned specifically for window tint, paint protection film, vinyl wrap, chrome delete and ceramic businesses. The product is currently in early access and says it is running in the founder's own film shop.

The core flow is unusually specific to automotive appearance work: capture a lead, text the customer, quote from the shop's configured pricing, send a proposal, collect a deposit, create a Square invoice and put the accepted job on the calendar.

DetailerFit lens: The biggest strategic difference is not “AI” by itself. It is that the AI, pricing model and proposal workflow are being built around film-shop services and panel-level pricing instead of a generic field-service job.

AI lead response and quoting

FilmIQ's central sales feature is an AI representative that can respond to incoming leads, ask qualifying questions, quote from the shop's configured price tables and continue following up until the customer books, opts out or a human takes over.

That is more ambitious than a standard missed-call text-back or scripted receptionist. The value proposition is that the AI can move beyond “How can I help?” into a service-specific price conversation using the shop's own tiers.

Important limitation: DetailerFit has not hands-on tested FilmIQ's quote accuracy, vehicle recognition, edge cases, human handoff or hallucination controls. Treat those as trial-validation items, not proven performance claims.

Per-panel pricing and proposals

FilmIQ publishes a pricing workspace built around panels, coverage packages, film choices, difficulty and markups. Its public PPF example prices a full-front package from individual hood, bumper, fender, mirror and headlight components, then applies coverage-level adjustments and fees.

For PPF and wrap shops, that is a meaningful design choice. Generic CRMs usually think in products, labor items or service packages; a film shop often needs to think in panels, material, coverage and vehicle-specific complexity.

Customer proposals can show package options and add-ons, display the deposit due and be approved from the customer's phone. The vendor also says proposal status can track sent, viewed and accepted states.

Square, deposits and payment workflow

FilmIQ is designed to keep Square rather than replace it. When a proposal is accepted, the vendor says FilmIQ can create an itemized Square invoice, collect the deposit first and mirror payment status back into the FilmIQ workflow.

This could reduce double entry for shops already committed to Square, but it also means Square is an important dependency. Shops using another payment stack should verify whether the workflow still fits before migrating.

Calendar and booking workflow

Accepted work becomes a job on FilmIQ's calendar, with Google Calendar sync and reminder texts. The public product flow shows the vehicle, service and price attached to the scheduled job.

FilmIQ is not positioning itself as a generic “pick any open slot” booking widget first. Its pitch is closer to lead → conversation → quote → proposal approval → scheduled install, which can make more sense for high-ticket PPF, wrap and premium tint jobs that need qualification before a time is committed.

FilmIQ Growth and Lead Radar

FilmIQ also advertises an early-access Growth add-on. The published concept includes ad creation for Google, Meta and TikTok, re-engagement of quiet quotes and attribution from ad spend to booked installs.

The more unusual feature is Lead Radar: FilmIQ says it monitors public car-owner forums for local buyers asking about PPF, tint or wrap, then sends the shop an alert with a drafted reply. The vendor says territories are limited to one shop per market.

Why this matters: If FilmIQ can turn local automotive-community intent into measurable booked revenue, Lead Radar would be genuinely differentiated from standard CRM marketing automation. It is still an early-access add-on, so DetailerFit would want real customer evidence before treating it as a proven acquisition channel.

What FilmIQ does not have yet

One useful sign of transparency is that FilmIQ publicly lists several missing capabilities. As of the September 25 source check, the vendor says the following are not yet built:

  • Instagram and Facebook DM capture. Leads in those inboxes still remain in their native apps.
  • QuickBooks integration.
  • A native mobile app.

Those gaps matter. A tint or wrap shop that lives in Instagram DMs may not get the unified inbox it expects yet, and an accounting-heavy operation may prefer a more mature integration stack.

Who should test FilmIQ?

FilmIQ is most compelling for a film-focused shop where missed leads and slow quoting are expensive. A PPF, tint or wrap business selling high-ticket work can justify a higher software bill more easily if the platform actually converts even a small number of leads that would otherwise go cold.

It is less obvious for a new solo operator with low lead volume, a shop that depends heavily on Instagram DMs, or a business that needs mature accounting, mobile-app and migration tooling immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How much does FilmIQ cost?

FilmIQ currently publishes one Shop plan at $299/month or $3,050/year. Every seat is included. The vendor says there is no setup fee or contract, while Twilio carrier fees pass through separately.

Does FilmIQ have a free trial?

Yes. FilmIQ publishes a 14-day trial that starts at onboarding. Its pricing page says a card is required upfront and the first charge occurs on day 15 unless the shop cancels before then.

Is FilmIQ only for PPF?

No. FilmIQ targets tint, PPF, vinyl wrap, chrome delete and ceramic services. Its pricing model and sales workflow are built around automotive film and appearance work.

Does FilmIQ replace Square?

No. FilmIQ is designed to create and synchronize invoices with Square. That is an advantage for existing Square shops, but a payment-stack dependency to evaluate if you use another processor.

Does FilmIQ have Instagram DM integration?

Not yet according to FilmIQ's current FAQ. Instagram and Facebook DM capture are listed among features still to be built.

Has DetailerFit hands-on tested FilmIQ?

No. This is a research-based review using FilmIQ's current official product pages. We do not claim real-world AI accuracy, support quality, uptime, mobile usability or migration performance.
