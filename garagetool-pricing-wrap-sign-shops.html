Annual Billing, Refund Policy & Cancellation

GarageTool's pricing page advertises 20% savings when annual billing is selected. Because checkout can change and employee charges may be handled separately, we would not treat a hand-calculated annual total as a guaranteed invoice. Confirm the exact annual amount on the checkout screen.

The page currently says there is no free trial. Instead, GarageTool advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee and says subscriptions can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled without a cancellation fee or long-term commitment.

Practical implication: shops evaluating GarageTool should schedule the estimator, proofing, payment and workflow tests inside the first 30 days rather than treating the refund window like an indefinite trial.

Solo Shop vs Team Shop

For a one-person wrap or sign business, Solo Shop is the natural first tier because it already contains the core operational features GarageTool is known for: visual estimating, vehicle templates, quotes, scheduling, proofing, payments, automation, reminders and QuickBooks integration.

Team Shop becomes relevant when multiple employees need access and the owner needs permissions, employee management and time tracking. The upgrade decision should be based on actual collaboration requirements, not just future growth plans.

If your second person only needs occasional information rather than an active seat, ask GarageTool how employee billing and permissions work before assuming the Team formula is necessary.

When Does the $200 CRM Tier Make Sense?

The plan boundary that deserves the most attention is Multiple Shop + CRM. GarageTool already stores customer and job information across the platform, but its pricing page puts the more advanced sales-pipeline features on this tier.

  • Customer / lead management
  • Unlimited emailing and SMS
  • Email and SMS recording
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Custom lead fields
  • Quote / lead plugin
  • Email lead-capture extension
  • Facebook lead-form integration

A shop that mainly needs estimating, proofing, scheduling and job execution may not need this tier. A business losing leads before they become quotes may have a stronger case for the CRM upgrade. Test the exact lead workflow rather than equating “CRM” with basic customer records.

GarageTool Costs to Verify Before Buying

The subscription is only one part of total software cost. Before migrating, get written answers for the pieces that could change the real monthly or one-time cost:

  • Employee billing: how GarageTool counts active/billable employees.
  • Payment processing: fees for the processor your shop actually uses.
  • QuickBooks: whether your current QuickBooks plan is sufficient and what sync behavior is included.
  • SMS/email usage: especially if you are comparing lower plans with the CRM tier's published unlimited communication features.
  • Data migration: whether customer, estimate, invoice and artwork history can be imported for you.
  • Annual billing: the exact discounted checkout total and refund/cancellation handling for annual accounts.

GarageTool Pricing FAQ

How much does GarageTool cost per month?

The current official pricing page lists Solo Shop at $100/month; Team Shop at $100 plus $10 per employee/month; Multiple Shop at $125 plus $10 per employee/month; and Multiple Shop + CRM at $200 plus $10 per employee/month.

Does GarageTool have a free trial?

No. The current pricing page says there is no free trial. It advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee instead.

Does GarageTool offer annual pricing?

Yes. The current pricing page advertises 20% savings when annual billing is selected. Confirm the exact annual checkout total before paying.

Can I cancel GarageTool anytime?

GarageTool's current pricing FAQ says there is no long-term commitment and that users can upgrade, downgrade or cancel without a cancellation fee.

Which GarageTool plan includes CRM lead management?
