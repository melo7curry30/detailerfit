Wrap Estimating & Vehicle Templates

GarageTool's most differentiated feature is its wrap-specific estimating workflow. The current product and pricing pages advertise a visual wrap estimator and more than 15,000 vehicle templates. The shop selects the vehicle, coverage and material inputs, then turns that information into a professional estimate rather than building every quote from scratch.

Why that matters

  • Faster quote creation: repeatable inputs reduce the amount of manual calculation per vehicle.
  • More consistent estimating: a team can use the same workflow instead of relying on each employee's memory.
  • Better project handoff: the estimate can stay attached to the job as it moves into scheduling, proofing and payment.

GarageTool also markets the platform beyond vehicle wraps, including fleet graphics, PPF, storefront signs, window graphics, interior branding and custom signage. That makes it more relevant to mixed wrap/sign businesses than software built only around detailing appointments.

Design Proofing, Scheduling & Job Workflow

GarageTool's workflow is designed to keep the customer-facing and production sides of a project connected. Current product pages list design proofing, job-status tracking, shared scheduling, vehicle/job photos, automated appointment reminders, payment requests and customer progress notifications.

For a wrap or signage project, proof approval is not a minor feature. A quote can be accepted while the artwork is still changing. Keeping proof feedback and approval tied to the same job reduces the risk that production works from an outdated email attachment or that a revision disappears inside a long thread.

Operational features worth testing in a demo

  • Visual job status / Trello-style workflow
  • Design proofing and revision history
  • Appointment and production scheduling
  • Automated email and SMS reminders
  • Vehicle photos, inspections and disclaimer checklists
  • Automatic invoice/job creation rules

CRM & Team Management

GarageTool stores customer, quote, invoice and project history so the shop can search previous work instead of rebuilding context each time. Team plans add permissions, employee management and time tracking, while multi-shop plans add location/division support and comparative reporting.

The highest listed tier expands that into a more conventional sales CRM with lead management, communication history, follow-up reminders, custom lead fields and lead-capture tools. That tier structure is important for shops that expect a full pre-sale pipeline rather than only customer/project records.

Payments, Accounting & Integrations

GarageTool's current official pages say shops can collect deposits and invoice payments through the workflow and list QuickBooks integration. The platform is cloud-based, so the team can access projects, schedules, customer records and estimates away from a single office computer.

Before migrating, verify the exact payment processor setup, accounting sync behavior and how historical invoices/customers are imported. Integration depth matters more than a logo on a feature list when the shop already has years of books and customer records.

  • 15,000+ vehicle templates tied to visual estimating.
  • Quote-to-job workflow instead of a contact-only CRM.

TRADE-OFFS

  • If plotter cutting/pattern production is the main bottleneck, compare a dedicated cutting tool separately; the official pages we reviewed do not position GarageTool as a plotter engine.

What to Verify Before Switching

  1. Import depth: customer records, old quotes, invoices, artwork and job history.
  2. Estimator setup: how your materials, waste, labor and custom pricing rules are configured.
  3. Proofing workflow: revision limits, approval records and customer notifications.
  4. Payments: processor fees, deposit workflow and refund handling.
  5. QuickBooks sync: what data syncs, in which direction and how duplicates are prevented.
  6. Mobile workflow: test the actual devices your installers and sales staff use.
  7. CRM plan gate: confirm whether your lead pipeline requires the $200 Multiple Shop + CRM tier.
  8. Exit plan: how customer/job data can be exported if you cancel.
