HighLevel lists unlimited contacts and unlimited users on all three core plans. The biggest structural difference between Starter and Unlimited is the sub-account limit: Starter includes three, while Unlimited and Agency Pro include unlimited sub-accounts.

Starter — $97/Month: The Plan Most Detailers Should Test First

For an owner-operator or a single detailing company, Starter is the most relevant pricing benchmark. It includes the core platform: CRM and pipelines, workflow automation, booking calendars, website/funnel tools, social media management, payments/invoicing and more.

That does not mean HighLevel is automatically the right software for a detailer. It remains a broad CRM and marketing platform rather than a vehicle-first shop operating system. But if your bottleneck is lead response, appointment follow-up, nurture and marketing automation, you do not need to jump to $297 just because it is labeled “Unlimited.”

Practical rule: if you operate one detailing brand and are not selling HighLevel to other businesses, prove the workflow on Starter before paying for agency-scale features.

Unlimited — $297/Month: Mainly a Sub-Account Upgrade

Unlimited includes everything in Starter, removes the three-sub-account cap, adds basic API access and allows phone/email usage to be rebilled without markup. HighLevel positions the plan for growing agencies.

A multi-location detailing company could still have a reason to use multiple sub-accounts, but do not assume “multiple technicians” requires Unlimited. HighLevel publishes unlimited users on Starter. The important distinction is separate sub-accounts/workspaces, not simply team member count.

Agency Pro — $497/Month: Usually Not a Detailing-Shop Upgrade

Agency Pro is designed around SaaS Mode, automated sub-account creation, advanced API access and the ability to rebill supported usage with markup. Those are agency/software-resale features rather than ordinary detailing operations.

For a detailing business using HighLevel internally, the $497 tier is usually difficult to justify unless the company is also operating a marketing agency, selling a software layer to franchisees/clients, or needs the Pro-specific API and SaaS tooling.

The $97 Subscription Is Not the Entire Cost

HighLevel explicitly states that usage-based charges apply to parts of the platform. Its pricing page specifically calls out telecommunications and AI-related usage, and notes extra costs for phone numbers, SMS, emails and premium AI usage.

  • SMS and calling: usage charges can apply.
  • Email: sending costs can apply depending on usage.
  • Premium AI: some AI functionality has usage or add-on pricing.
  • Optional products: several add-ons have their own monthly fees.
Do not budget only $97. A detailer planning automated SMS follow-up, calling and AI should estimate those usage costs before comparing HighLevel with an automotive CRM that bundles more communication into its subscription.

HighLevel Pricing FAQ

How much is HighLevel per month?

HighLevel currently lists Starter at $97/month, Unlimited at $297/month and Agency Pro at $497/month. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Does HighLevel offer annual billing?

Yes. Current annual prices are $970 for Starter, $2,970 for Unlimited and $4,970 for Agency Pro.

Does HighLevel have a free trial?

HighLevel’s standard pricing page currently advertises a 14-day free trial and says billing begins automatically after the trial unless canceled. Some promotional landing pages can advertise different trial lengths, so confirm the exact offer attached to your signup link.

Can I cancel HighLevel?

HighLevel's current pricing FAQ says the service is month-to-month with no long-term contract and that customers can upgrade, downgrade or cancel through the dashboard.

Are SMS, email and AI included in the $97 plan?

The platform features are included according to the plan, but HighLevel says usage-based charges apply to telecommunications, email and premium AI services. Estimate those costs separately.

Is Starter enough for an auto detailer?

It can be enough when the goal is CRM, booking and automation for one business. A detailer that needs automotive-specific vehicle intake, inspections, VIN tools or estimating should compare HighLevel with specialist software before subscribing.
