Housecall Pro Baseline: What Are You Replacing?

Housecall Pro currently publishes Basic at $79/month or $59/month on annual billing for one user, Essentials at $189 monthly / $149 annual for five users, and MAX at $329 monthly / $299 annual for eight users. A 14-day free trial is available.

That baseline matters because an alternative is only cheaper if the plan you actually need contains the workflow you are leaving Housecall Pro to get. A lower starting price can be misleading when online booking, extra users, vehicle records, routing, estimates or automation sit on a higher tier.

QuoteIQ — Lower Entry Cost, but Watch the Self-Booking Tier

QuoteIQ starts at $29.99/month for Essentials with one user. Its 2026 plan ladder then moves through Beginner $74.99, Pro $149.99, Elite $299 and Max $699. Every plan includes scheduling and AI tools through IQ Credits, while customer-facing InstaSchedule self-booking begins on Elite.

That makes QuoteIQ interesting for a solo detailer who wants CRM, estimates, invoices, payments and AI at a much lower entry price than Housecall Pro. But a detailer specifically leaving Housecall Pro for customer self-booking should compare the $299 Elite tier — not the $29.99 headline price.

Urable — Automotive-Specific CRM with Unlimited Users

Urable publishes Express at $70/month, Pro at $110 and Enterprise at $183 in the United States. All three plans list unlimited users. Express covers organization, reminders, invoicing, staff assignment and analytics; Pro is the first tier with online booking, lead forms, long-term follow-up and QuickBooks integration.

Urable is worth comparing when the reason you are leaving Housecall Pro is not simply price, but the desire for a vehicle-care workflow and team economics that do not scale by paid seat. The key price comparison for a booking-heavy shop is usually Pro at $110, not Express at $70.

Strata CRM — Vehicle CRM + Booking Without Feature-Gating by Plan

Strata currently advertises pricing from $39/month, a 30-day free trial with no card, and says the full product is available on every plan. Its published workflow includes client and vehicle CRM, online booking, deposits through Stripe, two-way texting, quotes, invoices, reminders, reviews and business intelligence.

That is a different pricing philosophy from Housecall Pro and from platforms that reserve major workflow features for a higher tier. The comparison point is therefore less about whether booking exists and more about plan limits, team size, support, integrations and whether Strata's younger product is mature enough for your operation.

OrbisX — Broad Automotive CRM in One Published Tier

OrbisX currently publishes a $100/month limited-time offer with a 30-day free trial, no contracts and one all-features tier. Its platform is built around automotive businesses and lists scheduling, quotes, invoices, payments, expenses, deposits, reports, automation, AI appointment booking, inventory, time tracking and mobile apps.

For a detailing shop, the appeal is consolidation: one automotive-specific platform rather than a generic field-service system plus several add-ons. The trade-off is that the published $100 rate is promotional, so confirm the lock-in terms and any usage-based third-party costs before treating it as a permanent comparison number.

Mobile Tech RX — When Estimating and Vehicle Documentation Lead the Decision

Mobile Tech RX publishes a Getting Started plan at $39/month per admin plus $15 per additional user. That plan includes one service module — Detail is one of the available choices — along with estimates, invoices, credit-card processing, scheduling, before/after photos and basic reporting.

This is a different reason to leave Housecall Pro. If your operational pain is defensible vehicle estimates, photos, documentation and recon workflow rather than front-office marketing or generic dispatch, Mobile Tech RX deserves a direct comparison. Customer-facing self-booking should be verified separately if it is a hard requirement.

Jobber — The General Field-Service Alternative

Jobber's Core plan is currently $49/month with no commitment or $29/month billed annually for one user. Core includes online booking, quotes, invoices, payments, a customer CRM and a client hub. Higher tiers add reminders, automations, QuickBooks, job costing, richer quoting and larger included teams.

Jobber makes the most sense when you like the general field-service model of Housecall Pro but want a different price ladder or workflow. It is less compelling if your reason for switching is specifically vehicle-first CRM, inspection evidence or detailing-specific operating logic.

Before You Switch: Five Checks That Prevent an Expensive Migration

  • Data: confirm how customer contacts, vehicles, notes, photos and service history export — and what the replacement can import.
  • Future jobs: recreate upcoming appointments, recurring work and deposits before canceling the old system.
  • Booking: test the replacement booking page on a phone, including service duration, buffer time, service area, deposits and fully booked days.
  • Team access: model the real cost at your current team size and the next hire, not only the solo starting price.
  • Exit timing: check billing date, cancellation terms, payment records and any integrations that stop working when Housecall Pro is disconnected.
Practical test: Run the replacement with a small set of real jobs before moving every workflow. The cheapest monthly plan is not a saving if it creates more manual scheduling, duplicate entry or missed bookings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a lower-cost alternative to Housecall Pro for a solo detailer?

QuoteIQ starts at $29.99/month and Strata starts at $39/month. Compare the exact booking, user and workflow tier you need rather than choosing from the entry price alone.

Which alternatives are most automotive-specific?

Urable, OrbisX, Mobile Tech RX and Strata publish automotive-focused workflows. QuoteIQ is broader home-service CRM software with a strong detailing use case; Jobber is general field-service software.

Does the cheapest alternative include online self-booking?

Not necessarily. QuoteIQ's customer-facing InstaSchedule begins on Elite, while Urable's online booking begins on Pro. Strata says online booking is available across its plans, and Jobber includes online booking in Core.

Should I leave Housecall Pro if it already works?

Not automatically. If Housecall Pro's dispatch, booking, team workflow and integrations already fit your business, migration has a real cost. Switching becomes more compelling when a replacement solves a specific pricing or workflow problem you can identify in advance.

What is the first thing to check before migrating?

Confirm data portability and future appointments first. Customer history, vehicle records, scheduled jobs, deposits and payment records are the hardest items to reconstruct after canceling the old platform.
