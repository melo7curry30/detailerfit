Why it makes the shortlist

Basic publishes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and invoicing, payments, price book, job costing and review management. That is a substantial entry-level field-service toolkit.

Where higher tiers matter

Essentials adds routes, photo reports and annotations, employee GPS, commissions and QuickBooks Online. Max adds route optimization, recurring service plans, API access and dedicated onboarding.

Which plan should you inspect first?

Solo operators should start with Basic. A 2–5 person team that needs routes and GPS should inspect Essentials. Larger or more operationally complex teams should compare Max against Jobber and specialist platforms before committing.
