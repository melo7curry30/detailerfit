Just need customers to book online?

Compare two booking-first options before choosing a full CRM. These are additional options, not a replacement for the independent shortlist above.

ServiceLink: a simple detailing booking link

Free includes 5 appointments to try it. Pro is $20/month for unlimited bookings and payment tools. Deposits are on Pro, not Free.

Consider it for a solo booking workflow; verify vehicle records and team requirements before switching.

Zenbooker: booking with service-area controls

Standard is $29/month with 2 territories. A 14-day trial needs no credit card. Drive-time-aware availability requires Enterprise.

Consider it for location-based booking; test the mobile web app and your vehicle-intake workflow.
