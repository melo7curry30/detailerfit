Current published entry pricing

For one user, Jobber currently publishes Core at $49/month with no commitment, $39/month with a one-year commitment paid monthly, or $29/month effective when billed annually.

For one to five users, these figures use Jobber's current published U.S. standard pricing. Jobber's pricing page also displays larger team configurations, but current Help Center documentation describes Connect as supporting up to five users.

Official-source conflict: DetailerFit does not treat 10- or 15-user Connect pricing as settled while Jobber's pricing page and Help Center are not perfectly aligned. If more than five people need access, confirm the eligible plan, included seats and current price directly with Jobber before subscribing.

Why it makes the shortlist

Core supports one user and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs and invoices. The current Core documentation includes Requests & Online Booking, scheduling, quotes, invoices and online payments.

Where higher tiers matter

Connect adds stronger follow-up, reminders, automatic payments, checklists, routing and QuickBooks Online. Grow adds job costing, richer quoting, two-way SMS and a custom automation builder. Plus is the top tier for larger and more complex operations.

Scheduling and booking

Jobber is attractive when customer online booking matters from the beginning because Core already includes that workflow. This is a major contrast with products that reserve customer self-booking for a much higher tier.

DECISION SUPPORT · VERIFIED SEP 17, 2026

Switching, automation, team permissions and mobile reality

Switching from another CRM

Jobber documents CSV import workflows for client data and also provides export tools for client information. Current Jobber import tooling also supports structured migration of operational records such as quotes, jobs and invoices where the required CSV format is available. Treat migration as a planned setup task rather than assuming every historical field transfers automatically.

Official import guidance · Official export guidance

What actually gets automated?

Jobber documents automated customer communications including quote follow-ups and invoice reminders. Availability depends on the selected plan, so compare the exact automation you need against the current pricing/help documentation before upgrading.

Official automation guide

Three-person example and permissions

For a three-person team, DetailerFit should compare the current multi-user configuration instead of the one-user Core headline. Jobber also supports role/permission controls so field staff, senior crew, crew leads and managers can be given different access to schedules, customer information and office workflows.

Official pricing · Official user-permission guide

Mobile reality

The Jobber mobile app covers core field work on iOS and Android, but not every setup/admin feature is app-only. If your goal is to run the business entirely from a phone, verify the specific setup, permission and customization tasks you expect to manage without the web dashboard.
