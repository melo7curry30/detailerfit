Current published entry pricing

For one user, Jobber currently publishes Core at $49/month with no commitment, $39/month with a one-year commitment paid monthly, or $29/month effective when billed annually.

For one to five users, these figures use Jobber's current published U.S. standard pricing. Jobber's pricing page also displays larger team configurations, but current Help Center documentation describes Connect as supporting up to five users.

Official-source conflict: DetailerFit does not treat 10- or 15-user Connect pricing as settled while Jobber's pricing page and Help Center are not perfectly aligned. If more than five people need access, confirm the eligible plan, included seats and current price directly with Jobber before subscribing.

Why it makes the shortlist

Core supports one user and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs and invoices. The current Core documentation includes Requests & Online Booking, scheduling, quotes, invoices and online payments.

Where higher tiers matter

Connect adds stronger follow-up, reminders, automatic payments, checklists, routing and QuickBooks Online. Grow adds job costing, richer quoting, two-way SMS and a custom automation builder. Plus is the top tier for larger and more complex operations.

Scheduling and booking

Jobber is attractive when customer online booking matters from the beginning because Core already includes that workflow. This is a major contrast with products that reserve customer self-booking for a much higher tier.
