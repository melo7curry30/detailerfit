When Does a Higher Marlie Plan Become Cheaper?

On pure minute economics, the plan breakpoints arrive sooner than the included-minute labels might suggest:

  • Basic → Standard: at roughly 393 minutes/month, Basic plus overage reaches about $99. Above that point, Standard is already cheaper before considering its booking features.
  • Standard → Pro: at roughly 786 minutes/month, Standard plus overage reaches about $199. Above that point, Pro becomes cheaper on subscription + minutes alone.
  • Pro → Premium: at roughly 1,667 minutes/month, Pro plus overage reaches about $399. Above that point, Premium becomes cheaper on subscription + minutes alone.
Do not optimize only for minutes. A plan can be the cheapest mathematically and still be the wrong plan operationally. Booking, transfer rules, knowledge-base access, custom fields and integrations can move you to a higher tier earlier.

Which Marlie Plan Fits an Auto Detailing Business?

Basic — call coverage, not automated booking

Basic fits a shop that mainly needs every call answered, questions collected and summaries sent to the owner. It can make sense when the owner still wants to personally approve and schedule every job.

Standard — the practical first look for many detailers

Standard is the first plan with automatic appointment booking, live availability checks, rescheduling and transfer to one number. For a solo mobile detailer or coating shop trying to stop losing calls while working, this is the most important plan boundary.

Pro — when callers need deeper answers and routing

Pro becomes relevant when the AI needs a knowledge base, custom post-call fields, more sophisticated transfers or secure checkout links. It also includes 1,000 minutes and a lower $0.30/minute overage rate.

Premium — high-volume or multi-location

Premium includes 2,000 minutes, drops overage to $0.25/minute and adds multi-location support, a dedicated account manager and custom CRM/line-of-business integrations. It is less about a solo shop and more about operational scale.

Marlie + Urable: Think in Total Workflow Cost

Marlie publishes a dedicated Urable integration for detailing and coating shops. That can let the receptionist qualify callers, check availability and place bookings into an Urable workflow.

But Marlie does not replace the CRM. A shop using both should budget for:

  • the Marlie plan,
  • any minute overage,
  • the separate Urable subscription, and
  • the operational cost of maintaining accurate services, durations, availability, service-area rules and escalation logic.

That combined cost can still make sense if phone leads are valuable, but the correct comparison is against recovered gross profit — not against the $49 headline price alone.

Trial & Billing Checks Before You Subscribe

Marlie's current site advertises a 14-day free trial. Its signup messaging says eligible new customers add a card with $0 due at checkout. Before activating, confirm the current trial eligibility, billing start, included usage and cancellation terms.

During the trial, test five real scenarios: a routine maintenance detail, a high-ticket ceramic/paint-correction lead, an out-of-area mobile request, a fully booked day and a caller asking for a human. Those tests reveal more than a generic demo call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Marlie AI cost?

Marlie currently publishes Basic at $49/month, Standard at $99, Pro at $199 and Premium at $399. Each tier includes a monthly minute allowance and charges overage after that allowance.

Which Marlie plan books appointments?

Standard is the first published plan with automatic booking, real-time availability checks and rescheduling. Basic answers calls and takes messages but does not include automatic appointment booking.

What happens if I exceed the included minutes?

Basic and Standard publish $0.35 per additional minute, Pro $0.30 and Premium $0.25.

Which plan is cheapest around 700 minutes?

Using current published subscription and overage rates only, Standard is about $169 at 700 minutes. Basic is about $206.50, Pro $199 and Premium $399.

Does Marlie replace Urable?

No. Marlie is a receptionist/front-office layer. Its Urable integration can connect calls and bookings into Urable, while Urable remains the CRM and operations platform.
