Pricing & Included Usage

Marlie currently publishes four main plans: Basic at $49/month with 250 minutes, Standard at $99 with 500 minutes, Pro at $199 with 1,000 minutes and Premium at $399 with 2,000 minutes. Overage rates fall from $0.35/minute on Basic and Standard to $0.30 on Pro and $0.25 on Premium.

My AI Front Desk publishes Business-in-a-Box at $99/month or $79/month billed annually. Business includes 200 voice minutes, 100 chatbot conversations, 400 SMS, CRM and automations. It also includes a monthly 1,000-credit overage pool; current published voice usage is 25 credits per minute, equivalent to $0.25/minute.

Do not compare only the $99 sticker price. Marlie allocates substantially more phone capacity at that tier. My AI Front Desk uses part of the same subscription to bundle non-phone channels and CRM/automation functionality.

Booking & Call Handling

Marlie Standard is the key plan boundary for a detailing business that wants the AI to do more than take messages. It publishes automatic appointment booking, real-time availability checks, rescheduling and transfer to one number. Pro adds a knowledge base, custom post-call fields, secure checkout links and more advanced transfer/routing.

My AI Front Desk publishes booking, rescheduling and follow-up workflows inside a broader front-office bundle. Its differentiator is not that it is the only product that can book; it is that booking sits beside voice, SMS, web chat, CRM and automation in the same subscription.

SMS, Chat, CRM & Automation

This is where My AI Front Desk changes the comparison. Business-in-a-Box includes 400 SMS messages, 100 chatbot conversations, a lightweight AI-native CRM, sequence automations, Zapier and outbound calling capacity in addition to the phone receptionist.

Marlie's value proposition is narrower and more phone-centric. That can be an advantage when a shop already runs Urable, QuoteIQ, OrbisX, Strata or another CRM and does not want to pay for a second customer-management layer.

Software-overlap test: if your existing CRM already handles texting, booking, lead follow-up and customer records, the broader My AI Front Desk bundle may duplicate tools. If your CRM is weak in those areas, the bundle may reduce the number of separate services you need.

How They Fit an Auto Detailing Workflow

Already happy with your detailing CRM

Marlie is the cleaner first inspection because it is easier to think of as a phone/front-desk layer around an existing operating system.

Front office is fragmented across several tools

My AI Front Desk can be more interesting because voice, SMS, web chat, CRM and automations are bundled together.

High call volume

Model Marlie's included-minute tiers carefully. At $99 it publishes 500 minutes; its higher tiers also reduce the overage rate.

High-ticket coating / correction / PPF leads

Whichever product you test, use owner-approved pricing rules and route uncertain condition-dependent work to a human before promising a final price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which includes more phone minutes at $99 per month?

Marlie Standard publishes 500 minutes at $99/month. My AI Front Desk Business-in-a-Box publishes 200 voice minutes at $99/month, plus a shared monthly credit pool.

Which includes more than phone answering?

My AI Front Desk bundles web chat, two-way SMS, a lightweight CRM and sequence automations. Marlie is more focused on phone answering, booking, availability and transfers.

Which is cheaper if I only need calls answered?

Marlie Basic starts at $49/month with 250 included minutes. It does not include automatic booking.

Which is better if appointment booking is required?

Both publish booking workflows. Marlie Standard is particularly easy to model because it includes booking and 500 phone minutes at $99/month. My AI Front Desk combines booking with a broader multi-channel bundle at the same monthly base price.

Are these full auto detailing CRMs?

Neither should automatically be treated as a vehicle-first detailing operating system. My AI Front Desk includes a lightweight CRM; Marlie is primarily a phone/front-desk layer. Test how either product fits your customer, vehicle and job records.
