MATCH SCORE MODEL V1.5.0 · VERIFIED SEP 27, 2026

A Match Score should be reproducible, not a gut feeling.

Pricing update · October 24, 2026: DetailPilot, QuoteIQ plan terms were rechecked. Other product research retains its original dates. Confirm live checkout terms. DetailPilot pricing · QuoteIQ pricing.

DetailerFit separates requirement fit from evidence strength. The score is personalized to the Finder inputs and is not a universal quality rating for a software vendor.

Core rule: unknown information is not scored as failure. Weak or missing evidence pulls a criterion toward neutral instead of toward zero. Affiliate relationships and commission amounts are not scoring inputs.

1. The formula

Each applicable criterion begins with a raw score from 0 to 100 and an evidence-confidence value from 0.00 to 1.00. The raw score is then shrunk toward neutral 50 according to the strength of the evidence:

Adjusted criterion score = 50 + Confidence × (Raw score − 50)

Examples: a verified 100 with 1.00 confidence stays 100. A raw 100 supported at 0.70 confidence becomes 85. A raw 0 at 0.70 confidence becomes 15. A completely unknown criterion has confidence 0 and therefore becomes neutral 50.

The final personalized Match Score is the weighted mean of all applicable adjusted criterion scores:

Match Score = Σ(Adjusted score × Criterion weight) ÷ Σ(Criterion weights)

Evidence Confidence is reported separately as the weighted mean of the confidence values. It is not hidden inside the headline score.

2. Raw criterion scores

DetailerFit avoids free-form 83/100-style editorial scoring where possible. Workflow requirements use a conservative three-state rubric:

100Requirement clearly confirmed for the selected plan or configuration.
50Partial, neutral or not sufficiently established for a positive or negative conclusion.
0Requirement clearly does not satisfy the stated need.

Industry fit uses a separate classification rubric because it describes product focus rather than a binary feature: 100 = purpose-built detailing/appearance workflow; 75 = automotive-specialist workflow; 50 = general field-service workflow; 25 = generic horizontal business software; 0 = unrelated workflow.

5. Hard constraints

Some inputs are requirements rather than preferences. The current Finder applies these gates before ranking:

  • Budget: if at least one eligible configuration has a verified monthly cost within the stated budget, over-budget and price-unverified configurations are removed before Match Score ranking.
  • Required customer self-booking: a product is not presented as a verified match when native customer-facing self-booking is either unverified or not available on the selected configuration.
  • Plan capacity: the Finder chooses the first plan configuration that can be supported by the current team-size/job-volume rules. Where the official documentation conflicts, the configuration is marked for verification instead of guessed.

6. Price fit

Known configurations at or below the stated monthly budget receive a raw Price Fit of 100. If no in-budget configuration exists and an over-budget option remains in the pool, Price Fit declines continuously using:

Raw Price Fit = 100 × (Budget ÷ Monthly cost)², capped between 0 and 100.

An exact price that is not verified receives neutral raw 50 with low confidence rather than a guessed dollar amount.

7. Setup readiness

Setup Readiness is only added when the user explicitly says a guided setup matters. It does not use subjective claims such as “easy to use” or a vendor's marketing estimate of how many minutes setup takes.

Instead, it is a composite of three observable support dimensions. Each subcriterion is evidence-weighted with the same shrink-to-neutral formula before the composite is calculated:

9. Unknowns, conflicts and missing evidence

“Not found” and “does not exist” are different claims. The model therefore does not assign zero merely because DetailerFit has not verified a feature. Unknown criteria use raw 50 and confidence 0 unless stronger evidence supports a different state. When official pages conflict, the model uses the conservative plan interpretation and lowers certainty or asks the reader to verify.

12. Current scope and limitations

Model v1.5.1 scores the requirements currently collected by the Software Finder across eight vendors in the normalized ranking set. Strata and DetailPilot joined the normalized set only after first-party plan, booking, automation, device and migration rules were documented well enough to reproduce their scores. The October 24, 2026 plan refresh uses DetailPilot Growth at $29/month after a 14-day trial. Processor fees are excluded; no continuing free plan or numeric booking cap is assumed. QuoteIQ prices and included seats were refreshed, and $25/month extra users are considered on each eligible plan. The lowest monthly configuration satisfying the selected features is used. Criteria weights and commercial neutrality are unchanged. Setup and migration are now included only when the user activates those needs.

The model intentionally does not score claimed setup time, UI aesthetics, generic review sentiment or undocumented migration capabilities. A documented import path can still have real-world edge cases, and a successful migration may depend on the quality and structure of the source data.

The Match Score should therefore be read as: “How well does this currently verified plan fit the requirements I entered?” It should not be read as “Which software company is objectively best?”

13. Commercial independence

The scoring implementation contains no commission amount, EPC, payout, affiliate-status bonus or revenue weight. Affiliate relationships can change the destination URL or disclosure text, but they do not change raw criterion scores, confidence multipliers, user weights, plan gates or the ranking formula.
