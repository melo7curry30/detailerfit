Why we separate starting price from usable-plan price

A software product can start cheaply while reserving customer booking, routing, automation or team tools for a much higher tier. DetailerFit compares the first plan that satisfies the stated use case whenever that difference matters.

How the Software Finder works

The Finder is a transparent rules-based shortlist, not an AI prediction and not a scientific product score. Team size, job volume, budget, customer booking, device fit and priorities add or subtract weight from each product. The result then shows the plan to inspect first and a trade-off to verify.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Current official pricing and feature documentation.
  2. Current official help-center documentation.
  3. Public product demos and release notes.
  4. User discussions as qualitative context—not as verified product facts.
  5. Hands-on testing, when explicitly labeled.

Affiliate relationships

Commercial relationships do not buy rankings, positive conclusions or inclusion. Products without affiliate programs may rank above products that pay commissions. Active relationships are disclosed near the relevant recommendation and on our Affiliate Disclosure page.
