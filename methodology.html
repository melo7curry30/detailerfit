EDITORIAL METHOD V1.0

We score the workflow, not the sales page.

Every research guide starts with the same operating scenarios so a long feature list cannot automatically win.

Important: Current reviews are research-based using official vendor documentation. A product only receives a “hands-on tested” label after we actually use it through the published workflow.

Our two reference businesses

Scenario A — Solo mobile operator

  • 1 van, 1 operator
  • 20–40 jobs per month
  • Three service packages plus common add-ons
  • Needs deposits, reminders, estimates, invoices and repeat-booking follow-up
  • Runs most of the day from a phone

Scenario B — Small crew

  • 2–5 people
  • 60–120 jobs per month
  • Needs staff assignment, route awareness, consistent job notes and better automation

Scorecard

20%Lead intake & booking
20%Estimating & payments
25%Mobile job-day workflow
20%Follow-up & repeat work
15%Total cost & plan limits

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Current official pricing and feature documentation.
  2. Current official help-center documentation.
  3. Public product demos and release notes.
  4. User discussions as qualitative context—not as verified product facts.
  5. Hands-on testing, when explicitly labeled.

Affiliate relationships

Commercial relationships do not buy rankings. If an affiliate program becomes active, the affected links will be clearly marked and the disclosure page will be updated.
