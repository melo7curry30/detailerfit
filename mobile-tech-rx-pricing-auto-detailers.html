Getting Started: the value tier for many solo detailers

Getting Started costs $39 per admin/month, plus $15 for each additional user. The plan lets a business choose one service module, including Detail.

Based on Mobile Tech RX's published plan information, Getting Started includes unlimited estimates and invoices, scheduling, payment processing, before/after photos, basic reporting and 50 customer text alerts per month.

That matters because these are not fringe features. They sit directly inside the work a solo detailer repeatedly performs: quote the vehicle, schedule the job, document the work, invoice the customer and collect payment.

Getting Started is compelling if this sounds like your day

You are still doing a lot of the business yourself, you want a vehicle-first system rather than a generic CRM, and you would actually use estimates, VIN/vehicle information, photos, scheduling and invoicing in the same workflow. In that situation, $39 is not buying a feature list — it is buying a more structured way to run the job.

Do not upgrade just because Standard looks more complete. If the entry plan already covers the work that slows you down, the most profitable decision can be staying on the lower tier until your operation creates a reason to move.

Standard: when the $60 jump starts to make sense

Standard costs $99 per admin/month, which is $60 more than Getting Started before additional users.

The upgrade becomes easier to justify when your business is no longer just about getting the job done. Mobile Tech RX publishes Standard features including basic QuickBooks Online integration, a client portal, technician pay tracking, subcontracting, customer statements, deposits on estimates, discounts, CSV exports, email history and pre/post diagnostic scan storage.

The most useful way to think about Standard is this: it is the tier where the software starts doing more around the business, not just around the vehicle.

  • Choose Standard if customers need a cleaner approval/payment experience through a client portal.
  • Choose Standard if deposits on estimates matter to your sales process.
  • Choose Standard if technician pay or subcontracting is becoming part of the operation.
  • Choose Standard if QuickBooks integration and cleaner financial handoff are now meaningful.
  • Stay on Getting Started if those additions would mostly sit unused.
Purchase test: If you can name the Standard feature that fixes a recurring problem in your business, the upgrade has a reason. If you cannot, keep the $60/month difference in your pocket.

Pro: buy it for operational complexity, not status

Pro costs $199 per admin/month, plus $29 per additional user. That is a $100 monthly jump from Standard at the base-admin level.

Mobile Tech RX positions Pro around more sophisticated operations. Published capabilities include workflow management, time tracking, service and job-specific checklists, check-in and QA processes, COGS/net-revenue tracking, broader QuickBooks functionality and other advanced business controls.

This is where Mobile Tech RX becomes most interesting for a shop that has moved beyond “keep my appointments and invoices organized” into “make the operation repeatable across people.”

Pro is probably overkill for many solo detailers. Its value rises when multiple jobs, technicians, workflow stages and quality-control steps are creating management friction. If you do not have that complexity yet, paying for the tools before the problem exists is hard to justify.

For the right business, however, Pro is the tier where Mobile Tech RX's automotive workflow depth becomes a much bigger part of the buying decision than the headline subscription price.

Do not add features because they sound useful. Build your expected total around what your team will genuinely use. A focused $39 or $99 setup can be a better business decision than a more expensive stack full of dormant functionality.

Why the entry plan can be more valuable than it looks

Mobile Tech RX is not just competing with another CRM. For the right detailer, it is competing with the fragmented way work often gets handled across separate tools: customer messages in one place, vehicle details somewhere else, photos in the camera roll, estimates in another system and scheduling in a calendar.

Getting Started is interesting because the published feature set pulls several of those tasks into an automotive-specific workflow without forcing a solo operator directly into a $99 or $199 plan.

If vehicle intake, estimating and documentation are already central to how you sell and deliver work, the $39 plan deserves serious attention. That is the scenario where Mobile Tech RX can feel less like “another monthly subscription” and more like the system the job runs through.

When Mobile Tech RX is easier to justify

Mobile Tech RX becomes easier to justify when several of these are already true:

  • Your quoting changes meaningfully by vehicle or condition.
  • You want VIN-driven vehicle information in the workflow.
  • Before/after photos and pre-existing damage documentation matter to customer communication.
  • You run detailing alongside other auto-reconditioning services.
  • You want estimates, work orders, invoices and vehicle records connected.
  • Your team is reaching the point where workflow stages, checklists, technician tracking or QA matter.

If you read that list and recognize your operation, Mobile Tech RX is worth evaluating against the cost of continuing with a more fragmented process.

When you should compare QuoteIQ instead

Mobile Tech RX is not automatically the best buy for every detailer. If your operation is primarily a simple service business and your priorities are low-cost CRM basics, quotes, invoices, internal scheduling and AI-assisted tools, QuoteIQ starts lower at $29.99/month.

The distinction is useful:

  • Start with QuoteIQ when your biggest problem is managing customers, quotes, invoices and appointments as cheaply as possible.
  • Start with Mobile Tech RX when your biggest problem is managing the vehicle, estimate, documentation and automotive workflow around the job.

Neither product wins because of the logo or the number of features. The better purchase is the one that matches the work you perform most often.

The buying rule we would use

Buy the lowest Mobile Tech RX plan that removes a real bottleneck

Getting Started is enough when the value is in estimates, scheduling, payments, photos and the core detailing module. Standard earns its higher price when client, accounting or technician-management features solve a recurring problem. Pro earns its price when operational complexity makes workflow management, time tracking and QA useful every week.

That approach protects you from two bad purchases: paying too much for features your business is not ready for, or choosing software that is cheaper but leaves the most expensive workflow problem untouched.

For many solo detailers, Getting Started is the plan we would inspect first. It is the lowest-risk way to find out whether Mobile Tech RX's automotive-first approach fits the way you actually work.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mobile Tech RX cost in 2026?

Current published monthly base pricing is $39 per admin for Getting Started, $99 per admin for Standard and $199 per admin for Pro. Additional users are $15 on Getting Started and $29 on Standard and Pro. Taxes and optional add-ons can increase the total.

Which Mobile Tech RX plan is best for a solo auto detailer?

Getting Started is the logical first plan to evaluate for many solo detailers. It includes one service module, scheduling, unlimited estimates and invoices, payments, before/after photos and basic reporting without forcing the business into a higher tier.

Is Mobile Tech RX Standard worth $99 per month?

It can be if you will use the added customer, accounting and technician-management features. Standard is more compelling when a client portal, deposits on estimates, basic QuickBooks integration, technician pay tracking or related tools solve real recurring problems.

Who should pay for Mobile Tech RX Pro?

Pro is most relevant to more sophisticated operations that need workflow management, time tracking, service or job-specific checklists, QA processes and deeper management controls. Many solo detailers will not need that level of operational structure.

Does Mobile Tech RX charge for additional users?

Yes. The current published monthly additional-user price is $15 on Getting Started and $29 on Standard and Pro.

Is Mobile Tech RX cheaper than QuoteIQ?

Not at the entry level. QuoteIQ currently starts at $29.99/month for one user, while Mobile Tech RX Getting Started starts at $39 per admin/month. However, the products emphasize different workflows, so compare the first plan that contains the features your business actually needs rather than choosing on starting price alone.
