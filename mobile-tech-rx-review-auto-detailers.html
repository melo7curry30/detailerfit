What Is Mobile Tech RX?

Mobile Tech RX is business-management software built around auto reconditioning. It supports detailing alongside PDR, paint and body, interior, PPF/vinyl, wheels, glass, tint and other automotive services.

The core workflow runs from estimate → work order → invoice, with vehicle data, scheduling, customer records and payment tools connected around that process. That automotive-first architecture is the biggest reason to consider it over a generic field-service CRM.

Getting Started: Surprisingly Capable for a Solo Detailer

The $39/month Getting Started plan lets a business choose one service module, including Detail. It includes 50 customer text alerts per month, estimating modules, credit-card processing, unlimited estimates and invoices, scheduling, before/after photos and basic reporting.

Our assessment: that is a strong feature set at the entry price if the business primarily performs detailing. The main limitation is that more sophisticated operational features sit on higher plans.

VIN Scanning and Vehicle Intake

Vehicle intake is one of Mobile Tech RX's clearest strengths. Its workflow tools let technicians scan a VIN to pull vehicle information instead of manually entering every detail. The detailing product page also highlights paint-code access through VIN scanning.

For a detailer working on multiple vehicles per day, that reduces repetitive data entry and creates a more vehicle-centric record than a generic contact CRM.

Estimates and Pricing

Estimating is central to Mobile Tech RX. Businesses can configure pricing and service packages, create estimates, convert work through the job lifecycle and ultimately invoice the customer. The platform's automotive heritage is especially visible in its more specialized estimating modules.

Our assessment: if accurate, repeatable vehicle estimates are a major part of your workflow, Mobile Tech RX deserves serious consideration. A business that sells only a few fixed-price detailing packages may not need all of this depth.

Before/After Photos and Damage Documentation

Mobile Tech RX supports timestamped before-and-after photos. Its detailing tools also emphasize documenting pre-existing vehicle damage and attaching those images to customer-facing documents.

This is more than a marketing feature. Condition documentation can help establish what was already present when a vehicle arrived and gives the business a structured visual record of completed work.

Scheduling and Customer Communication

Scheduling is included even on Getting Started. Mobile Tech RX connects the calendar with customers, vehicles and estimating workflow, and supports customer text alerts for reminders, promotions and updates.

Getting Started currently includes 50 text alerts per month, Standard 100, while Pro lists unlimited text alerts.

Standard: Where the Business Tools Expand

Standard currently costs $99/admin/month plus $29 per additional user. It adds features including basic QuickBooks Online integration, a client portal, technician pay tracking, subcontracting, pre/post diagnostic scan storage, customer statements, deposits on estimates, discounts, CSV exports and email history.

The client portal lets customers approve estimates, make payments and see vehicle repair updates. For a growing operation, that can make Standard materially more useful than the entry plan.

Pro: Workflow Management and Quality Control

Pro currently costs $199/admin/month plus $29 per additional user. Mobile Tech RX positions it for sophisticated auto-recon businesses that want deeper performance, time and workflow management.

Published Pro capabilities include workflow management, time tracking, service and job-specific checklists, COGS/net-revenue tracking, broader QuickBooks functionality, check-in/QA checklists, Zapier integration availability and other advanced operational features.

Workflow Management uses stages to show where vehicles are in the process. Service checklists can standardize work, while time tracking helps management understand repair/service duration by technician and vehicle type.

Our assessment: Pro makes the most sense when a business has enough job volume or staff complexity to benefit from standardized stages, quality control and performance visibility. It is likely overkill for many solo detailers.

CRM and Repeat Business

Mobile Tech RX includes a customer database that stores contact information and history. Automated text and email functionality can be used for appointment reminders, repeat-service follow-up and promotions, while automated review requests support reputation building.

It therefore functions as a CRM, but its identity is broader and more vehicle/workflow-centric than a classic sales CRM.

Payments and Accounting

Card payments can be collected through the platform, including from the app or remotely. QuickBooks functionality starts becoming more important on Standard and expands on Pro.

Businesses considering Mobile Tech RX should compare payment-processing costs and accounting requirements separately from the subscription fee because total software cost is more important than headline plan price.

Who Should Choose Mobile Tech RX?

Choose Mobile Tech RX if...

  • You want software designed around vehicles rather than generic field-service jobs.
  • VIN intake and vehicle documentation matter to your operation.
  • You create detailed estimates instead of relying only on fixed packages.
  • You want before/after photos and damage documentation attached to the workflow.
  • You run multiple auto-recon services such as detailing, tint, PPF or other vehicle services.
  • You need standardized workflow stages and QA as the team grows.

Consider another platform if...

  • You mainly need a lightweight CRM, quotes and invoices.
  • You want unlimited-user pricing as your team expands.
  • Your biggest priority is customer self-booking or route optimization.
  • You do not need automotive-specific estimating or vehicle documentation.

Mobile Tech RX vs Urable: The Short Version

Mobile Tech RX has the edge when the workflow revolves around estimating, VIN intake, vehicle condition documentation and structured auto-reconditioning processes. Urable is especially compelling for broader detailing-business operations, online booking, scheduling/routing and teams that benefit from unlimited-user pricing.

Is Mobile Tech RX Worth It?

DetailerFit verdict

Yes, particularly for vehicle-centric detailing and auto-recon businesses. Mobile Tech RX is more compelling when estimates, VIN data, condition photos and repeatable service workflow are central to the operation. Getting Started offers a credible entry point for a solo detailer, while Standard and Pro make more sense as customer experience, accounting, technician management and workflow complexity increase.

For a simple one-person business, compare the $39 Getting Started plan directly with lower-cost CRM alternatives and decide whether the automotive-specific tools will actually save time. For a growing auto-recon operation, Mobile Tech RX becomes much harder to dismiss.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Mobile Tech RX cost in 2026?

Current US monthly pricing is $39 per admin plus $15 per additional user for Getting Started, $99 plus $29 per additional user for Standard, and $199 plus $29 per additional user for Pro. Taxes and optional add-ons may increase the total.

Is Mobile Tech RX good for auto detailing?

Yes. Mobile Tech RX has a dedicated Detail module and publishes detailing-specific features including VIN scanning, scheduling, estimates, payments, photos, damage documentation, reminders and checklists.

Does Mobile Tech RX scan VINs?

Yes. VIN scanning can populate vehicle information, and the detailing product materials also highlight access to vehicle information and paint codes.

Which plan is best for a solo detailer?

Getting Started is the logical first plan to evaluate because it includes one service module, scheduling, unlimited estimates/invoices, payments, photos and basic reporting. Move up only if you need the additional Standard or Pro capabilities.

Is Mobile Tech RX better than Urable?

It depends on the workflow. Mobile Tech RX is particularly strong for estimating and vehicle documentation. Urable is particularly strong for broad detailing operations, booking, routing and unlimited-user team economics. See our full comparison.
