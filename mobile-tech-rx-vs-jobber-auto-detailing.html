Mobile Tech RX vs Jobber for auto detailers: which is actually the better fit?

Mobile Tech RX and Jobber can both run a detailing business from quote to payment, but they start from different assumptions. Mobile Tech RX is built around the vehicle and auto-reconditioning workflow. Jobber is a broader field-service system built around customers, online booking, jobs, routing and repeatable service operations.

Quick verdict

Choose Mobile Tech RX when the vehicle itself drives the workflow: VIN intake, condition-aware estimating, before/after documentation, automotive service modules and structured auto-recon operations. Choose Jobber when customer acquisition and field-service operations drive the workflow: online booking, Client Hub, lead management, routing, broad integrations and general service-business automation.

For many pure detailing and recon shops, Mobile Tech RX has the more specialized fit. For many consumer-facing mobile detailers selling standardized packages, Jobber can be easier to justify.

The fundamental difference: vehicle-first vs service-business-first

This is the most important part of the comparison.

Mobile Tech RX starts with automotive reconditioning. Its official workflow emphasizes scanning the VIN, pulling vehicle information, creating structured estimates, documenting the vehicle, scheduling work, moving it through the job process and collecting payment. It supports dedicated service modules for Detail, PDR, PPF/Vinyl, Tint, Wheels, Glass, Interior and other automotive work.

Jobber starts with the service business. Its workflow revolves around leads and clients, online requests and bookings, quotes, jobs, schedules, routing, invoices and payments. Jobber now has a dedicated auto-detailing industry page, but the product itself remains a broader field-service platform used across many service categories.

The buying question: Is your operational “unit” primarily the vehicle and estimate, or the customer appointment and field-service job? Your answer predicts the better fit more reliably than the headline price.

Online booking and lead capture: Jobber wins

Jobber publishes online booking on Core. Customers can submit a request form or, when booking is enabled, choose an available appointment based on the business's schedule. Jobber's dedicated auto-detailing page explicitly promotes online booking for detailers through websites, social media and Client Hub.

That matters for a consumer-facing mobile detailer. If someone finds you after business hours, a direct booking path can turn demand into a scheduled job without a text-message conversation.

Mobile Tech RX publishes scheduling on Getting Started and connects scheduling to customers, vehicles and estimates. However, the official Mobile Tech RX sources reviewed for this comparison did not clearly establish an equivalent native customer self-booking flow. That is not the same as claiming the feature does not exist; if self-booking is critical, verify the current workflow with Mobile Tech RX before buying.

Winner: Jobber. It has the clearer, explicitly documented customer self-booking proposition.

VIN scanning and vehicle intake: Mobile Tech RX wins

Mobile Tech RX explicitly publishes VIN scanning as part of its workflow. A technician can scan a vehicle VIN and pull vehicle information instead of entering it manually. The detailing product page also highlights paint-code access through VIN scanning.

This is a meaningful difference. In Mobile Tech RX, the vehicle is not merely a note attached to a customer. It is part of the operational workflow used for estimating and documentation.

Jobber's official materials reviewed here center on clients, property/job information, requests, quotes, jobs and invoices. Jobber may be flexible enough to store vehicle details through fields or job information, but DetailerFit did not find a current official VIN-first workflow comparable to Mobile Tech RX's.

Winner: Mobile Tech RX.

Estimates and quotes: different strengths

Mobile Tech RX's estimating system is one of the strongest reasons to choose it. The company positions standardized estimating and pricing calculators as core automotive capabilities, and Getting Started includes estimating modules plus unlimited estimates and invoices.

For detailing, that can be valuable when price changes with vehicle size, condition, service complexity or other job variables.

Jobber's quoting is more general-purpose, but it is strong from a sales-workflow perspective. Core includes professional quotes. Grow adds richer quote presentation, optional line items and images—useful for add-ons such as pet-hair removal, engine-bay cleaning, polish upgrades or coating packages.

DetailerFit lean: Mobile Tech RX wins when pricing the vehicle correctly is the hard part. Jobber becomes more compelling when presenting, approving and upselling a standardized service quote is the hard part.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX for automotive estimating depth; Jobber Grow for flexible general-service sales quotes.

Before/after photos and damage documentation: Mobile Tech RX wins

Mobile Tech RX includes timestamped before/after photos on Getting Started and its detailing materials explicitly discuss documenting pre-existing damage and attaching images to customer-facing documents.

That is directly relevant to detailers. Vehicle-condition documentation can protect the business when a customer later questions a scratch, dent, wheel mark or other defect that existed before the job.

Jobber supports job notes, photos and attachments in its field workflow, and the pricing documentation also publishes checklists and media-management features on higher tiers. Those tools are useful, but Mobile Tech RX's official product positioning is more explicitly centered on vehicle-condition evidence.

Winner: Mobile Tech RX.

Scheduling, routing and mobile teams: Jobber wins when travel is the bottleneck

Both products publish scheduling.

Mobile Tech RX connects scheduling to customers, vehicles and estimates and publishes employee scheduling inside its workflow. That can work well for a shop or an auto-recon operation where the job itself is complex.

Jobber goes further in the official documentation reviewed here by explicitly publishing routing and route optimization. Jobber's route tools can reorder visits to reduce backtracking, and its auto-detailing industry page specifically markets routing for mobile detailers.

If a mobile business is trying to fit four or six driveway jobs into a day, drive time can become as important as service time.

Winner: Jobber for multi-stop mobile operations. If routing is not a major constraint and vehicle workflow is more important, Mobile Tech RX can still be the better overall platform.

CRM and client experience: Jobber has the broader self-service model

Jobber's Client Hub is available across plans. Customers can view and approve quotes, check appointment details, pay invoices, download documents and request more work. Jobber also publishes lead management and a client CRM that connects the customer to requests, quotes, jobs, invoices and billing history.

Mobile Tech RX also has customer records and publishes a Client Portal on Standard, where customers can approve estimates, make payments and view vehicle-repair updates.

The difference is emphasis. Jobber's customer-facing workflow is central to the product from the entry plan. Mobile Tech RX's portal arrives on Standard and sits inside a more automotive/vehicle-oriented system.

Winner: Jobber for broad customer self-service. Mobile Tech RX remains strong when the customer experience needs to stay attached to a detailed vehicle record.

Payments and deposits: both can close the job

Mobile Tech RX includes credit-card processing on Getting Started. Standard adds deposits on estimates, which can help protect time before a higher-ticket job begins.

Jobber Core publishes invoices and online payments. With Jobber Payments, customers can pay quote deposits and invoices through Client Hub, including supported card and U.S. ACH options. Jobber also supports saved payment methods and automatic payments on eligible workflows.

Verdict: Tie for the core ability to get paid. Compare processing rates and the exact plan required for the payment workflow you want rather than assuming the subscription price tells the whole story.

QuickBooks, integrations and data: Jobber has the broader ecosystem

Mobile Tech RX Standard publishes a basic QuickBooks Online integration. Pro expands QuickBooks functionality and also publishes Zapier availability as an add-on.

Jobber Connect publishes QuickBooks Online sync, and Jobber's pricing and auto-detailing pages promote a large app marketplace and broad integration ecosystem.

For an owner who already depends on multiple business systems—accounting, marketing, payroll or automation—the general-purpose platform can offer more connection points.

Winner: Jobber for integration breadth. Mobile Tech RX is sufficient if the integrations you actually need are already supported.

Checklists, time tracking and profitability: compare the higher tiers

Mobile Tech RX Pro is the important tier for a shop that wants structured operational control. It publishes Workflow Management, time tracking, service checklists, job-specific checklists, check-in/QA checklists and COGS/net-revenue tracking.

Jobber Connect publishes checklists plus time and expense tracking. Grow adds job costing, automatic time tracking, richer quoting, two-way SMS and a custom automation builder. Plus adds even deeper job/media/team features.

These products are solving a similar management problem from different directions:

  • Mobile Tech RX Pro: standardize the automotive job and technician workflow.
  • Jobber Grow: standardize the broader service-business process around sales, jobs, teams and profitability.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX for auto-recon process control; Jobber for broader service-business operations.

Free trial and evaluation window: Jobber gives you more time

Mobile Tech RX currently publishes a 5-day free trial with no commitment. The trial signup page says it includes scheduling, payments, text messaging, unlimited estimates and invoices, plus a personalized walkthrough.

Jobber publishes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Winner: Jobber. Fourteen days gives a detailer more time to run actual jobs through the system before deciding.

Use the trial correctly: Do not spend the entire trial clicking through menus. Recreate one real lead, one quote, one booked job, one photo/documentation workflow, one invoice and one payment. The product that handles your real job with less friction is the better signal.

Which is better for a solo mobile detailer?

For a solo operator, both products have a credible case.

Mobile Tech RX is the stronger first look when...

  • Your price changes materially by vehicle or condition.
  • You want VIN scanning and vehicle information in the same system.
  • Before/after and previous-damage documentation matter every day.
  • You perform detailing alongside PDR, tint, PPF, wheels, paint or other recon services.

Jobber is the stronger first look when...

  • You sell a relatively standardized menu of consumer-facing detailing packages.
  • Customers booking themselves online is a major growth priority.
  • Your biggest pain is organizing leads, quotes, jobs, invoices and payments rather than estimating the vehicle.
  • You expect routing and general field-service tooling to matter as you add more mobile jobs.

Solo verdict: Mobile Tech RX for vehicle-centric specialists; Jobber for appointment-centric mobile service businesses.

Which is better for a growing detailing team?

Team size changes the economics and the feature requirement at the same time.

Mobile Tech RX charges for additional users, but Getting Started remains relatively inexpensive at five users if that feature tier is enough. Once a business needs Standard or Pro, monthly cost rises substantially—but so does the automotive workflow depth.

Jobber sells team configurations across Connect, Grow and Plus. Its value rises when a growing crew needs customer booking, routing, team scheduling, checklists, time/expense tracking, sales automation and job costing in one field-service system.

Growing-team verdict: Jobber has the stronger general field-team operating model; Mobile Tech RX has the stronger vehicle/recon operating model. Calculate both at the exact seat count and required feature tier.

Which is better for a fixed detailing shop or auto-recon business?

A fixed shop does not benefit as much from field-routing tools, which removes one of Jobber's clearer advantages.

At the same time, a shop handling correction, PPF, tint, interior repair, wheels or dealership recon can extract more value from Mobile Tech RX's VIN intake, multiple automotive service modules, estimating, condition documentation, tech-pay tracking and Pro workflow features.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX. The more the business behaves like an auto-reconditioning operation rather than a generic field-service company, the stronger the fit becomes.

Final verdict: do not choose the “better software”—choose the better operating model

DetailerFit verdict

Mobile Tech RX is our lean for vehicle-centric detailing and auto-recon businesses. The combination of VIN intake, automotive estimating, vehicle-condition documentation and Pro workflow control is difficult to replace with a generic field-service system.

Jobber is our lean for consumer-facing mobile detailers whose growth problem is booking and field operations. Online booking on Core, Client Hub, routing, CRM, integrations and a longer trial make it compelling when the job behaves like a standard service appointment.

The wrong way to decide is “Mobile Tech RX is for detailers, therefore it wins” or “Jobber has more general business features, therefore it wins.” The right way is to identify which part of the business currently creates the most friction.

If the hard part is pricing and documenting the vehicle, start with Mobile Tech RX.

If the hard part is turning leads into booked, routed and paid appointments, start with Jobber.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mobile Tech RX or Jobber better for auto detailers?

Mobile Tech RX is the stronger first look when VIN scanning, vehicle-first estimating, condition documentation and auto-recon workflow are central. Jobber is the stronger first look when online booking, customer self-service, routing and a broader field-service CRM matter more.

Which is cheaper, Mobile Tech RX or Jobber?

For one user on flexible monthly billing, Mobile Tech RX Getting Started is currently $39/admin/month while Jobber Core is $49/month. Jobber publishes a lower effective Core rate with annual billing. Team costs can reverse depending on user count and the feature tier required.

Does Jobber have online booking for auto detailers?

Yes. Jobber publishes online booking on Core and has a dedicated auto-detailing product page that describes booking, quoting, scheduling, routing, invoicing, payments and CRM for detailing businesses.

Does Mobile Tech RX scan VINs?

Yes. Mobile Tech RX publishes VIN scanning to retrieve vehicle information, and its detailing materials also highlight paint-code access through VIN scanning.

Can Jobber store photos for detailing jobs?

Jobber publishes notes, photos and attachments as part of field documentation. Higher plans add checklists and deeper media functionality. Mobile Tech RX is more explicit about timestamped before/after and previous-damage documentation for vehicles.

Which is better for mobile routing?

Jobber is the stronger research-based pick because its current official documentation explicitly publishes routing and route optimization. Mobile Tech RX publishes scheduling, but DetailerFit did not establish an equivalent route-optimization claim from the official sources reviewed.

Which is better for a five-person detailing team?

It depends on workflow. Mobile Tech RX can be cheaper if Getting Started is sufficient, while Standard and Pro rise with additional-user charges. Jobber's team tiers become more compelling when the crew needs routing, checklists, time tracking, CRM and field-service automation. Compare the exact required tier, not the lowest headline price.

Which has the better free trial?

Jobber offers more evaluation time: 14 days with no credit card required. Mobile Tech RX currently publishes a 5-day free trial with no commitment.
