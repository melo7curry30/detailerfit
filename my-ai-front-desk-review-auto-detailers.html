What My AI Front Desk Can Handle

The current Business plan combines more than phone pickup. Published capabilities include:

  • AI phone answering and voice: 200 included voice minutes.
  • Appointment booking: the vendor describes booking, rescheduling and no-show follow-up workflows.
  • Two-way SMS: 400 messages per month on Business.
  • Website chatbot: 100 conversations per month.
  • Outbound follow-up: up to 20 outbound calls per day on Business.
  • AI-native CRM: calls, chats and texts can feed customer context into the same front-office system.
  • Sequence automations: follow-up actions can be triggered from conversations.
  • Zapier: included on Business for connecting external tools.
  • Human transfer: agent actions can support call-transfer workflows when configured.

How It Fits an Auto Detailing Business

My AI Front Desk is not a detailing-specific operating system. The vendor does publish a dedicated car-detailing outbound use-case page, showing that detailing is a market it targets, but that does not make the underlying CRM vehicle-first.

The practical fit is front-office lead handling: answering when a technician is polishing or driving, booking routine services, sending texts, following up on leads and keeping contact context in one place.

Strong use case

Routine maintenance detailing, washes or clearly defined packages where the AI can follow approved pricing, service-area and appointment rules.

Needs human escalation

Paint correction, ceramic coating, PPF or unusual vehicle-condition work where final scope and price depend on inspection.

The vendor's detailing page also emphasizes outbound lead response. That can matter if your shop runs paid ads or collects quote requests and wants leads contacted quickly. DetailerFit has not independently measured the resulting booking rate.

Limitations and Overlap Risk

  • Not vehicle-first: do not assume the built-in CRM replaces Urable, QuoteIQ or another system that stores vehicle/job-specific history the way your shop needs.
  • Credit math matters: multiple channels can consume usage, so call volume alone does not determine the final bill.
  • Possible software overlap: if your CRM already includes SMS, booking, automations and customer records, part of the $99 bundle may duplicate tools you already pay for.
  • Complex quotes need boundaries: train the system on approved packages and route uncertain high-ticket jobs to a human.
  • No hands-on DetailerFit finding yet: this article does not score latency, voice naturalness, support or booking accuracy from direct testing.

How a Detailer Should Use the 7-Day Trial

The current pricing page advertises a 7-day free trial. Do not spend the trial only listening to the demo voice. Recreate actual lead situations:

  1. Call after hours asking for a basic detail.
  2. Ask for a time that is unavailable and test rescheduling behavior.
  3. Ask for a complex ceramic-coating or paint-correction quote and confirm it escalates correctly.
  4. Test an outbound follow-up from a new lead.
  5. Inspect the SMS, CRM record and follow-up sequence created from the conversation.
  6. Check credit usage after several realistic calls so you can estimate the monthly bill.
