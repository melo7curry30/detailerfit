Current price

The official OrbisX pricing page, checked September 17, 2026, advertises one plan at $100/month under a limited-time promotion. It describes no feature tiers and no contract commitment, while explicitly allowing taxes and pay-per-use or third-party costs.

For budgeting, twelve months at that advertised rate would equal $1,200 before extras. This is arithmetic at a constant rate, not a published annual contract price or a guarantee of renewal terms. Capture the actual checkout offer before you subscribe; do not treat the promotional OrbisX price as an unconditional permanent list price.

What is included

The core offer is a broad automotive platform rather than separate basic and advanced feature tiers. The pricing page describes scheduling, estimates, invoices, payments, reporting, staff tools and automation. Consult the full OrbisX review for product fit; this guide concentrates on what enters the bill.

Included functionality and included consumption are different. Access to a messaging feature does not by itself establish unlimited messages. Similarly, connecting a processor does not remove transaction charges. Make a list of the tools you intend to use and ask which have a separate supplier or usage meter.

Promotion caveats

The promotion says customers can lock in a promotional price, but this page does not invent a guaranteed duration, future renewal amount or universal eligibility. Ask how cancellation and reactivation affect the offer and whether it applies to your account and business configuration.

Record the offer date, currency, amount due after the trial and the applicable subscription terms. If a salesperson provides a different offer, compare the scope in writing. A lower starting invoice can be less useful than a clearly specified ongoing cost.

Messaging/add-ons

For OrbisX cost, create separate allowances for subscription, taxes, processing and communications. The vendor identifies credits and third-party services such as phone or SMS integrations as possible extras. Their rates depend on the service and usage; no unsupported rate has been inserted here.

Illustrative budget only: a $100 base plus an assumed $25 connected-service allowance makes $125 before tax and payment processing. The $25 is a planning assumption, not an OrbisX quote. Replace it with your provider's estimate for expected messages, calls and any chargeable automation.

Team fit

A solo detailer should first identify what the platform replaces. If you only need a calendar and a few invoices, a broad system may cost more in setup and attention than it saves. For a staffed shop, shared records and connected operations can be more valuable, but feature breadth still needs a practical trial.

To compare OrbisX monthly cost with a seat-based alternative, use the same staff count, permissions and workflow. Confirm account or location rules directly; do not infer an unlimited multi-location entitlement from the absence of feature tiers. A fair comparison includes training and the time spent moving records.

Trial

The published OrbisX free trial lasts 30 days. Confirm payment-method requirements, the date of the first charge and cancellation steps in the signup flow. A trial is most useful when you reserve time to test operations rather than leaving the account untouched until the last day.

Use the first week for service setup and sample imports, the second for quote-to-payment tests and the third for staff access and communications. Before the trial ends, export sample records and check what survives. Do not cancel your existing system until you know how to retrieve historic invoices and customer information.

Alternatives

If the subscription or setup is excessive, compare the specific missing workflow rather than looking for an identical feature list at a lower price. A booking-first solo tool, an Apple-first field app and a vehicle-estimating system solve different problems.

FAQ

Is OrbisX software pricing exactly $100 all-in? No. That is the checked promotional base price. The official pricing page identifies taxes and some usage or connected-service costs as additional.

Is there a separate published annual price here? We have not established one. The $1,200 example is twelve times the monthly promotion, not an annual discount offer.

Does a single plan mean every integration is free? No. Ask who bills each connected service and how usage is counted.

What should I ask before subscribing? Confirm the post-trial charge, promotion terms, communications allowance, processor fees, staff/location scope, export access and cancellation procedure for your account.
