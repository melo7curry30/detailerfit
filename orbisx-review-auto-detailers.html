OrbisX Review (2026): Is It Worth It for Auto Detailers?

OrbisX is an automotive-specific CRM built for detailers, tint and wrap shops, PPF installers and other automotive businesses. We reviewed its current public documentation to see where it fits—and where a simpler platform may make more sense.

Quick verdict

OrbisX is one of the most feature-dense automotive CRMs we have researched. It is especially compelling for established detailers that want booking, CRM, AI automation, estimates, payments, staff management, VIN tools, inventory and mobile fleet functionality in one system. A brand-new solo detailer may find the $100/month promotional price and breadth of features more than necessary.

Research note: DetailerFit has not completed a hands-on test of OrbisX. This review is based on official OrbisX documentation checked September 7, 2026. We separate published facts from our assessment.

What Is OrbisX?

OrbisX is an automotive business-management and CRM platform. The company says it originated from the needs of a detailing and tinting shop that had been using multiple disconnected systems. That background shows in the product's scope: OrbisX is trying to replace a stack of separate calendar, CRM, estimating, invoicing, payment, marketing, inventory and staff tools.

Unlike generic field-service software, the platform explicitly targets auto detailing, mobile detailing, tint, wrap, PPF and related automotive businesses.

OrbisX Pricing in 2026

OrbisX currently advertises a limited-time promotional price of $100/month in USD. It also advertises a 30-day free trial, no contract or commitment, iOS and Android apps, and access to the core feature set without traditional feature tiers.

Important: $100/month is explicitly described by OrbisX as promotional pricing. The vendor also notes that pay-per-use features, credits and third-party services such as communications integrations can create additional costs. Check the current checkout terms before subscribing.

For a one-person startup, $100/month is not the cheapest route. But the economics change if OrbisX replaces several separate subscriptions or if a team needs broad access to one operating system.

Core CRM and Customer Management

OrbisX combines customer records with appointments, estimates, invoices, communications and automotive information. Its public feature set includes unlimited customers, bookings, estimates and invoices, along with communication history and automation.

Our assessment: this is a better fit for a detailer who wants CRM to be the center of the whole operation than for someone who only needs a contact database and basic invoicing.

Online Booking and Scheduling

Online booking is one of OrbisX's stronger areas. The platform publishes customizable booking-form controls, service and package options, deposits, scheduling rules, staff availability and overbooking prevention.

For mobile businesses, automated appointment reminders and customer communication can reduce the amount of manual texting required around each job.

AI and Automation: Luna

OrbisX's AI assistant is called Luna. According to OrbisX, Luna can help rebook past customers, manage inventory, generate reports, write sales scripts and emails, create service descriptions and assist with visual content.

The broader automation system includes review requests, appointment reminders, upsell prompts, referral requests, sales-cycle follow-up, no-show follow-up and weather-related notifications for mobile service providers.

One particularly interesting retention use case is automated rebooking: rules can be configured around previous service timing and calendar availability so the system can reach out to past customers when slower days are approaching.

Our assessment: this is where OrbisX differentiates itself most clearly from a basic scheduling-and-invoicing tool. The value is highest for a business that will actually configure and use the automations.

VIN Scanning and Automotive Workflow

OrbisX publishes AI-based VIN scanning, license-plate detection and receipt scanning. It also lists CARFAX integration and damage-intake functionality among its automotive capabilities.

That matters because a detailer's customer record is only half the story—the service history, condition and identity of the vehicle can be equally important.

Mobile Detailing and Fleet Management

OrbisX has a dedicated mobile-detailing feature set. Published tools include route trip planning, fleet management, GPS tracking, mileage tracking, appointment directions and customer ETA functionality.

For a solo detailer with one van, some of this may be unnecessary. For a company dispatching several mobile units, it can become a major reason to choose OrbisX over a simpler CRM.

Estimates, Invoices and Payments

OrbisX includes estimates, invoices, deposits, expenses, gift cards and online/offline payment functionality. Its integration list includes payment providers such as Stripe, Square and Clover, alongside accounting and business integrations.

The platform also publishes unlimited estimates and invoices as part of its core proposition.

Staff and Operations

OrbisX includes staff-oriented functions such as schedules, time tracking, time off, permissions, payroll-related tracking, tips and commissions. Inventory tools include low-stock warnings, barcode/POS functionality and AI-assisted inventory management.

Our assessment: these functions make the platform more compelling as headcount and operational complexity increase. They also explain why a solo operator may initially feel that OrbisX is more system than they need.

Who Should Choose OrbisX?

Choose OrbisX if...

  • You want an automotive-specific CRM rather than generic field-service software.
  • You operate multiple mobile units or need route/fleet visibility.
  • Automated rebooking, reviews, upsells and lead follow-up matter to your growth strategy.
  • You want vehicle tools such as VIN scanning and damage intake in the same platform.
  • You are trying to consolidate several software subscriptions into one system.
  • Your team needs staff, inventory, payments and CRM functionality together.

Consider something else if...

  • You are a new solo detailer who only needs basic CRM, quotes and invoices.
  • Your primary goal is the lowest possible monthly software cost.
  • You prefer a very simple system with fewer operational features.
  • Your biggest need is specialized estimating and vehicle-condition documentation rather than broad CRM automation.

Is OrbisX Worth It?

DetailerFit verdict

Yes—for the right business. OrbisX makes the strongest case when a detailer wants to run customer management, booking, marketing automation, vehicle intake, payments, staff and mobile operations from one automotive-specific platform. The $100/month promotional price is harder to justify for a brand-new solo operator using only a fraction of those capabilities.

We would put OrbisX high on the demo list for an established detailing shop, a growing automotive appearance business or a multi-van mobile operation. For a lean solo business, compare the real workflow and total cost against Jobber, QuoteIQ, ServiceM8 and Urable before deciding.
