OrbisX vs Jobber for Auto Detailers: Which Is Better? (2026)

OrbisX and Jobber can both run a professional auto detailing business—but they approach the problem from opposite directions.

OrbisX was built specifically for automotive businesses such as detailers, tint shops, PPF installers, wrap shops, and mobile operators. Jobber is a mature general field-service platform used across many service industries, with a dedicated auto detailing solution layered on top of its broader CRM, scheduling, quoting, and field operations system.

That distinction matters. One product starts with the vehicle and automotive workflow. The other starts with the customer, job, technician, and service workflow.

Quick verdict: Choose OrbisX if you want automotive-specific tools such as VIN scanning, damage intake, vehicle-oriented booking, fleet GPS, inventory, AI-assisted follow-up, and marketing in one platform. Choose Jobber if you value a polished general field-service workflow, strong quoting, online booking, scheduling, routing, CRM, and mature job-costing capabilities.

What Is OrbisX?

OrbisX is a CRM and business-management platform designed specifically for automotive professionals. The company markets the product to auto detailers, tint businesses, wrap and PPF shops, mobile detailing companies, and related automotive operators.

Its feature set includes scheduling, estimates, invoices, payments, deposits, online booking, VIN scanning, plate detection, damage intake forms, inventory, staff management, commissions, route planning, fleet GPS, automated reminders, review requests, follow-up, bulk marketing, accounting integrations, and AI-assisted workflows.

OrbisX currently positions these capabilities inside a broad single-plan approach rather than dividing its main feature set across several traditional subscription tiers.

What Is Jobber?

Jobber is field-service management software built for service businesses across many industries. It provides CRM, client portals, requests, online booking, quotes, jobs, scheduling, routing, invoicing, payments, communication, job forms, reporting, and team-management tools.

Jobber also publishes a dedicated auto detailing solution. It highlights detailing use cases such as premium package quotes, optional line items, deposits, route planning, inspection checklists, scratch and stain photos, on-my-way messages, online booking, invoices, and customer follow-up.

Unlike OrbisX, Jobber does not fundamentally treat the vehicle itself as the center of the database. Its strength is a mature service-business workflow that can be adapted to detailing.

Pricing

OrbisX Pricing

OrbisX currently advertises a $100/month limited-time promotional price, a 30-day free trial, no contract, and unlimited staff/user accounts. The vendor states that the core feature set is not split into conventional pricing tiers.

Some messaging credits, third-party integrations, payment processing, or pay-per-use services may create additional costs.

Jobber Pricing

Jobber currently offers multiple plans with pricing that varies by billing method and team size. For one user, published pricing includes:

  • Core: $29/month billed annually or $49 month-to-month
  • Connect: $99/month billed annually or $139 month-to-month
  • Grow: $149/month billed annually or $199 month-to-month
  • Plus: team-based pricing, with advanced scaling and automation features

Core includes the basic customer, booking, quoting, invoicing, payment, website, and reporting workflow. Connect adds automation, checklists, QuickBooks Online sync, time and expense tracking, and more. Grow adds features such as richer quotes, optional line items, job costing, two-way SMS, and custom workflow automation. Plus adds deeper profitability automation, Pipeline, Marketing Suite, AI Receptionist, and team features.

Pricing Winner

For a solo operator who needs basic software: Jobber. Core's $29 annual-billing price is much cheaper than OrbisX.

For a growing team that will use automotive-specific tools: OrbisX can become more attractive. Its current $100 promotional price and unlimited users compare differently once you begin adding Jobber users and higher-tier requirements.

Online Booking

Jobber's online booking can let clients schedule work directly based on team availability. Businesses can also use request forms or assessment bookings when they want to review a lead before putting a full job on the calendar.

Services can have descriptions, estimated prices, estimated durations, and eligible team members. This is useful for standardized detailing services such as maintenance details or interior packages.

OrbisX approaches booking from a more automotive-specific angle. Its published booking functionality includes packages, add-ons, deposits, vehicle selection, mobile-versus-shop rules, staff availability, blocked dates, service limits, waitlists, and rules designed to reduce overbooking.

Winner: OrbisX for detailing-specific booking flexibility.

Customer and Vehicle Management

Jobber has an excellent client-management workflow. Customer information connects to requests, quotes, jobs, invoices, payment history, notes, attachments, and communication inside one platform.

For many service companies, that is exactly what a CRM should do.

OrbisX adds an important extra layer for detailers: the vehicle itself. Published features include VIN scanning, plate detection, automotive records, Carfax connectivity, damage intake workflows, and vehicle-oriented booking.

Winner: OrbisX for auto detailing.

Jobber has the stronger generic customer-service model; OrbisX has the better fit when the vehicle record is operationally important.

Vehicle Inspections and Damage Documentation

Detailers should document scratches, dents, stains, wheel damage, paint defects, and other pre-existing conditions before touching a customer's vehicle.

OrbisX explicitly lists damage intake forms and automotive hardware/features such as VIN scanning and plate detection.

Jobber can attach photos, signed service documents, job details, and forms/checklists to jobs and client records. Its auto detailing page specifically suggests using photos for scratches and stains and custom checklists for detailing services.

Winner: OrbisX.

Jobber can document condition well, but OrbisX is built around an automotive context rather than adapting generic job documentation to a vehicle.

Quotes, Packages, and Upsells

Jobber is very strong here. Detailers can build professional quotes, use optional line items for upgrades, collect signatures and deposits, automate quote follow-up, and let customers approve or request changes online.

Jobber's own auto detailing workflow highlights upsells such as paint correction, interior cleaning, and odor removal.

OrbisX also supports estimates, online acceptance, deposits, alternative options, add-ons, upsells, automated follow-up, and automotive packages.

Winner: Tie.

Both can support a professional higher-ticket sales process for coatings, correction, tint, PPF, and premium detailing packages.

Invoices and Payments

Jobber connects quotes, jobs, invoices, and payments in a clean workflow. Customers can pay through the client portal and supported online payment methods. Jobber also supports automatic payment collection on eligible plans.

OrbisX supports invoices, deposits, payments, stored payment methods, recurring invoices, auto-charge, gift cards, discounts, taxes, and integrations with payment providers such as Stripe, Square, and Clover.

Winner: Tie.

Scheduling, Routing, and Mobile Detailing

Jobber is a field-service platform, so scheduling and routing are major strengths. Its auto detailing solution highlights drag-and-drop scheduling, map and route optimization, team assignment, progress tracking, GPS-related location workflows, turn-by-turn directions, and mobile notifications.

OrbisX also has serious mobile capabilities. It publishes fleet management, route-trip planners, real-time GPS tracking, mileage tracking, mobile appointment directions, ETA messaging, Street View, and route planning.

Winner: OrbisX by a narrow margin for automotive fleets.

Jobber's field-service routing is excellent. OrbisX wins only because it combines similar mobile functionality with vehicle-centric and automotive fleet features.

Job Costing and Profitability

This is one area where Jobber is especially mature.

Jobber's job costing on Grow and Plus tracks job profit, profit margin, materials, labor, and expenses. Plus adds automated job costing that can extract supplier invoice information, match costs to jobs, and generate profit alerts.

OrbisX includes expenses, financial reporting, staff commissions, inventory, reporting, forecasting, and accounting-oriented tools that can help businesses understand their performance.

Winner: Jobber.

For a business owner obsessed with service-level margin and disciplined job costing, Jobber's published workflow is particularly clear and sophisticated.

Automation and AI

Jobber offers automated reminders, quote and invoice follow-ups, custom workflow automations on higher tiers, Marketing Suite capabilities, and an AI-powered Receptionist on Plus.

OrbisX makes AI and automation a much larger part of its core product identity. Its published features include AI-based bookings, automated lead follow-up, review requests, reminders, upsells, rebooking, sales-cycle follow-up, marketing tools, inventory automation, and other customer-growth workflows.

Winner: OrbisX.

Jobber's automation is strong, but OrbisX provides more automotive-specific automation under its current all-in-one positioning.

Team Management

Jobber's plans scale by team size. Higher plans add more users and deeper operational controls. Scheduling, time tracking, team notifications, permissions, routes, and profitability tools can all become part of the workflow.

OrbisX advertises unlimited user and staff accounts and includes staff schedules, time tracking, time off, permissions, payroll-related tracking, tips, commissions, training, task management, and performance tools.

Winner: OrbisX for value as headcount grows.

Jobber remains highly capable, but its pricing becomes more sensitive to team size.

Marketing and Customer Retention

Repeat business is a major part of detailing economics. Maintenance details, coating inspections, seasonal services, interior maintenance, and reactivation campaigns can create recurring revenue from existing customers.

OrbisX provides automated reviews, AI follow-up, bulk SMS/email, loyalty tools, contests, referral features, lead follow-up, upsells, and rebooking-oriented automation.

Jobber provides automated follow-up and customer communication, and higher plans can add more advanced marketing capabilities.

Winner: OrbisX.

Integrations

Jobber connects to QuickBooks Online, Mailchimp, and a large app marketplace, and supports connections to many other tools through its ecosystem.

OrbisX advertises integrations with QuickBooks, Carfax, Zapier, Stripe, Square, Clover, Google-related services, communications providers, and thousands of apps through connected platforms.

Winner: Tie.

The important question is not which platform has the longest logo wall. Verify the specific accounting, payment, marketing, and communication tools your business already uses.

Which Is Better for a Solo Mobile Detailer?

Jobber Core is hard to ignore for a solo operator because it starts at $29/month when billed annually and covers booking, quotes, invoices, payments, CRM, and basic business management.

OrbisX costs more at its current promotional price, but provides much more automotive-specific depth from the start.

Verdict: Jobber for a lean startup; OrbisX for a solo operator who wants a full automotive operating system.

Which Is Better for a Growing Detailing Shop?

Once a business adds employees, high-ticket packages, inventory, damage documentation, commissions, customer reactivation, and automotive-specific workflows, OrbisX becomes increasingly compelling.

Jobber remains an excellent alternative if the team prefers a more conventional field-service workflow and values Jobber's job costing and general service-business ecosystem.

Verdict: OrbisX.

Which Is Better for a Multi-Van Mobile Detailing Team?

Both are strong. Jobber can schedule and route technicians efficiently, while OrbisX adds real-time automotive fleet GPS, mileage tracking, vehicle tools, and mobile-focused automation.

Verdict: OrbisX, narrow edge.

Which Is Better for Job Costing?

Jobber. Grow includes job costing and Plus adds automated job costing. That makes it particularly attractive to owners who want disciplined labor, material, expense, and margin tracking at the job level.

OrbisX vs Jobber: Final Verdict

Best overall for dedicated auto detailers: OrbisX

Best low-cost option for a solo operator: Jobber Core

Best automotive CRM and vehicle workflow: OrbisX

Best job costing: Jobber

Best automotive marketing and AI automation: OrbisX

Best general-purpose field-service platform: Jobber

OrbisX wins this comparison for most established detailing businesses. It is simply more aligned with the way an automotive appearance business operates: vehicles, VINs, damage intake, automotive booking, inventory, mobile fleet management, customer reactivation, and staff workflows all live within the same product philosophy.

Jobber remains a very strong choice. A solo detailer can get started at a much lower price, and businesses that value polished field-service management, professional quoting, routing, client experience, and rigorous job costing may actually prefer it.

In other words: choose OrbisX when detailing-specific workflow is the priority; choose Jobber when general service-business execution is the priority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OrbisX or Jobber better for auto detailers?

OrbisX is our overall pick for dedicated detailing businesses because it is built specifically for automotive professionals. Jobber is a strong alternative for operators who prioritize general field-service management, quoting, scheduling, routing, CRM, and job costing.

Which is cheaper, OrbisX or Jobber?

Jobber Core currently starts at $29/month when billed annually for one user. OrbisX currently advertises a limited-time promotional price of $100/month with unlimited staff/user accounts. The better value depends on your required features and team size.

Does Jobber work for auto detailing?

Yes. Jobber publishes a dedicated auto detailing solution and supports online booking, professional quotes, scheduling, routing, CRM, invoices, payments, reminders, job checklists, photos, and customer communication.

Does OrbisX support VIN scanning?

Yes. OrbisX lists VIN scanning and plate detection among its automotive technology features.

Which is better for mobile detailing?

Both are strong. Jobber has mature field-service routing and scheduling. OrbisX adds automotive fleet GPS, mileage tracking, vehicle tools, route planning, ETA communication, and mobile-focused automation.

Which is better for a detailing shop with several employees?

OrbisX is particularly attractive because it advertises unlimited users and includes broad staff, commission, inventory, booking, vehicle, marketing, and automation tools. Jobber is also capable, but plan and team-size pricing should be compared carefully.

Which is better for job costing?

Jobber has the advantage. Job costing is available on Grow and Plus, while Plus adds automated job-costing capabilities for supplier expenses and profitability tracking.
