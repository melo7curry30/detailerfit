OrbisX vs QuoteIQ: which is better for auto detailers?

QuoteIQ is the easier value pick for a solo operator who wants core CRM tools at a low entry price. OrbisX becomes especially compelling for established shops and growing teams that want unlimited users, automotive-specific workflow, customer booking, VIN and fleet tools, and broad automation under one plan.

Quick verdict

Choose QuoteIQ if you are a solo or very small operator prioritizing price, estimates, invoices, internal scheduling and AI-assisted service-business tools. Choose OrbisX if you want a more automotive-native operating system with unlimited users, online booking, VIN tools, mobile routing, fleet capabilities and a wider all-in-one feature set.

Pricing: low entry cost vs all-in-one value

Price is where these platforms look most different. QuoteIQ gives a solo operator a much lower way in. Its current published plans start at $29.99/month for Essentials, followed by Beginner at $74.99, Pro at $149.99, Elite at $299 and Max at $699. QuoteIQ also advertises a 14-day free trial.

OrbisX currently promotes a $100/month limited-time price, a 30-day free trial and a single-plan philosophy where the published feature set is not split across traditional tiers. That matters because the $100 figure can be more competitive than it first appears once a business needs multiple staff accounts, customer booking or more advanced automotive workflow.

Important: OrbisX labels $100/month as promotional pricing. Compare the live vendor pages before buying. QuoteIQ plan limits and annual-billing discounts can also change.

Business focus: automotive CRM vs broader field service

OrbisX is the more explicitly automotive-native product. Its public materials target detailers, tint shops, PPF installers, wrap businesses and related automotive operations. The feature set includes vehicle-oriented tools such as VIN scanning, plate detection, fleet management, route planning and automotive integrations.

QuoteIQ is broader. Its pricing page positions it as field-service software, while its product and editorial materials also target auto detailing businesses. That broader service-business DNA is not inherently a weakness: for many solo detailers, estimates, invoices, calendar management, payments and lead handling are exactly the workflows that matter.

The difference is mostly one of depth and operating model. OrbisX looks more like an automotive business operating system; QuoteIQ can feel more like a flexible service CRM that a detailer can grow into.

Scheduling and customer self-booking

Both platforms publish internal scheduling. The bigger distinction is customer-facing booking.

OrbisX publishes a customizable online booking form as part of its feature set and promotes AI-assisted booking capabilities. QuoteIQ's internal calendar is available on Essentials, but its customer self-scheduling product, InstaSchedule, is currently published for Elite and Max.

That creates a notable value difference for a detailing business that specifically wants customers to book themselves online. A solo operator who only needs internal scheduling may prefer QuoteIQ Essentials. A shop that considers self-booking essential should compare OrbisX's current $100 promotional plan directly against QuoteIQ Elite at $299/month rather than comparing OrbisX only with QuoteIQ's entry tier.

VIN, vehicle intake and automotive workflow

This is one of OrbisX's clearest differentiators. Its public feature list includes VIN scanning, plate detection, CARFAX integration, route planning and fleet-management tools. Those features are closely aligned with businesses where the vehicle—not just the customer—is the central record.

QuoteIQ can absolutely run the commercial side of a detailing business, but VIN and automotive intake are not the main differentiators emphasized in the official sources we reviewed. If your shop does coatings, tint, PPF, wraps, fleet work or higher-volume vehicle intake, OrbisX deserves a closer look.

Estimates, invoices and payments

Both products cover the basics a detailer expects: estimates, invoices and payment workflow.

QuoteIQ includes estimates, invoices and online payments from its Essentials plan, which is a major reason it remains attractive for a solo business. OrbisX publishes unlimited estimates, invoices, customers, bookings and expenses on its referral and feature pages, plus online and offline payment support.

For a low-volume solo operator, QuoteIQ's entry economics are difficult to ignore. As transaction volume, staff count and operational complexity rise, OrbisX's published lack of booking/customer/estimate/invoice limits becomes more relevant.

AI and automation

Both vendors lean heavily into AI, but they package it differently.

QuoteIQ's current plans use IQ Credits and publish AI features including its AI Virtual Call Team. The number of monthly credits rises with plan level. That gives buyers a reasonably clear plan-based framework for AI usage.

OrbisX emphasizes AI across the operating system, including its booking calendar, inventory-oriented tools and automation stack, alongside automated email and SMS communication. The OrbisX approach is appealing if you want AI woven into a broader automotive CRM rather than purchased mainly as a layer inside a general service workflow.

Neither vendor should be chosen on the word “AI” alone. Compare the exact automation you will actually use: missed-call handling, booking, reminders, follow-up, lead conversion, inventory, customer reactivation or internal operations.

Users, teams and scaling

This category can materially change the value calculation.

OrbisX publishes unlimited users and staff accounts. QuoteIQ currently includes one user on Essentials, two on Beginner, four on Pro, ten on Elite and unlimited users on Max.

That means QuoteIQ can be the obvious value choice for one person, but OrbisX can become financially interesting much earlier for a business with several technicians, front-desk staff or multiple mobile units. Do not compare only the headline starting prices—compare the plan each business would actually need at your team size.

Mobile detailing, routing and fleet tools

OrbisX has a strong published mobile-services story. Its feature set includes route planning, fleet-management GPS tracking and tools aimed at businesses operating vehicles in the field.

QuoteIQ also expands into routing, GPS and operational tools on higher plans, so it is not limited to basic quoting and invoicing. But for a multi-van operation, OrbisX's combination of unlimited users, route planning and fleet emphasis makes it the more natural first comparison.

Which is better for a solo detailer?

QuoteIQ is our first look for most price-sensitive solo detailers. At $29.99/month, Essentials covers estimates, invoices, internal scheduling and payments, while also providing access to QuoteIQ's AI ecosystem.

The main reason to choose OrbisX instead as a solo operator is that you already know you want its automotive-specific depth: customer self-booking, VIN-oriented workflow, vehicle tools, more extensive automation or a system you expect to keep as you add staff.

Which is better for an established shop?

OrbisX gets the edge for many established detailing shops. Once the business has multiple staff members, a fixed location, a meaningful appointment volume or multiple service lines such as detailing, coatings, PPF and tint, its unlimited-user model and broader automotive feature set become easier to justify.

QuoteIQ can still make sense—especially if your team already likes its interface and AI tools—but compare the required QuoteIQ tier, not Essentials, against the OrbisX offer.

Pros and cons

OrbisX pros

  • Automotive-specific product design
  • Unlimited users and staff accounts published
  • Customizable online booking
  • VIN scanning, plate detection, route and fleet tools
  • Broad all-in-one automation and AI feature set

OrbisX cons

  • Higher entry price than QuoteIQ Essentials
  • Feature breadth may be excessive for a brand-new solo business
  • Current $100/month price is explicitly promotional

QuoteIQ pros

  • Low $29.99/month entry plan

QuoteIQ cons

  • User limits on lower plans

Final verdict

There is no single winner for every detailer because the two products optimize for different buying decisions.

QuoteIQ wins the entry-level value battle. A solo detailer who mainly needs quotes, invoices, scheduling, payments and AI-assisted business tools can start for substantially less.

OrbisX wins our comparison for an established or growth-oriented automotive business. Its current combination of unlimited users, online booking, automotive-specific vehicle tools, route/fleet capabilities and broad automation gives the $100 promotional plan a strong value proposition once your workflow extends beyond a single operator.

Our pick by business stage

Solo / just starting: QuoteIQ first.
Growing team / shop / multi-service automotive business: OrbisX first.
