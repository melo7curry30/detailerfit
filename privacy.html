Current data collection

DetailerFit is a static informational website. The on-page Software Finder runs in your browser and does not send your quiz answers to DetailerFit. DetailerFit also uses the analytics described below to understand site usage and outbound CTA interactions.

Hosting

The website may be hosted through a third-party infrastructure provider. Like most web infrastructure, hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser type and timestamps for security and delivery purposes.

Analytics and cookies

DetailerFit uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand traffic and engagement. GA4 may use cookies or similar technologies and process usage information such as page views, device/browser details, referral information and interaction events. DetailerFit's custom events distinguish sponsored affiliate clicks from ordinary vendor outbound clicks and may include the vendor name, clicked URL/host, link text and CTA position. DetailerFit does not send Software Finder answer values as custom analytics parameters. Google and Cloudflare process analytics data under their own privacy terms.

Affiliate links

DetailerFit currently uses affiliate links on selected pages. Affiliate partners may use referral parameters, cookies or similar attribution technology after you click a marked affiliate link so they can identify a qualifying signup or purchase. The relevant vendor's privacy policy governs data processed on that vendor's website.

Children

This site is intended for business software research and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may change as the site adds forms, newsletters, advertising, new analytics features or additional monetization. The updated date will be shown above.
