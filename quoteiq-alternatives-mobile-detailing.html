Before replacing QuoteIQ, compare the plan you actually need

QuoteIQ starts at $29.99/month for Essentials, but its feature ladder matters more than the headline price. Beginner is $74.99, Pro is $149.99, Elite is $299 and Max is $699. User limits are 1, 2, 4, 10 and unlimited respectively.

That structure is attractive for a solo operator who mainly needs estimates, invoices, internal scheduling and payments. But some important workflows arrive later: customer self-booking through InstaSchedule and route optimization begin on Elite at $299/month.

So the right question is not simply, “What costs less than QuoteIQ?” It is, “Which product gives me the workflow I need at the first plan that includes it?”

1. Urable — best detailing-specific alternative for teams

Urable is the first alternative we would compare if your business is specifically automotive and you expect multiple employees to use the platform.

Its U.S. plans are Express at $70/month, Pro at $110/month and Enterprise at $183/month. Urable publishes unlimited users on all three plans, which is a meaningful difference from QuoteIQ's seat limits below Max.

Urable also leans directly into auto detailing, PPF, tint, wrap and other vehicle-service workflows. Its product documentation highlights vehicle records, route management, inspections, scheduling, quoting, invoicing, inventory and team operations.

Where Urable beats QuoteIQ on published plan structure

  • Unlimited users: available on Express, Pro and Enterprise.
  • Customer online booking: included on Pro at $110/month.
  • Automotive specialization: product positioning and workflows are explicitly built around vehicle-service businesses.
  • QuickBooks Online: available on Pro at $110/month.
  • Video inspections: available on Enterprise.

Where QuoteIQ can still make more sense

QuoteIQ starts much cheaper for a solo operator and publishes a broader AI toolkit, including AI-assisted estimating, reporting, image tools and call-related features powered by IQ Credits. If you do not need unlimited users or customer self-booking yet, paying $29.99 instead of $70–$110 can be meaningful.

2. Mobile Tech RX — best for auto recon and PDR-heavy businesses

Mobile Tech RX is less of a generic field-service CRM and more of an automotive reconditioning operating platform. Its current site emphasizes detailing, paintless dent repair, glass, interior repair, paint touch-up, PPF/vinyl, wheels and tint.

Published product material highlights VIN scanning, estimating, scheduling, customer records, checklists, payments, automated marketing messages and automotive pricing tools. For businesses that do detailing alongside PDR or dealer-reconditioning work, that specialization can matter more than a generic CRM feature checklist.

Why consider Mobile Tech RX

  • Automotive-first workflow: designed around auto reconditioning rather than broad home services.
  • VIN-based job flow: its current product page specifically highlights checking vehicles in with VIN scanning.
  • Recon estimating: pricing and estimating tools are a central part of the product.
  • Multiple automotive service modules: useful if your shop does more than detailing.
  • Current homepage trial: the vendor currently advertises a 5-day free trial.
Pricing note: Mobile Tech RX currently publishes Getting Started at $39/admin/month plus $15 per additional user, Standard at $99/admin/month plus $29 per additional user, and Pro at $199/admin/month plus $29 per additional user. Pricing can change, so verify the current plan page before purchasing.

3. Jobber — best broad field-service alternative

Jobber is not detailing-specific, but it is a mature field-service platform with a wide client-management and job-management workflow. For a mobile detailer who wants software that can also support adjacent service businesses, Jobber deserves a serious look.

For one user, Jobber's current no-commitment monthly pricing starts at $49/month for Core, followed by Connect at $139 and Grow at $199. Annual billing brings the one-user entry price down to $29/month for Core, with Connect at $99 and Grow at $149. Larger team pricing increases by tier and seat count.

Core includes online booking, quotes, invoices, online payments, reporting and a website. Connect adds automated reminders, automatic payments, checklists, quote/invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online and time/expense tracking. Grow adds richer quotes, optional line items, job costing, two-way SMS and custom workflow automations.

Why Jobber can beat QuoteIQ

  • Online booking on Core: useful if customer self-booking is important from day one.
  • General field-service maturity: strong fit if your company may expand beyond detailing.
  • Client Hub: a customer-facing area for quotes, appointments and payments.
  • Broad scheduling and routing stack: designed for field teams.

Why QuoteIQ can beat Jobber

QuoteIQ is cheaper on a no-commitment monthly basis at the entry level and its product roadmap is more aggressively centered on AI features. For a solo detailer who does not need online self-booking immediately, QuoteIQ Essentials is a lower-cost starting point.

4. ServiceM8 — best low-cost option for Apple-heavy teams

ServiceM8 is one of the most interesting alternatives on pure entry economics. Its U.S. pricing currently includes a Free plan at $0/month for one user and up to 30 new jobs per month. Starter is $29/month with unlimited users and up to 50 new jobs per month.

Growing is $79/month for 150 jobs, Premium is $149 for 500 jobs and Premium Plus is $349 for 1,500+ jobs. ServiceM8 publishes unlimited users on all paid plans.

The biggest caveat is device strategy. ServiceM8 explicitly says it optimizes for Apple devices and recommends iPhone or iPad to get the most from the platform. It offers ServiceM8 Lite for Android field technicians who need basic functionality.

Why ServiceM8 can beat QuoteIQ

  • Free entry tier: useful for a very small operation testing field-service software.
  • $29 Starter: slightly below QuoteIQ Essentials and includes unlimited users.
  • Unlimited users on paid plans: unusual at this price level.
  • QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations: useful for accounting workflows.
  • Apple-first design: potentially attractive for iPhone/iPad-based teams.

What to watch

ServiceM8 prices plans partly around the number of new jobs per month. That is a different scaling model from QuoteIQ's user-and-feature tiers, so a busy high-volume detailing business should compare job limits—not just monthly price.

5. Housecall Pro — best for a broader home-service operating system

Housecall Pro is another general field-service platform rather than a detailing specialist. Its current U.S. Basic plan is $79/month on monthly billing or $59/month billed annually for one user.

Basic includes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates, invoicing, payments, consumer financing, a price book and reporting. Housecall Pro currently offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Its wider feature set makes more sense for an operator who wants a general service-business platform, especially if detailing is only one part of the company or the business could expand into adjacent home-service categories.

Why Housecall Pro can beat QuoteIQ

  • Online booking on Basic: customer booking is available without moving to a $299 tier.
  • Scheduling and dispatch: core to the product from the entry plan.
  • Broad home-service ecosystem: useful for diversified service businesses.
  • No-card trial: current pricing page says the free trial does not require a credit card.

Why QuoteIQ can beat Housecall Pro

QuoteIQ's $29.99 entry price is substantially lower than Housecall Pro's $79 monthly Basic price. A detailing-only solo operator may also prefer software with stronger alignment to quoting, visual job documentation and the specific workflows emphasized in the detailing market.

QuoteIQ vs its alternatives: the biggest trade-offs

If price matters most

ServiceM8 has the lowest published entry point in this comparison with a free plan, while QuoteIQ Essentials and ServiceM8 Starter sit almost neck-and-neck at $29.99 and $29 per month respectively. Jobber Core reaches $29/month only with annual billing; its no-commitment monthly price is $49.

If customer self-booking matters most

QuoteIQ requires Elite at $299/month for InstaSchedule customer self-booking. Urable includes online booking on Pro at $110. Jobber Core includes online booking, and Housecall Pro Basic includes online booking. This is one of the strongest reasons to compare alternatives before choosing QuoteIQ solely on its entry price.

If team size matters most

Urable and ServiceM8 stand out because their published paid plans include unlimited users. QuoteIQ does not become unlimited until Max at $699/month. Jobber and Housecall Pro price more explicitly around plan and team size.

If automotive specialization matters most

Urable and Mobile Tech RX are the clearest specialist alternatives in this group. Both explicitly market workflows around vehicle-service businesses, while Jobber, ServiceM8 and Housecall Pro are broader field-service products.

When sticking with QuoteIQ is the better move

An alternatives article should not assume switching is the answer. QuoteIQ still makes a lot of sense if you are a solo operator who wants a low monthly starting price, estimates, invoicing, internal scheduling, payments and a large set of AI-assisted tools in one system.

It also gives you a five-tier growth path rather than forcing you to migrate software as soon as your operation becomes more complex. If its exact workflow matches your business, the switching cost of moving customer data, templates, automations and staff habits can outweigh a small monthly price difference.

Decision rule: Do not switch because another product has more features. Switch only if another product solves a high-frequency bottleneck in your business materially better at a total cost you can justify.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best QuoteIQ alternative for mobile detailing?

Urable is our strongest research-based pick for a detailing-specific team workflow, especially when unlimited users and customer online booking matter. Mobile Tech RX is more specialized for auto recon and PDR. Jobber, ServiceM8 and Housecall Pro are broader field-service alternatives.

What is the cheapest QuoteIQ alternative?

ServiceM8 currently publishes a free plan for one user and up to 30 new jobs per month. Its Starter plan is $29/month with unlimited users and a 50-new-job monthly limit. QuoteIQ itself starts at $29.99/month.

Which alternative is best for unlimited users?

Urable publishes unlimited users on every plan. ServiceM8 also publishes unlimited users on all paid plans, although each plan has a monthly new-job allowance.

Which alternative is best if customers need to book online?

Urable Pro, Jobber Core and Housecall Pro Basic all publish online-booking functionality at substantially lower plan prices than QuoteIQ Elite, which is the first QuoteIQ tier with InstaSchedule customer self-booking.

Should a solo mobile detailer leave QuoteIQ?

Not automatically. QuoteIQ Essentials is still one of the lowest-cost published entry plans among serious all-in-one options. A switch makes sense only when another platform solves a specific bottleneck—such as booking, user limits, recon estimating or device compatibility—better for your operation.
