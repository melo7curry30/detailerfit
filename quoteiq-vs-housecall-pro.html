Pricing: QuoteIQ Starts Much Lower

QuoteIQ currently publishes five monthly plans: Essentials at $29.99 for one user, Beginner at $74.99 for two, Pro at $149.99 for four, Elite at $299 for ten and Max at $699 with unlimited users.

Housecall Pro currently publishes Basic at $79 month-to-month or $59/month billed annually for one user, Essentials at $189 monthly or $149/month annual for five users, and Max at $329 monthly or $299/month annual for eight users. Housecall Pro lists $35/month per additional user beyond the eight included on Max.

Online Booking & Scheduling: Housecall Pro Has the Earlier Customer-Facing Path

Housecall Pro publishes Online Booking on Basic, allowing customers to book through Google, a website or a direct booking link. Basic also includes scheduling and dispatch.

QuoteIQ includes its internal job calendar and scheduling tools at the entry level. Its current Help Center documentation says customer-facing self-booking through InstaSchedule starts on Elite and Max, with the Client Portal beginning on Pro but self-scheduling inside it still requiring InstaSchedule.

QuoteIQ source discrepancy: QuoteIQ's live public pricing matrix currently includes wording that can imply online booking/self-scheduling across plans, while its current Help Center pricing guide, overview and InstaSchedule setup article explicitly gate customer-facing InstaSchedule to Elite or Max. DetailerFit uses the more specific Help Center plan-gating documentation here and recommends verifying the entitlement during trial or with support before buying.

For a mobile detailer whose main goal is “customers should book themselves today,” Housecall Pro Basic has the clearer published entry path. For a solo operator who is comfortable scheduling jobs internally, QuoteIQ Essentials can keep software cost much lower.

Quoting & AI: QuoteIQ Puts More AI Inside the Core Product Story

Both platforms support estimates and invoices on their entry plans. QuoteIQ also publishes multiple estimate types and AI-assisted tools across its plans through IQ Credits. Current QuoteIQ documentation lists tools such as AutoPilot, AI CoPilot, Virtual Call Team and AI Estimator, with monthly credit allowances that rise by plan.

Housecall Pro's Basic plan includes quotes and proposals. Its pricing page also lists AI capabilities across its product ecosystem, with some AI and communication products sold or packaged separately. Confirm the exact entitlement and cost of any AI or phone feature you consider essential.

Do not compare “AI included” as a yes/no checkbox. QuoteIQ meters AI usage with IQ Credits. Housecall Pro separates some AI and communication capabilities into distinct products. Compare the actual workflow, included usage and total price.

Photos & Documentation for Detailing Jobs

QuoteIQ Beginner and higher plans publish QuoteIQ Cam, inspection reports, before/after tools, job media and photo annotations. Those capabilities can be useful for recording vehicle condition and keeping job documentation organized.

Housecall Pro Essentials publishes photo reports and annotations. Housecall Pro is broader home-service software rather than automotive-first software, so a detailer should test whether its customer/job structure matches the way vehicles, packages and before/after media are handled day to day.

DetailerFit has not hands-on tested either platform's camera workflow in this comparison, so this section reflects published feature availability rather than a usability score.

Routing & Dispatch: Similar Destination, Different Ladder

Housecall Pro has scheduling and dispatch on Basic, adds Routes on Essentials, and publishes Route Optimization on Max.

QuoteIQ includes internal scheduling on Essentials and publishes automatic route optimization, route-density tools and broader employee operations on Elite and Max.

If dispatch itself is the main reason you are buying software, Housecall Pro exposes more of that field-service structure earlier. If route optimization is the must-have, both comparisons move into higher-priced tiers.

For a five-person team, Housecall Pro Essentials and QuoteIQ Pro sit close if Housecall Pro is billed annually. At ten users, QuoteIQ Elite includes all ten at $299 monthly, while Housecall Pro Max includes eight and publishes a $35/month charge for each additional user beyond eight.

That does not automatically make one platform cheaper overall because the included workflows differ. It does mean headcount should be modeled explicitly rather than treated as an afterthought.

Trials & Switching Risk

Both vendors publish a 14-day trial. Housecall Pro says its trial requires no credit card. QuoteIQ's Help Center says a payment card is required, every trial starts with 250 IQ Credits, and changing plans during the trial ends the trial and bills the newly selected plan immediately.

Use the trial to recreate a real workflow: one estimate, one customer booking path, one completed job, one payment, one follow-up and — if relevant — one multi-stop route. A feature list cannot show how much admin work the workflow actually removes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuoteIQ cheaper than Housecall Pro?

At the entry level, yes. QuoteIQ Essentials is $29.99/month for one user. Housecall Pro Basic is $79 monthly or $59/month billed annually for one user.

Which has cheaper customer online booking?

Based on current specific plan documentation, Housecall Pro. Online booking is included on Basic. QuoteIQ's Help Center says customer-facing InstaSchedule begins on Elite at $299/month, although QuoteIQ's live pricing matrix currently contains conflicting wording that buyers should verify.

Which is better for route optimization?

Both publish automatic route optimization on higher tiers: QuoteIQ on Elite/Max and Housecall Pro on Max. Housecall Pro Essentials also includes route sequencing.

Do both offer free trials?

Yes. Both publish 14-day trials. Housecall Pro says no card is required; QuoteIQ says a payment card is required and the trial starts with 250 IQ Credits.

Which is better for a solo mobile detailer?

QuoteIQ has the much lower entry price. Housecall Pro costs more but includes customer online booking at entry. A solo operator should choose based on whether low cost or self-booking is the more important first requirement.
