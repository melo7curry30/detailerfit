Quick verdict

Start with QuoteIQ if you are a solo mobile detailer who wants estimates, invoices, internal scheduling and payments at the lowest flexible monthly price of the two. Put Jobber high on the shortlist if customer online booking or a broader field-service ecosystem matters immediately.

Entry pricing

QuoteIQ Essentials is published at $29.99/month for one user. Jobber Core for one user is published at $49/month with no commitment, with lower effective rates under longer billing commitments.

The headline price is only useful if the entry plan contains the workflow you need. Compare required features before deciding which product is cheaper for your operation.

For a solo operator

QuoteIQ's Essentials tier covers estimates, invoices, internal scheduling, online payments, contacts and e-signatures. That makes it a strong value proposition for a one-person detailing business that is comfortable manually confirming customer appointment times.

Jobber Core covers quoting, invoicing, online payments and scheduling while also publishing online booking. For a solo operator who spends too much time trading messages with customers about availability, that feature can justify the higher flexible monthly price.

Online booking is an important difference

Jobber publishes online booking on Core. QuoteIQ includes internal scheduling on lower tiers, but customer self-booking through InstaSchedule begins on Elite at $299/month.

If customers choosing their own live appointment slot is a non-negotiable requirement, compare Jobber Core against the QuoteIQ tier that actually contains that feature rather than comparing Jobber against QuoteIQ Essentials.

When automation becomes important

QuoteIQ Pro adds email/text automation, ClientHub calling and texting, a client portal, website contact forms, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration.

Jobber Connect adds automated reminders, automatic payments, quote and invoice follow-ups, checklists, QuickBooks Online and time/expense tracking. Grow adds richer quoting, job costing, two-way SMS and custom workflow automations.

For both products, the correct comparison is the first tier containing your required automation—not the cheapest advertised plan.

Our decision rule

  • Want the lower flexible monthly entry price? QuoteIQ.
  • Want customer online booking on the entry plan? Jobber.
  • Want a broader general field-service platform? Jobber deserves a serious look.
  • Want QuoteIQ's AI-heavy toolkit and detailing-friendly path? QuoteIQ may fit better.
  • Need specific automation? Compare the exact QuoteIQ Pro / Jobber Connect or Grow features before deciding.
