QuoteIQ vs Jobber: cheaper contractor-first start or broader field-service platform?

The right answer depends more on plan level and team complexity than on the logo.

QUOTEIQ

Starts lower on flexible monthly billing

$29.99/month Essentials, 1 user.

JOBBER

Broader field-service ladder

$49/month Core for one user with no monthly commitment; lower effective rate with annual billing.

For a solo operator

QuoteIQ has the lower published flexible monthly entry price. Its Essentials tier already covers estimates, invoices, scheduling and online payments. Jobber Core covers a similar foundation while also positioning itself as a broader field-service platform.

When automation becomes important

Do not compare the two cheapest plans and stop. QuoteIQ gates features such as email/text automation, in-app calling/texting and job costing to Pro. Jobber places automated reminders and quote/invoice follow-ups in Connect, while Grow adds richer job-costing and workflow automation capabilities.

Our decision rule

  • Want the lowest flexible entry price and contractor-focused path? Start with QuoteIQ.
  • Want a broader field-service ecosystem as operations become more structured? Put Jobber on the shortlist.
  • Need a specific automation? Compare the exact tiers that contain it before looking at headline prices.
