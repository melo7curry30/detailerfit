QuoteIQ vs Jobber: lower-cost start or broader field-service platform?

Quick verdict

Choose QuoteIQ first if you are a solo mobile detailer who wants estimates, invoices, payments, internal scheduling and AI tools at the lower flexible monthly entry price. Choose Jobber first if customer self-booking, routing and a more mature general field-service workflow are higher priorities.

There is no universal winner. The correct comparison is the first plan that contains your must-have workflow—not the cheapest headline price.

Pricing: compare the plan you actually need

QuoteIQ starts at $29.99/month on Essentials for one user. Annual billing is published at $299.99/year, or about $25/month effective. Beginner is $74.99/month for two users, Pro $149.99 for four, Elite $299 for ten and Max $699 for unlimited users.

Jobber Core for one user is $49/month with no commitment. Jobber also publishes $39/month with a one-year commitment and $29/month effective when billed annually. Connect and Grow move materially higher as you add automation, routing, time tracking, richer quoting and profitability tools.

Do not compare $29.99 to $29 and stop. QuoteIQ's $29.99 is a flexible monthly price. Jobber's $29 is an annual effective rate. For an apples-to-apples flexible monthly comparison, the current solo entry is QuoteIQ $29.99 vs Jobber $49.

If your required workflow is customer self-booking, the economics change sharply: Jobber publishes that capability on Core, while QuoteIQ's native InstaSchedule self-booking starts on Elite.

Online booking: Jobber wins earlier

This is the clearest plan-gating difference for many mobile detailers. QuoteIQ Essentials includes an internal calendar for scheduling your own work, but customer-facing InstaSchedule starts on Elite at $299/month.

Jobber publishes online booking on Core. Clients can use a request or booking form and, when booking is enabled, choose an available time based on your schedule.

Winner: Jobber when customer self-booking is a must-have. A detailer who manually confirms every appointment may not care, but a business trying to reduce phone/text scheduling friction should price the actual booking tier rather than the entry tier.

Quotes, invoices and payments: both cover the core loop

QuoteIQ Essentials publishes estimates, invoices, online payments, e-signatures, customer contacts and internal scheduling. It also includes consumer-financing options and web, iOS and Android access.

Jobber Core publishes professional quotes, invoices, online payments, scheduling and online booking. Its broader product model connects those tools to client management and the rest of a general field-service workflow.

Verdict: both can handle the basic lead-to-payment loop. QuoteIQ is the lower-cost flexible monthly start; Jobber's advantage is how much customer-facing booking and broader service-business infrastructure sits around that loop.

Automation and AI: QuoteIQ is the more AI-forward product

QuoteIQ publishes AI tools across all plans using IQ Credits. Its 2026 documentation includes tools for CRM assistance, estimating, images, reporting and other automated tasks. Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over, so usage matters when comparing plans.

QuoteIQ Pro is an important operational step-up: it adds email/text automation, ClientHub calling and texting, a client portal, website contact forms, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration.

Jobber Connect adds automated client reminders, automatic payments, checklists, quote/invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online and time/expense tracking. Grow adds richer quoting, job costing, two-way SMS and custom workflow automations.

Winner: QuoteIQ if AI breadth is a primary buying reason. Jobber if you care more about a mature field-service automation stack than AI as a category.

Routing and mobile field workflow

Driving is non-billable time, so routing matters more to a mobile detailer than it does to a fixed-location studio.

QuoteIQ publishes route optimization on Elite, the same tier that introduces customer self-booking. Jobber's Connect documentation publishes routing as part of its scheduling and team workflow.

This means the routing decision is not simply “does the product have a map?” Ask whether your business needs route ordering, technician assignment, real customer time windows and a workflow that still makes sense when several jobs are spread across a city.

Lean: Jobber for a field-service-first operation. QuoteIQ becomes more competitive when you are already moving to Elite for self-booking, routing and broader team features.

Team economics: QuoteIQ is simple to understand, but plan gates matter

QuoteIQ publishes a clear user ladder: one user on Essentials, two on Beginner, four on Pro, ten on Elite and unlimited on Max. That makes seat capacity easy to model, but required features can force a higher tier before user count does.

Jobber's current pricing page displays several team configurations, while its August 2026 Connect Help article says Connect supports up to five users. Because those official sources are not perfectly aligned, DetailerFit treats teams above five conservatively and recommends verifying the exact eligible plan and seat configuration with Jobber before buying.

Decision rule: calculate cost at the team size you expect 12 months from now, not only the seat count you have today.

Free trial and evaluation: same length, different friction

QuoteIQ publishes a 14-day free trial. A card is required to start, and its help center says changing plans during the trial ends the trial and bills the new plan immediately.

Jobber publishes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Use the trial with a real workflow. Recreate one lead, one quote, one booking, one completed mobile job, one invoice and one payment. If routing matters, simulate a day with several addresses. That reveals more than clicking through every menu.

Final decision rule

Solo + low flexible monthly cost + AI/detailing-friendly workflow → QuoteIQ.

Customer self-booking + routing + broader field-service operations → Jobber.

For a one-person mobile detailer who can still manage appointments manually, QuoteIQ Essentials is difficult to ignore at $29.99/month. If self-booking is already a must-have, Jobber Core changes the comparison because that capability is available much earlier in the plan ladder.

Do not buy based on which product has the longest feature list. Buy the first plan that removes the bottleneck that currently wastes the most time or loses the most jobs.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuoteIQ or Jobber better for a solo mobile detailer?

QuoteIQ is the stronger first look when lower flexible monthly cost and a detailing-friendly quote-to-payment workflow matter most. Jobber is the stronger first look when customer online booking and broader field-service operations matter immediately.

Which is cheaper, QuoteIQ or Jobber?

QuoteIQ Essentials is $29.99/month for one user. Jobber Core is $49/month with no commitment for one user, while Jobber publishes $39/month with a one-year commitment and $29/month effective with annual billing.

Does QuoteIQ include online booking?

QuoteIQ includes internal scheduling on lower tiers. Native customer-facing self-booking through InstaSchedule begins on Elite, currently $299/month.

Does Jobber include online booking?

Yes. Jobber publishes online booking on Core.

Which is better for routing?

Jobber is the stronger first look for a field-service-first mobile operation because routing is part of its Connect workflow. QuoteIQ publishes route optimization on Elite.
