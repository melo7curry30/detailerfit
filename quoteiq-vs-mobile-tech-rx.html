Core workflow: service CRM vs automotive operations

The biggest difference is not price. It is what each product appears designed to organize.

QuoteIQ is a service-business platform built around estimates, invoices, internal scheduling, payments, customer communication and an increasingly broad AI toolkit. For a solo mobile detailer trying to replace spreadsheets, texts and disconnected apps, that can be a clean starting point.

Mobile Tech RX is built around auto reconditioning. Its workflow connects customers with vehicles, estimates, work orders, invoices, photos and automotive service data. The product supports detailing alongside other vehicle services such as PDR, paint/body, interior, PPF/vinyl, wheels, glass and tint.

That makes Mobile Tech RX especially interesting when the vehicle record matters as much as the customer record.

VIN scanning and vehicle intake

This is one of Mobile Tech RX's clearest advantages. Its detailing materials publish VIN scanning that can populate vehicle information, and they also highlight access to vehicle information and paint codes.

For a detailer handling multiple vehicles every day, vehicle-first intake can reduce repetitive entry and create a more structured record around the car itself. That becomes more useful for shops combining detailing with tint, PPF, paint correction or other reconditioning work.

QuoteIQ is not positioned around VIN intake in the official sources used for this comparison. Its stronger published differentiators are service workflow, AI tools, customer communication and the way capabilities expand through its plan ladder.

Best fit: If VIN data and vehicle-centric intake are high-priority buying criteria, Mobile Tech RX deserves the stronger first look.

Estimates, invoices and payments

Both platforms cover the commercial core of a detailing job: estimate the work, invoice the customer and collect payment.

QuoteIQ includes estimates, invoices and payments on its entry plan. That is one reason Essentials can be attractive to a solo operator with a straightforward menu of detailing services.

Mobile Tech RX also includes estimating and invoicing on Getting Started, but estimating is more central to the platform's automotive identity. Businesses can configure services and pricing, move work through the estimate-to-work-order-to-invoice lifecycle and connect that process to vehicle records and photos.

Our assessment: QuoteIQ is easier to justify when you mainly need a low-cost service-business workflow. Mobile Tech RX becomes more compelling when accurate, repeatable vehicle estimates are a major operational task.

Scheduling and customer booking

Both platforms publish internal scheduling at the entry level, so either can replace a disconnected calendar for basic job planning.

QuoteIQ makes an important distinction between internal scheduling and customer self-booking. Its customer-facing InstaSchedule feature is published on Elite at $299/month and Max at $699/month.

That means a solo detailer can use QuoteIQ Essentials for internal scheduling without paying $299, but a business that specifically wants customers to choose live availability should compare the Elite tier rather than the $29.99 headline price.

For Mobile Tech RX, this comparison focuses on the scheduling and automotive workflow features that are clearly published in the vendor materials used here. If customer self-booking is a non-negotiable requirement, verify the exact current booking workflow directly with Mobile Tech RX before choosing.

Photos and job documentation

Detailers have a strong reason to care about photo documentation: pre-existing scratches, paint condition, interior damage and completed work can all become part of the job record.

QuoteIQ publishes QuoteIQ Cam, inspection forms, job reports and before/after tools from Beginner. Mobile Tech RX publishes before/after photos on Getting Started and emphasizes timestamped vehicle documentation and pre-existing damage records.

Mobile Tech RX has the stronger entry-tier position here based on the published plan structure, because photo documentation is already part of Getting Started. QuoteIQ's richer photo/inspection toolkit begins one tier above Essentials.

AI and automation

QuoteIQ has the clearer edge if AI-assisted workflow is one of the reasons you are shopping for software. Its current materials describe a broad set of AI capabilities powered by IQ Credits, including tools for estimating, assistance, image work, data import and other workflow tasks.

That does not mean every AI function is unlimited. Businesses should compare the IQ Credit allowances and the specific tools available on the plan they are considering.

Mobile Tech RX is not primarily positioned as an AI-first product in the vendor materials used for this comparison. Its strengths are more concrete and automotive: estimating, VIN intake, condition documentation, vehicle records and structured workflow.

Decision shortcut: Buy QuoteIQ for AI only if the specific AI tools will actually remove work from your process. Buy Mobile Tech RX for vehicle depth only if you will actually use that depth. Feature count by itself is not a buying strategy.

Client portals and customer communication

QuoteIQ's higher tiers expand beyond basic CRM into ClientHub calling/texting, automation, website contact forms and a client portal. Pro is the important tier to inspect when those customer-communication tools become necessary.

Mobile Tech RX Standard adds a client portal that lets customers approve estimates, make payments and view vehicle repair updates. Standard also expands financial and customer-management capabilities beyond Getting Started.

For either product, the right question is not simply whether a portal exists. Ask what you want the customer to accomplish without calling or texting you: approve an estimate, pay a deposit, see job status, book a slot, review documents or communicate with the business.

Team growth and workflow management

QuoteIQ scales through five plan tiers with user limits of one, two, four, ten and then unlimited. That gives a solo operator a cheap starting point, but team growth can push the business into a different tier even before every advanced feature is needed.

Mobile Tech RX prices additional users separately. Its Pro plan is where the deeper management layer becomes prominent, with published capabilities including workflow management, time tracking, service and job-specific checklists, check-in/QA processes and more advanced business controls.

For a small team, neither pricing model is universally better. QuoteIQ bundles users with plan upgrades. Mobile Tech RX separates base-plan capability from additional-user cost. Model your actual headcount and feature requirements before deciding.

Which should a solo detailer choose?

Start with QuoteIQ if:

  • You are one person and want the lowest published entry price.
  • You mainly need quotes, invoices, internal scheduling and payments.
  • AI-assisted tools are a meaningful part of what you want from the platform.
  • You do not currently need deep VIN-driven vehicle intake.
  • You are comfortable evaluating higher QuoteIQ tiers later as the operation grows.

Start with Mobile Tech RX if:

  • Your workflow begins with the vehicle as much as the customer.
  • VIN scanning and vehicle data would save you time.
  • You create detailed or variable vehicle estimates.
  • Before/after photos and condition documentation are central to your process.
  • You may expand into multiple auto-reconditioning services.

Which should a growing shop choose?

As the business grows, the comparison becomes less about $29.99 versus $39 and more about operating model.

A growing shop that wants a broader CRM/automation stack may prefer QuoteIQ's Pro or Elite feature set. A shop that wants standardized automotive workflow, technician time visibility, QA/checklists and structured vehicle processing should inspect Mobile Tech RX Standard and Pro carefully.

Neither vendor should be chosen from the starting price alone. Build a short list of the five workflows your team performs every day, then compare the first plan on each platform that supports all five.

Final verdict: QuoteIQ vs Mobile Tech RX

There is no universal winner because these products solve overlapping problems from different starting points.

QuoteIQ is the better first look for many solo mobile detailers who want low-cost CRM-style operations and a broad AI toolkit. The $29.99 Essentials plan creates a low barrier to moving estimates, invoices, scheduling and payments into one system.

Mobile Tech RX is the better first look for vehicle-centric detailers and auto-reconditioning businesses. VIN scanning, estimating depth, vehicle information, condition documentation and structured workflow are more central to its product identity.

If you are still unsure, identify which of these two sentences sounds more like your business:

  • "I need a simpler way to run customers, quotes, invoices and appointments." Start by evaluating QuoteIQ.
  • "I need a better way to intake vehicles, estimate work and document the job." Start by evaluating Mobile Tech RX.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuoteIQ or Mobile Tech RX cheaper for a solo detailer?

QuoteIQ has the lower published entry price: Essentials is $29.99/month for one user. Mobile Tech RX Getting Started is $39/admin/month. However, price should be compared at the plan that contains the features your business actually needs.

Which is better for VIN scanning and vehicle intake?

Mobile Tech RX. Its detailing materials specifically publish VIN scanning, vehicle information and paint-code access as part of the automotive workflow.

Which is better for AI tools?

QuoteIQ has the stronger published AI emphasis. Its plan materials describe a broader AI toolkit powered by IQ Credits. Mobile Tech RX is positioned more heavily around automotive estimating, vehicle data, documentation and workflow.

Which is better for a new solo mobile detailer?

QuoteIQ is a strong first look if low cost, quotes, invoices, scheduling, payments and AI-assisted tools are the priority. Mobile Tech RX can be a better fit if VIN intake, vehicle documentation and estimating depth already matter to the business.

Does QuoteIQ have customer self-booking?

Yes. QuoteIQ's native InstaSchedule customer self-booking feature is published on Elite and Max. Lower plans include internal scheduling but not InstaSchedule.
