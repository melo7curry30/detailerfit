Pricing: QuoteIQ starts much cheaper

QuoteIQ has the clear advantage if your priority is getting a solo operation off spreadsheets and text messages for the lowest monthly software cost. Essentials starts at $29.99/month and includes one user, estimates, invoices, internal scheduling, payments, contacts, e-signatures and web/mobile access.

Urable starts at $70/month for Express. That is more than twice QuoteIQ's entry price, but the comparison changes when you add employees: Urable's published pricing says all plans allow unlimited users, while QuoteIQ increases user limits by tier.

Online booking: Urable has the stronger price position

This is one of the biggest differences for a mobile detailer.

Urable lists online booking on its Pro plan at $110/month. Its pricing page says Pro can be configured as a request or direct-booking platform, show available time slots, support in-shop or in-field jobs, collect deposits and block the calendar based on estimated duration.

QuoteIQ's customer-facing self-booking feature, InstaSchedule, starts on Elite at $299/month. Lower QuoteIQ plans still include internal scheduling, and Pro adds website contact forms and a client portal, but customers cannot book themselves into live availability until Elite or Max.

Decision shortcut: If online self-booking is a must-have and you are comparing purely on published monthly software cost, Urable Pro has a significant advantage over QuoteIQ Elite.

Routing: Urable can make sense earlier for mobile crews

Mobile detailing businesses lose real money to drive time, poor job sequencing and inefficient service areas. Urable's published pricing matrix includes the ability to create and optimize routes for in-field jobs and assign staff across its plans, including Express.

QuoteIQ places Route Optimization and Route Density Zones on Elite and Max. That means QuoteIQ's routing toolkit arrives with a broader team-operations package, while Urable exposes routing at a lower published subscription price.

If you are running one van and rarely stack multiple jobs across a day, this may not matter yet. If you operate multiple mobile technicians or cover a large service area, routing can become a more important buying criterion than the headline starting price.

Users and team growth: Urable is unusually simple

Urable's three published U.S. plans list unlimited users. That removes one common scaling question: whether adding another technician or office person forces a plan upgrade.

QuoteIQ's limits are one user on Essentials, two on Beginner, four on Pro, ten on Elite and unlimited on Max. The lower entry cost can be excellent for a solo operator, but teams should compare the plan they will actually need—not the $29.99 starting price.

Where QuoteIQ stands out

  • Low-cost solo entry: Essentials is $29.99/month for one user.
  • AI toolkit: QuoteIQ publishes a broad set of AI functions, including AutoPilot, AI CoPilot, AI Estimator, AI image tools, AI Smart Import and a Virtual Call Team, all using IQ Credits.
  • Built-in financing: QuoteIQ says consumer financing through Affirm, Klarna and Afterpay is included across all plans.
  • Growth ladder: Five tiers let a business move from a basic solo workflow to crews, routing, pipelines and unlimited users without changing platforms.
  • 14-day trial: QuoteIQ publishes a 14-day free trial on every plan, with 250 IQ Credits during the trial.

Where Urable stands out

  • Unlimited users: Express, Pro and Enterprise all list unlimited users.
  • Online booking at $110: Pro includes customer online booking, substantially below QuoteIQ's $299 Elite tier.
  • Mobile-route operations: Urable publishes route creation and optimization for in-field jobs across its plans.
  • Automotive workflow: Urable's product pages specifically emphasize auto detailing, vehicle profiles, VIN scanning, inspections, mobile teams, PPF and tint workflows.
  • Pro at $110: Pro adds online booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up messaging, drag-and-drop rescheduling, QuickBooks integration and profit analytics.

Inspections and job documentation

Both platforms support visual job documentation, but their plan structures differ.

QuoteIQ adds QuoteIQ Cam, inspection forms, job reports, before-and-after tools and media storage on Beginner at $74.99/month. Urable lists HD inspection photos on all plans, while high-definition video inspections are an Enterprise feature at $183/month.

For detailers documenting pre-existing scratches, paint condition or high-value coating work, inspection capability can be more than a convenience: it can support clearer customer communication and better job records.

Automation and customer communication

QuoteIQ Pro at $149.99/month adds ClientHub calling and texting, email/text automation, "On The Way" texts, website contact forms and a client portal. Elite adds automated review requests, campaigns and more advanced operational tools.

Urable Express includes automated reminder messaging and an "On My Way" customer message in its pricing matrix. Pro adds long-term follow-up messaging, while Enterprise adds customizable workflows and automated payment-request reminders.

The better option depends on the exact automation you need. QuoteIQ leans heavily into a broader AI-assisted operating system. Urable's published workflow is particularly aligned with automotive service operations.

Which one should a solo mobile detailer choose?

Start with QuoteIQ if: you are working alone, want to keep software spend low, and are comfortable manually confirming appointments rather than giving customers live self-booking.

Start with Urable if: online booking, route optimization or adding team members soon are already part of your operating model and you are willing to pay more than QuoteIQ's entry tier.

For a new solo detailer, the difference between $29.99 and $70–$110 per month is meaningful. But if the cheaper plan forces you to manually handle a workflow that matters every day, the lower sticker price can become false economy.

Which one should a growing detailing team choose?

Urable deserves serious consideration for teams because unlimited users are included across its published plans. Pro at $110 combines unlimited users with online booking, QuickBooks integration and profit analytics.

QuoteIQ becomes more competitive when its specific Pro or Elite feature set matches your operation—for example, ClientHub, AI tools, client portal, job costing, InstaQuote, route density, EmployeeHub or its wider automation stack.

The right comparison is therefore not simply QuoteIQ vs Urable. It is the QuoteIQ plan you actually need vs the Urable plan you actually need.

Final verdict: QuoteIQ vs Urable

There is no universal winner.

QuoteIQ is the stronger value proposition for many solo mobile detailers at the beginning because its $29.99 Essentials plan covers a substantial core workflow at a low monthly cost. It also has a deep AI roadmap and a clear path into more advanced features as the business grows.

Urable can be the better operational value for a team or a booking-heavy mobile business. Unlimited users, online booking on Pro and routing at a lower published plan level can materially change the economics once the business is no longer just one person with one calendar.

Before choosing, identify the one workflow that costs you the most time today—booking, routing, follow-up, quoting, team coordination or job documentation—and compare the first plan on each platform that actually fixes it.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuoteIQ or Urable cheaper?

QuoteIQ is cheaper at the entry level: Essentials is $29.99/month versus Urable Express at $70/month. But Urable includes unlimited users on every published plan, so the cost comparison can reverse for a team.

Which is better for online booking?

Urable has the lower-priced published path to customer online booking. Urable Pro is $110/month. QuoteIQ customer self-booking through InstaSchedule begins on Elite at $299/month.

Which is better for a solo mobile detailer?

QuoteIQ is a strong starting point when the main goal is affordable estimates, invoices, scheduling and payments. Urable becomes more attractive if online booking, route optimization or rapid team growth are priorities from the start.

Which is better for multiple technicians?

Urable's unlimited-user policy across its published plans is a meaningful advantage. QuoteIQ supports one user on Essentials, two on Beginner, four on Pro, ten on Elite and unlimited users on Max.

Do both work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both vendors publish support for web, iOS and Android access.
