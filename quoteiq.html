Which plan should a detailer inspect first?

Solo + internal scheduling: Essentials is the logical starting point. Automation, ClientHub and job costing: inspect Pro. Customer self-booking, routing and EmployeeHub: inspect Elite. Max becomes relevant when unlimited users and crew-level tools matter.

Why it makes the shortlist

QuoteIQ combines estimates, invoices, payments and internal scheduling on the entry plan, while higher tiers add photo documentation, communications automation, job costing, customer self-service, routing and employee operations.

Scheduling and booking

Every plan includes the internal job calendar. True customer self-booking uses InstaSchedule and begins on Elite. That distinction is important enough that we treat internal scheduling and customer booking as separate comparison criteria.

Trial detail worth knowing

QuoteIQ publishes a 14-day trial on all plans. A card is required, and changing plans during the trial ends the trial and bills the new plan immediately. New trials start with 250 IQ Credits.
