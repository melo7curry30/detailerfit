QuoteIQ for mobile detailers: where it fits.

Low published entry price, contractor-focused workflow and deeper automation on higher plans.

8.6/10
Research fit*

Best first look for

Solo or small detailing businesses that want estimates, invoices, scheduling and payments at a relatively low starting monthly price, with an upgrade path into automation and operations.

*Editorial score based on public vendor evidence, not hands-on testing.

Current published pricing

  • Essentials: $29.99/mo, 1 user
  • Beginner: $74.99/mo, 2 users
  • Pro: $149.99/mo, 4 users
  • Elite: $299/mo, 10 users
  • Max: $699/mo, unlimited users

What matters to a detailer

Essentials includes estimates, invoices, scheduling and online payments. Beginner adds items such as review tools and expense tracking. Pro adds email/text automation, in-app calling/texting, job costing and QuickBooks integration. Elite adds route optimization, inventory, dispatching and additional employee operations.

Trade-offs

The plan ladder matters. Some of the more compelling automation and team operations features are not on the entry tier. A buyer should compare the plan they actually need—not only the $29.99 headline.
