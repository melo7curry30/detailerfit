QuoteIQ at a glance

QuoteIQ is a field-service business platform that combines quoting, invoicing, payments, customer management, scheduling and a growing set of AI-assisted tools. It is available through the web plus iOS and Android apps.

For a mobile detailing business, the most important thing to understand is that QuoteIQ's entry price and its advanced operational price are very different. Essentials starts at $29.99/month and already covers core back-office work. Features such as InstaSchedule customer self-booking, route optimization and EmployeeHub begin on Elite at $299/month.

DetailerFit lens: Compare QuoteIQ by the first plan that contains your must-have workflow, not by the lowest advertised price. A solo operator and a multi-vehicle crew can be evaluating completely different versions of the product.

Which QuoteIQ plan fits a mobile detailer?

Essentials: the logical first look for many solo operators

Essentials includes estimates, invoices, online payments, customer management, internal job scheduling and calendar tools, web access, iOS and Android apps, and QuoteIQ's AI features through a monthly IQ Credit allowance. For a solo detailer who mainly needs to quote jobs, schedule work internally, invoice customers and collect payment, this is the tier to inspect first.

Beginner: when documentation starts to matter more

Beginner adds QuoteIQ Cam, inspection forms and related job-documentation tools, along with a larger user allowance and more IQ Credits. That makes it more relevant for detailers who want a more structured photo-and-inspection record around vehicles and jobs.

Pro: when communication and profitability tools become important

Pro adds ClientHub, email and text automation, "On The Way" notifications, website contact forms, a client portal, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration. This tier is more aligned with a business that has moved beyond basic scheduling and invoicing into repeatable customer communication and job-level financial tracking.

Elite: the major operational jump

Elite is the first plan with InstaQuote, InstaSchedule customer self-booking, mass campaigns, pipelines, inventory management, route optimization, route density tools and EmployeeHub. For a mobile detailing team that wants customers to book themselves or wants routing and employee operations inside QuoteIQ, Elite is the key comparison point.

Max: for unlimited users and crew-level operations

Max adds unlimited users, crew creation and scheduling, additional sales-team tools, unlimited pipelines, included additional businesses, the AI Website Builder and a dedicated account manager. It is aimed at operations whose requirements extend well beyond a solo or small-team setup.

Core workflow: estimates, invoices, payments and scheduling

QuoteIQ's entry tier already covers the basic administrative loop a mobile detailer handles every day: create an estimate, convert work into a scheduled job, invoice the customer and collect payment. The vendor also publishes support for card payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay and ACH bank transfers.

This is one of QuoteIQ's strongest structural advantages for a solo operator: you do not have to move to a mid-tier plan just to get the core quote-to-payment workflow.

Important distinction: internal scheduling is not the same as customer self-booking. Every plan includes scheduling and calendar tools, but InstaSchedule customer self-booking starts on Elite.

Customer self-booking: the most important plan boundary

QuoteIQ's official documentation says InstaSchedule is available on Elite and Max. It lets customers select appointments against the availability configured in QuoteIQ, and scheduled appointments are added to the QuoteIQ calendar.

For mobile detailers, that plan boundary matters because self-booking can be a high-frequency workflow. If you want a public booking link that lets customers choose a time without calling or texting you, compare QuoteIQ at $299/month for Elite, not at the $29.99 Essentials price.

Photo documentation and inspection tools

Beginner and higher plans add QuoteIQ Cam and inspection-related tools. For detailing businesses, photo documentation can be useful for recording vehicle condition, organizing job media and creating a more consistent record around each service.

DetailerFit has not hands-on tested the camera or inspection workflow, so we do not make claims about upload speed, image quality, field usability or reliability. Our assessment here is limited to what QuoteIQ officially publishes as included by plan.

Automation, ClientHub and job costing

Pro is where QuoteIQ's business-management layer expands. Official documentation lists ClientHub for calling and texting, automated email and text follow-ups, "On The Way" notifications, website contact forms, a client portal, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration.

For a growing mobile detailing business, this tier is less about adding another basic CRM feature and more about reducing repetitive communication and getting better visibility into job-level profit.

AI features and IQ Credits

QuoteIQ includes AI-assisted features across every plan, but usage is tied to IQ Credits. Paid plans currently include 500 credits on Essentials, 1,500 on Beginner, 3,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Elite and 8,000 on Max. New free trials start with 250 credits regardless of plan.

Official documentation says IQ Credits power tools such as AutoPilot, the Virtual Call Team, AI Estimator, AI image generation and other AI-assisted actions. Credits reset with the billing cycle and unused credits do not roll over.

Buying implication: "AI included" does not mean unlimited AI usage. If AI-heavy workflows are central to your operation, compare both the plan price and the included credit allowance.

Team operations, routing and growth features

Elite adds the operational features most relevant to a larger mobile team: route optimization, route density tools, EmployeeHub, GPS-related employee tools, time tracking, team communication, inventory and pipelines. Max extends this with unlimited users and additional crew-management capabilities.

The plan ladder therefore changes the QuoteIQ value proposition as a detailing business grows. A one-person operation can access the core system at a low price; a larger team that wants routing, self-booking and employee operations is evaluating a much higher monthly tier.

QuoteIQ pros

  • Low published entry price: Essentials starts at $29.99/month.
  • Core workflow on the entry tier: estimates, invoices, payments and internal scheduling are included.
  • Web and mobile access: QuoteIQ publishes web, iOS and Android availability.
  • Broad AI toolkit: AI-assisted features are available across every plan through IQ Credits.
  • Clear growth ladder: higher plans add documentation, automation, self-service, routing and employee operations in defined stages.
  • 14-day trial: every plan currently has a published free trial.

QuoteIQ cons and trade-offs

  • Customer self-booking starts high in the plan ladder: InstaSchedule begins on Elite at $299/month.
  • User limits can force a plan jump: published limits are 1, 2, 4 and 10 users before Max becomes unlimited.
  • AI usage is metered: IQ Credits are a real usage constraint to understand before relying heavily on AI workflows.
  • Large price steps between tiers: the first plan containing a must-have feature can cost substantially more than the entry plan.
  • No hands-on DetailerFit finding yet: we cannot currently score real-world interface speed, support quality, reliability or day-to-day field usability.

Who should choose QuoteIQ?

QuoteIQ deserves a close look if you are a solo or small mobile detailer who wants a low-cost way to manage estimates, invoices, payments and internal scheduling while also having access to AI-assisted tools.

It becomes especially interesting for operators who want to stay inside one platform as they add customer communication, job costing, client self-service, routing and employee operations — provided the higher-tier pricing still makes sense for the business.

For a solo detailer who does not need customer self-booking, Essentials is the clearest starting point. For a growing business that wants automation and job-cost visibility, inspect Pro. For self-booking, routing and employee operations, evaluate Elite directly.

When to compare alternatives

You should compare other products before committing if one of your must-have workflows only appears on a higher QuoteIQ tier than your budget supports, or if you want a platform with a different specialization.

Free trial details worth knowing

Trial caution: If you want to use the full trial period, QuoteIQ's own documentation says not to change plans until the trial ends.
