What changed in QuoteIQ's September update

QuoteIQ's September 11 update covers changes released from August 18 through September 10. The biggest operational change is a rebuilt calendar with Day, Week and Month views, drag-to-reschedule, conflict warnings, reusable templates and a travel ribbon that shows real road distance between stops.

  • Google Calendar: the two-way sync was rebuilt, with one-screen setup, field-level controls, Google events shown inside QuoteIQ and recurring-job sync. Current Help Center documentation lists it from Beginner upward and meters sync activity in IQ Credits.
  • Routing: the travel ribbon is available across accounts; automatic Route Optimization remains Elite/Max and now lives inside the calendar.
  • InstaQuote: QuoteIQ says the customer-facing form was rebuilt with server-side pricing, discount codes, ZIP-based travel fees and a map-measure step. InstaQuote remains an Elite/Max feature.
  • Migration: the September update says CSV import was fixed and contacts can be imported directly from the contact list. That is a meaningful onboarding improvement, although DetailerFit has not hands-on tested field mapping, cleanup requirements or import reliability.

DetailerFit migration lens: this improves QuoteIQ's paper case for businesses moving off spreadsheets or another CRM. We now treat CSV import availability as a positive migration signal, while keeping real-world migration difficulty unscored until it is hands-on tested.

QuoteIQ at a glance

QuoteIQ is a field-service business platform that combines quoting, invoicing, payments, customer management, scheduling and a growing set of AI-assisted tools. It is available through the web plus iOS and Android apps.

For a mobile detailing business, the most important thing to understand is that QuoteIQ's entry price and its advanced operational price are very different. Essentials starts at $29.99/month and already covers core back-office work. QuoteIQ's current documentation lists internal job scheduling and calendar tools on every plan. The Client Portal starts on Pro, while customer self-booking through InstaSchedule, InstaQuote, automatic route optimization and EmployeeHub start on Elite.

DetailerFit lens: Compare QuoteIQ by the first plan that contains your must-have workflow, not by the lowest advertised price. A solo operator and a multi-vehicle crew can be evaluating completely different versions of the product.
Setup / migration: QuoteIQ's September 2026 update says CSV import was fixed and can now be launched directly from the contact list. That lowers one obvious migration barrier, but DetailerFit has not hands-on tested import mapping, duplicate handling or cleanup effort.

Which QuoteIQ plan fits a mobile detailer?

Essentials: the logical first look for many solo operators

Essentials includes estimates, invoices, online payments, customer management, internal job scheduling and calendar tools, web access, iOS and Android apps, and QuoteIQ's AI features through a monthly IQ Credit allowance. For a solo detailer who mainly needs to quote jobs, schedule work internally, invoice customers and collect payment, this is the tier to inspect first.

Beginner: when documentation starts to matter more

Beginner adds QuoteIQ Cam, inspection forms and related job-documentation tools, along with a larger user allowance, more IQ Credits and the rebuilt Google Calendar sync listed from Beginner upward. That makes it more relevant for detailers who want a more structured photo-and-inspection record around vehicles and jobs.

Pro: when communication and profitability tools become important

Pro adds ClientHub, email and text automation, "On The Way" notifications, website contact forms, the Client Portal, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration. The portal is available on Pro, but customer self-scheduling inside it runs on InstaSchedule and therefore requires Elite or Max. This tier is more aligned with a business that has moved beyond basic scheduling and invoicing into repeatable customer communication and job-level financial tracking.

Elite: the major operational jump

Elite remains the first plan with InstaQuote, automatic route optimization, mass campaigns, pipelines, inventory management, route-density tools and EmployeeHub. Internal job scheduling and calendar tools are available across all plans. The Client Portal starts on Pro, while customer self-booking through InstaSchedule starts on Elite.

Max: for unlimited users and crew-level operations

Max adds unlimited users, crew creation and scheduling, additional sales-team tools, unlimited pipelines, included additional businesses, the AI Website Builder and a dedicated account manager. It is aimed at operations whose requirements extend well beyond a solo or small-team setup.

Core workflow: estimates, invoices, payments and scheduling

QuoteIQ's entry tier already covers the basic administrative loop a mobile detailer handles every day: create an estimate, convert work into a scheduled job, invoice the customer and collect payment. The vendor also publishes support for card payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay and ACH bank transfers.

This is one of QuoteIQ's strongest structural advantages for a solo operator: you do not have to move to a mid-tier plan just to get the core quote-to-payment workflow.

September 2026 distinction: QuoteIQ lists internal scheduling and calendar tools across all plans. The Client Portal starts on Pro, but customer self-booking through InstaSchedule, automatic route optimization and InstaQuote start on Elite.

Online booking and self-scheduling: what each QuoteIQ tier includes

QuoteIQ's current documentation lists internal job scheduling and the rebuilt Day / Week / Month calendar on every plan. Google Calendar sync is listed from Beginner upward. These are operator-side scheduling tools and should not be treated as customer self-booking.

The Client Portal starts on Pro, but QuoteIQ's current pricing documentation says customer self-scheduling inside the portal runs on InstaSchedule. InstaSchedule / customer self-booking is first included on Elite at $299/month and is also included on Max.

What starts on Elite: InstaSchedule / customer self-booking, InstaQuote and automatic route optimization are listed on Elite and Max.

Photo documentation and inspection tools

Beginner and higher plans add QuoteIQ Cam and inspection-related tools. For detailing businesses, photo documentation can be useful for recording vehicle condition, organizing job media and creating a more consistent record around each service.

DetailerFit has not hands-on tested the camera or inspection workflow, so we do not make claims about upload speed, image quality, field usability or reliability. Our assessment here is limited to what QuoteIQ officially publishes as included by plan.

Automation, ClientHub and job costing

Pro is where QuoteIQ's business-management layer expands. Official documentation lists ClientHub for calling and texting, automated email and text follow-ups, "On The Way" notifications, website contact forms, a client portal, job costing and QuickBooks Online integration.

For a growing mobile detailing business, this tier is less about adding another basic CRM feature and more about reducing repetitive communication and getting better visibility into job-level profit.

AI features and IQ Credits

QuoteIQ includes AI-assisted features across every plan, but usage is tied to IQ Credits. Paid plans currently include 500 credits on Essentials, 1,500 on Beginner, 3,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Elite and 8,000 on Max. New free trials start with 250 credits regardless of plan.

Official documentation says IQ Credits power tools such as AutoPilot, the Virtual Call Team, AI Estimator, AI image generation and other AI-assisted actions. Credits reset with the billing cycle and unused credits do not roll over.

September AI update: QuoteIQ's current matrix lists the ability to connect a compatible assistant across plans, but tells buyers to check current platform availability. Treat this as rollout-sensitive rather than guaranteed universal access.

Buying implication: "AI included" does not mean unlimited AI usage. If AI-heavy workflows are central to your operation, compare both the plan price and the included credit allowance.

Team operations, routing and growth features

Elite still adds automatic route optimization, route-density tools, EmployeeHub, GPS-related employee tools, time tracking, team communication, inventory and pipelines. The September matrix separately lists road distances between stops across all plans. Max extends this with unlimited users and additional crew-management capabilities.

The plan ladder therefore changes the QuoteIQ value proposition as a detailing business grows. A one-person operation can evaluate core CRM and internal scheduling at the low end; customer self-booking through InstaSchedule, automatic routing, InstaQuote and employee operations push the comparison toward Elite.

QuoteIQ pros

  • Low published entry price: Essentials starts at $29.99/month.
  • Core workflow on the entry tier: estimates, invoices, payments and internal scheduling are included.
  • Web and mobile access: QuoteIQ publishes web, iOS and Android availability.
  • Broad AI toolkit: AI-assisted features are available across every plan through IQ Credits.
  • Clear growth ladder: higher plans add documentation, automation, self-service, routing and employee operations in defined stages.
  • 14-day trial: every plan currently has a published free trial.

QuoteIQ cons and trade-offs

  • Customer self-service is gated: the Client Portal starts on Pro, but customer self-scheduling through InstaSchedule, plus InstaQuote, starts on Elite/Max.
  • User limits can force a plan jump: published limits are 1, 2, 4 and 10 users before Max becomes unlimited.
  • AI usage is metered: IQ Credits are a real usage constraint to understand before relying heavily on AI workflows.
  • Large price steps between tiers: the first plan containing a must-have feature can cost substantially more than the entry plan.
  • No hands-on DetailerFit finding yet: we cannot currently score real-world interface speed, support quality, reliability or day-to-day field usability.

Who should choose QuoteIQ?

QuoteIQ deserves a close look if you are a solo or small mobile detailer who wants a low-cost way to manage estimates, invoices, payments and internal scheduling while also having access to AI-assisted tools.

It becomes especially interesting for operators who want to stay inside one platform as they add customer communication, job costing, client self-service, routing and employee operations — provided the higher-tier pricing still makes sense for the business.

For a solo detailer, Essentials is a strong starting point for estimates, invoicing, payments and internal scheduling. For the Client Portal, automation and job-cost visibility, inspect Pro. For customer self-booking through InstaSchedule, InstaQuote, automatic routing and employee operations, inspect Elite.

When to compare alternatives

You should compare other products before committing if one of your must-have workflows only appears on a higher QuoteIQ tier than your budget supports, or if you want a platform with a different specialization.
