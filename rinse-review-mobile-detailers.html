Quick verdict: Rinse deserves a look for a small detailing operation that wants client and vehicle records, basic scheduling, invoices and a low upgrade price. The permanent Free tier is useful for evaluating the workflow, but its five-active-job limit and Pro-only public booking are important constraints.

What the Free and Pro plans include

Free — $0

Unlimited client and vehicle profiles, five active jobs at a time, basic schedule and job notes, invoices, PDF invoices and customer payments. The free tier does not advertise the public booking widget.

Pro — $6/month

Unlimited active jobs, public booking link and website widget, lead pipeline and quotes, full customer portal, inventory tools, expenses and reports, branding and email automation. Published plan names and gates should be rechecked at checkout.

Where Rinse may fit

Rinse focuses on detailing-native records: customer profiles, vehicle history, job photos and condition documentation, schedule, invoices and payment status. That can matter more to a solo operator than complex team dispatch features. Rinse says Stripe processing charges are separate where card payments apply.

Important trade-offs

  • Desktop-first administration: The founder explained by email that the dashboard was designed for computers. DetailerFit observed compressed layout on iPhone Safari; a native app was in TestFlight at the time of correspondence. Do not assume complete mobile admin parity.
  • Free tier limitations: Five active jobs may be restrictive for a full schedule; online customer booking requires Pro.
  • Early-stage product: Check workflow reliability, mobile experience, customer data export and support expectations before moving a production operation.

Who should compare alternatives?

If you specifically need deposits tied to ZIP-based territory rules and vehicle size surcharges, examine RouteShine. If you need a general booking-first product, compare ServiceLink or Zenbooker. Pricing alone should not determine the decision.

Common questions

Is Rinse free?

Yes. The published Free plan has no subscription charge and allows five active jobs at a time. Unlimited client and vehicle profiles are listed.

Does Rinse include customer online booking?

Public booking links and a website booking widget are listed on Pro, currently $6/month.

Is the Rinse dashboard optimized for iPhones?

The founder described the dashboard as desktop-first in October 2026, with an iOS app in TestFlight. Verify the live mobile experience before adopting it.
