Quick verdict

Best fit: detailers that want online booking, scheduling, customer and vehicle history, invoicing and payment workflow connected in one operational system.

Compare alternatives first if: your priority is a very lightweight booking tool, a marketing-first CRM, or a narrowly specialized estimating or inspection workflow.

What RO App does for a detailing business

RO App's official car-detailing page describes a workflow from enquiry to paid job. Customers can book online, jobs can be organized in a diary, customer and vehicle history can stay together, and invoices and payments can be handled when work is complete.

Online booking and scheduling

RO App documents 24/7 booking, scheduling and automatic reminders. This can reduce manual back-and-forth for mobile detailers and busy studios.

Customer and vehicle history

The detailing-specific page emphasizes keeping customer and vehicle history together, which is more relevant to detailing operations than a generic contact-only CRM when repeat service and vehicle context matter.

Jobs, invoicing and payments

RO App also positions jobs, scheduling, customer communication, inventory and payments inside the same broader system. The practical value is fewer handoffs between disconnected tools.

Mobile operations and automation

RO App says its platform can be used from mobile apps and includes automation and built-in AI. Test the exact mobile workflow your business relies on before migrating from an existing stack.

Pricing note

RO App introduced an updated plan structure in September 2026. Because pricing can vary by plan, region and current offer, this review does not hard-code a monthly price that may go stale. Check the official pricing page for the current plan required by your workflow.

Where RO App may be weaker

An all-in-one operations platform can be more system than a new solo operator needs. A detailer whose only pain point is simple online booking, lead follow-up or quoting may prefer a narrower tool. Specialty PPF, tint or deep inspection workflows should be verified directly rather than assumed.
