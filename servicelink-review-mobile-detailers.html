ServiceLink for Mobile Detailers

ServiceLink focuses on a customer booking page and mobile operations. Its published workflows cover appointments, deposits, quotes, customers and payments. Consider it when customers need a straightforward way to book your detailing services.

Where it may fit

  • Mobile detailers who need a shareable customer booking page.
  • Businesses that collect deposits and manage appointments.
  • Operators who want quotes, customer records and payments accessible on mobile.

What to verify before choosing it

The official site says it is free to start. That does not establish that every feature is free or that there are no payment-processing costs. Check the current pricing page for booking limits, SMS inclusion, paid-plan requirements and transaction charges. The current pricing table lists 5 lifetime online bookings on Free, with SMS on those bookings, and Pro at $20/month with unlimited bookings. Deposits, in-app payments and quote requests are listed on Pro; the pricing page also lists creating and sending invoices on Pro. Confirm current terms before upgrading.

Test one representative detailing service with your vehicle sizes, service duration, travel area, availability and deposit rules. Check customer data export/import, cancellation terms and the device support you require before moving your existing workflow.

Trade-offs and alternatives

A booking-focused product may be a weaker fit if you need detailed vehicle inspections, wrap estimating or complex shop production. Compare BookingKoala for a broader booking workflow, ServiceM8 for field-service operations, and the Software Finder for your business requirements.
