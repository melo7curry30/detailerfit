ServiceM8 for mobile detailers: especially interesting if you live on iPhone.

A free solo tier, paid plans with unlimited users, and an Apple-first product direction.

8.4/10
Research fit*

Best first look for

Solo operators who want to keep software spend close to zero while proving their workflow, plus small teams that value iPhone/iPad field use.

*Editorial score based on public vendor evidence, not hands-on testing.

Current published pricing

  • Free: $0/mo, 1 user, 30 jobs/month
  • Starter: $29/mo, unlimited users, 50 jobs/month
  • Growing: $79/mo, unlimited users, 150 jobs/month
  • Premium: $149/mo, unlimited users, 500 jobs/month
  • Premium Plus: $349/mo, unlimited users, 1,500+ jobs/month

Why it stands out

ServiceM8 says its main product is optimized for Apple devices and recommends iPhone/iPad for the best experience. It includes scheduling, quoting, invoicing and client management across plans, while limits primarily scale with job volume.

Trade-offs

The Apple-first direction is a strength for some teams and a constraint for others. ServiceM8 Lite exists for Android field workers, but buyers with an Android-heavy operation should verify their exact workflow before committing.
