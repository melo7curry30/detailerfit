ServiceM8 for mobile detailers: the strongest $0 starting point if Apple-first fits your business.

A free solo tier, paid plans with unlimited users and online bookings across published plans—built with iPhone and iPad in mind.

ENTRY PRICE$0/mo
PAID USER MODELUnlimited users
DEVICE DIRECTIONApple-first
BEST FIRST LOOK FOR

Solo iPhone/iPad operators and small teams that want scheduling, quoting, invoicing, online bookings and job management without per-user pricing on paid plans.

WATCH BEFORE YOU BUY

The Apple-first direction is a real differentiator. Android field workers can use ServiceM8 Lite, but it is not the same full field experience.

Current published pricing

  • Free: $0/mo · 1 user · 30 jobs/month
  • Starter: $29/mo · unlimited users · 50 jobs/month
  • Growing: $79/mo · unlimited users · 150 jobs/month
  • Premium: $149/mo · unlimited users · 500 jobs/month
  • Premium Plus: $349/mo · unlimited users · 1,500+ jobs/month

Why it makes the shortlist

ServiceM8 publishes scheduling, online bookings, quoting, invoicing and client history across all plans. Paid plans are not priced per user; the main scaling variable is monthly new-job volume plus feature availability.

What mobile detailers should notice

The Dispatch Map and Apple CarPlay are listed across all plans. Auto Routing is an add-on that ServiceM8 says is available across all plans—so “available” should not be read as “included at no extra cost.” That distinction matters when comparing route economics.

Which plan should you inspect first?

Solo and 30 or fewer new jobs per month: start with Free. Up to 50: Starter. Up to 150: Growing. Up to 500: Premium. Higher-volume businesses should inspect Premium Plus.
