Smarfle Review for Solo & Mobile Auto Detailers

Smarfle combines client management, public booking, work orders, invoicing and Stripe payments in one service-business platform. Its $19 Solo plan makes it especially interesting for one-person operators, but it is a general local-service CRM rather than detailing-specific software.

Quick verdict

Best fit: solo or small mobile detailers who want a low-cost system for clients, online booking, work orders, photos, invoices and payments.

Look elsewhere first if: you require a detailing-native workflow with proven vehicle-specific records, detailing package logic or other specialist automotive features.

Why it can fit a mobile detailer

The Solo plan publishes a public booking link and calendar, client management, work orders with photos, invoices, Stripe payments, activity log, tags, custom fields, Mobile PWA and CSV import/export. That covers a useful operating core for a one-person detailing business.

Smarfle's payment documentation currently lists a 0.5% platform fee in addition to payment-processing fees, so compare payment economics before committing.

Where it is weaker for detailing

Smarfle is built broadly for local service businesses. DetailerFit does not treat general custom fields or work orders as equivalent to specialist vehicle inspection, package, coating, shop or automotive workflows. Test those requirements directly before migrating.

Final check before subscribing

Use the free trial to create a customer, book a mobile detail, attach job photos, generate an invoice and test the payment flow. If those steps feel natural and you do not need deeper automotive-specific tooling, Smarfle's Solo plan may be a compelling low-cost option.
