How to read this: Flexible monthly prices are used as the main comparison. Annual-billing figures are shown where DetailerFit has a current published figure. Taxes, payment-processing fees and optional add-ons are excluded. If a required add-on has no clear public price, the result is marked as incomplete rather than guessed.

Why this calculator exists

A $29.99 starting price is only useful if the $29.99 plan contains your workflow. Customer self-booking pushes QuoteIQ to Elite, while Urable publishes online booking on Pro and Jobber publishes online booking on Core. Route optimization creates a different set of plan gates again.

What the calculator uses

  • QuoteIQ: user limits plus Elite gating for customer self-booking and route optimization.
  • ServiceM8: user/job-volume limits; $0.20 per additional job above the 1,500-job Premium Plus allowance; Online Bookings across plans; Auto Routing treated as an unpriced add-on when required.
  • Jobber: current team-size pricing configurations; Core is one user; Connect is the lowest plan with route optimization and is published in 1 / 5 / 10 / 15-user configurations.
  • Urable: unlimited users; Pro for online booking; route creation/optimization published on Express and above.
  • Housecall Pro: included-user counts, published additional-user charges and Max gating for route optimization.
