Shared Core vs SMS Gate

Every current plan advertises booking, deposits through Stripe, quotes, invoices, branded emails, client and vehicle CRM, mobile access and other shop workflows. Two-way customer SMS does not come with Solo; it starts on Pro. Premier additionally provides a locally provisioned number and unlimited capacity.

Why this matters: A solo detailer can still take online bookings and deposits on Solo, but a business that needs SMS conversations or automated text confirmations must compare the Pro price, not just the headline $39 introductory rate.

Solo: One Operator and Email Reminders

Solo lists for $59/month (currently $39/month for the first three months), with one login and 500 clients. It includes online booking, deposits, quotes and invoices plus email confirmations, but not built-in two-way SMS.

Pro: Texting for Small Teams

Pro lists for $89/month (currently $69/month for the first three months), supporting up to five team logins and 1,000 clients. It adds two-way texting, SMS reminders and confirmation messages; it is not an unlimited-seat plan.

Premier: Unlimited Scale and a Local Number

Premier lists for $199/month, with unlimited team logins and clients. It also provides a local business number that Strata provisions and registers with the carrier. Existing Twilio numbers may be connected on eligible Pro/Premier configurations; external messaging charges may apply.

Introductory Discounts vs Long-Term Cost

Strata currently advertises Solo at $39/month and Pro at $69/month for the first three months. After the promotion, the published list prices are $59 and $89 respectively. Premier has no advertised introductory discount. Do not confuse the price-lock claim for the list price with a permanent introductory discount.

Migration & Client Limits

Strata publishes a self-serve CSV import workflow for customers, vehicles and history from other software. It specifically references migration from tools such as OrbisX, Urable and QuickBooks.

The vendor says imports do not count differently from manually entered records. If a shop reaches its client limit, existing clients, vehicles, photos and invoices remain usable; Strata asks the shop to upgrade before adding the next client manually. Public booking requests are not turned away solely because the limit was reached.

Migration still needs testing. A CSV import being available does not prove that every custom field, attachment, automation, payment record or historical workflow migrates cleanly. Export a sample from your current system and validate it during the trial before switching.

Trial, Cancellation & Price Lock

Strata publishes a 30-day free trial. Its current pricing FAQ states that a payment card is required before the trial starts, with no charge until the trial ends. Confirm the live signup terms because other vendor pages may display older no-card wording.

The pricing page also says plans are cancel anytime with no contract, and that the rate you sign up at is locked for the life of the account.

For a detailer, a good 30-day test should include a real booking with deposit, a quote-to-invoice flow, a cancellation or reschedule, an automated reminder, a review request, one vehicle-intake record and — for teams — separate staff logins and job assignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Strata CRM cost?

Solo is $59/month list ($39 for the first three months), Pro $89 ($69 for the first three months), and Premier $199/month. The intro discount and list price are different.

Which plan includes two-way texting?

Two-way SMS starts on Pro. Solo includes email confirmations and reminders, but not built-in two-way texting.

Which plan fits a solo mobile detailer?

Solo offers one login and up to 500 clients with booking and deposits, but operators who need SMS should consider Pro.

Which plan fits a team?

Pro supports up to five team logins and 1,000 clients; Premier supports unlimited logins and clients.

Does Strata offer a free trial?

Yes, 30 days. The current official pricing FAQ says a card is required before the trial starts, although nothing is charged until it ends. Confirm checkout conditions.
