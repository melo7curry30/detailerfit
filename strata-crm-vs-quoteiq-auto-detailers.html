Pricing Model: All Features vs Feature Ladder

Strata currently lists Solo at $59, Pro at $89 and Premier at $199 per month. Solo is $39 and Pro $69 for the first three months under the current promotion. Booking, deposits and invoices are on every plan, but two-way SMS starts on Pro. Solo supports one login/500 clients, Pro up to five logins/1,000 clients, and Premier unlimited logins/clients.

QuoteIQ uses five plans: Essentials at $29.99, Beginner at $99.99, Pro at $199.99, Elite at $299.99 and Max at $699.99 per month. User limits and IQ Credit allowances rise with the plan, and major operational features are introduced at different tiers.

Buying implication: Strata Solo includes booking and deposits but not two-way SMS; QuoteIQ's Essentials has a lower list price but needs a higher tier for InstaSchedule. QuoteIQ can still be the better value when the lower-tier feature set is enough for your workflow.

Customer Booking & Deposits

Strata publishes booking, deposits, quotes, invoices and a client hub across plans; built-in two-way texting starts on Pro. That makes Solo a practical starting point for a solo detailer who wants customers to take a real slot and pay a deposit without moving to a higher tier.

QuoteIQ publishes core scheduling and business-management functionality at lower tiers, while its current pricing page lists InstaQuote & InstaSchedule on Elite at $299.99/month. If customer self-booking is non-negotiable, compare the actual QuoteIQ tier required before treating Essentials as the relevant price.

Automotive Workflow

Strata is explicitly built for automotive businesses and publishes client + vehicle CRM, vehicle intake and records, inventory and true job cost, warranties and shop-focused scheduling. Its positioning covers detailing, mobile detailing, wrap & PPF, window tint and other automotive shops.

QuoteIQ is a broader field-service management platform. It has strong quoting, invoicing, scheduling, payments, AI and team tools, but a detailer should test whether its customer/job structure matches the vehicle-history and intake workflow the shop wants.

Do not confuse specialization with overall quality. Automotive-specific structure can reduce setup work for a detail shop, while a broader field-service platform can offer different automation and estimating advantages.

AI & Automation

QuoteIQ currently says its AI suite is available across every plan, with monthly IQ Credits rising from 500 on Essentials to 8,000 on Max. Its broader AI story includes estimation, automation and business-assistance tools tied into the CRM.

Strata publishes its Assistant and Autopilot as part of the whole product stack on every plan. Its emphasis is operational: ask the shop about its own numbers, queue actions for approval, run follow-up and connect revenue workflows to booking, invoices and customer history.

These are different product philosophies. During a trial, test one real workflow — for example a missed lead, a deposit-backed booking, an overdue invoice and a review request — instead of comparing the word “AI.”

Trials & Switching

Strata publishes a 30-day free trial (its pricing FAQ currently says a card is required) and says every feature is turned on. It also publishes self-serve CSV import and a lifetime price lock for the rate a shop signs up at.

QuoteIQ publishes a 14-day free trial, CSV contact import and a price-lock guarantee on its current pricing page. Its current documentation also says a card is required to start the trial.

For either product, use the trial to import a small real dataset, create a booking, quote a job, collect or simulate a deposit/payment, send a reminder and inspect the customer/vehicle record afterward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper, Strata CRM or QuoteIQ?

QuoteIQ has the lower standard entry price at $29.99/month. Strata Solo lists at $59/month, with $39/month for the first three months currently advertised.

Which gives customer online booking on the cheaper plan?

Strata publishes its booking page and deposits on every plan. Built-in SMS starts on Pro. QuoteIQ's current pricing page lists InstaSchedule on Elite at $299.99/month.

Which is more automotive-specific?

Strata. It is explicitly built for automotive businesses and publishes client + vehicle CRM, intake, job cost and shop-specific workflows. QuoteIQ is a broader field-service platform.

Which has the longer free trial?

Strata publishes 30 days; current pricing FAQ requires a card. QuoteIQ publishes 14 days.

Which is better for a growing team?

The economics differ sharply. Strata Pro supports up to five team logins at $89 standard pricing; Premier at $199 supports unlimited team logins. QuoteIQ includes 1, 2, 4, 10 or 20 users by tier, with extra users at $25/month on every paid plan. Compare feature needs as well as headcount.
