What TrueReview does

TrueReview is a review-management platform built around customer review requests, review monitoring and response workflows. Its published tools include email and SMS review requests, follow-up sequences, review-source monitoring, surveys, shareable links, review widgets, team access and AI-assisted review responses.

For an auto detailing business, the obvious use case is the post-service moment: after a completed detail, coating, tint, PPF or wrap job, the shop can trigger a structured request rather than relying on staff to remember to ask every customer manually.

What changes between plans?

Starter is the lower-cost entry point and centers on email review requests, one location and one campaign. Small Business adds SMS plus email workflows, surveys, broader review-source monitoring and more AI functionality. Premium raises request volume, supports multiple locations, unlimited campaigns and broader team and automation features.

The practical breakpoint for many detailers is SMS. If text-based review requests matter, compare the Small Business plan against the review automation already included in your existing CRM before paying for a second platform.

Review requests and automation

TrueReview publishes SMS and email request workflows, follow-up sequences and detailed request status tracking. It also supports CRM-triggered automation through integrations such as Zapier, which can reduce the need for staff to manually send each request after a job.

For a busy detailing shop, automation matters more than the existence of a review link. The value comes from consistently asking the right customers at the right point in the workflow without adding another recurring task for the owner.

Monitoring, responses and AI tools

TrueReview can monitor supported review sources and centralize review-response work. Published AI features include review-response generation, automated replies on supported reviews, tagging/filtering and Review Radar tools for identifying potential policy issues in incoming Google reviews.

These features are most relevant once a shop has enough review volume that reading, sorting and answering every review manually becomes time-consuming.

Pros for auto detailers

  • Dedicated workflow for asking customers for reviews after completed jobs
  • SMS and email automation on higher plans
  • Central monitoring of supported review sources
  • AI-assisted and automated review responses
  • Surveys and direct-feedback options on qualifying plans
  • Widgets and shareable links for using review content across marketing channels
  • CRM automation options through integrations

Cons and trade-offs

  • It is an additional subscription on top of your core detailing software
  • The lower Starter tier is more limited than the SMS-enabled plans
  • It does not replace scheduling, quoting, invoicing or vehicle-management software
  • Some detailers may already have sufficient review-request automation in their CRM
  • Premium pricing is difficult to justify for a small single-location shop unless review volume and workflow complexity are high

Who should consider TrueReview?

TrueReview is most interesting for detailing businesses that already have a workable operating system for bookings and payments but want a more deliberate reputation-growth process. It becomes easier to justify when review generation is inconsistent, the owner forgets to follow up, or multiple review sources and responses are becoming hard to manage.

Who should skip it?

If your current CRM already sends effective review requests automatically and review volume is manageable, adding a second tool may create more software cost than value. Very new operators may get more immediate ROI from fixing booking, estimating, payments or lead capture first.

Final verdict

TrueReview is a focused reputation-management add-on rather than an all-in-one detailing platform. Its strongest fit is a shop that already has core operations covered and wants a more systematic way to request, monitor and respond to reviews. Compare the Small Business plan especially if SMS automation is the feature you need.
