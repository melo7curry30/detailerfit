Urable vs Jobber for auto detailers: automotive specialist or broader field-service platform?

Jobber has the lower solo entry price and online booking on Core. Urable answers back with unlimited users, detailing-native vehicle tools and route optimization from its $70 Express plan.

Quick verdict

URABLE

Best first look for detailing-specific teams and route-heavy mobile work

Express is $70/month with unlimited users, vehicle-focused workflows and published route creation and optimization.

JOBBER

Best first look for a solo operator who wants a lower entry price

Core is $49/month with no commitment for one user, publishes unlimited jobs and includes online booking.

Our decision rule: choose the plan that matches your real workflow, not the cheaper logo. Jobber is difficult to beat for a solo operator who needs basic quoting, invoicing, scheduling and online booking. Urable becomes especially compelling when you have multiple users, need route optimization, or want vehicle-specific customer and job records.

1. Price: Jobber wins the solo entry-price battle

For one user on flexible monthly billing, Jobber Core is $49/month. Core supports one user and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs and invoices. Jobber also publishes lower commitment and annual rates, including a $29/month effective annual Core rate.

Urable starts at $70/month for Express. Pro is $110/month and Enterprise is $183/month. Urable also publishes yearly totals of $840, $1,320 and $2,200 respectively.

Solo operator with simple needs: if you only need one user, quoting, scheduling, invoicing and customer online booking, Jobber Core has the lower published flexible-monthly cost.

2. Team economics: Urable changes the math fast

The most important pricing difference is not $49 versus $70. It is the user model.

Urable publishes unlimited users on Express, Pro and Enterprise. The company does not make you move up a plan simply because a second, fifth or tenth employee needs access.

Jobber uses team-size configurations. Current no-commitment monthly pricing is:

Core$49/mo1 user
Connect$139–$399/mo1 / 5 / 10 / 15-user configurations
Grow$199–$499/mo1 / 5 / 10 / 15-user configurations
Plus$499–$699/mo5 / 10 / 15-user configurations

That means a growing detailing crew can reach a very different result from a solo business even when both started by looking at Jobber's $49 headline price.

Example: five-person detailing crew

If five people need access and you only need Urable Express features, Urable remains $70/month. Jobber Core does not fit five users; the first published five-user Connect configuration is $199/month.

The correct comparison can still change if you need a feature that pushes Urable from Express to Pro or Enterprise, but the unlimited-user model is a major structural advantage for crews.

3. Online booking: Jobber gets there cheaper

Jobber Core includes Requests & Online Booking. Current Jobber documentation says clients can request work through your website or client hub and can also book directly into your calendar with online booking.

Urable's customer-facing booking system, the Virtual Shop, begins on Pro at $110/month. Urable says the Virtual Shop can be configured for in-shop and in-field jobs, service availability, upsells, capacity rules and deposits.

Internal scheduling is not customer self-booking. Urable Express includes internal scheduling, but its published online-booking features begin on Pro. Jobber Core already publishes online booking.

For a solo detailer whose main software goal is simply letting customers book online, Jobber Core has the stronger price position. For a detailing business that wants a more vehicle-care-specific booking experience and also values Urable's other specialist tools, Pro may justify the higher price.

4. Routing: Urable is much cheaper on published pricing

This is where the comparison flips.

Urable's pricing table lists “Create and optimize routes for in-field jobs and easily assign staff” across Express, Pro and Enterprise. Its dedicated routing documentation describes creating, optimizing and assigning personnel and resources to routes. That puts published route optimization at the $70/month Express level.

Jobber publishes route optimization on Connect, Grow and Plus, not Core. For one user on flexible monthly billing, Connect starts at $139/month. For a five-person team, Connect is $199/month.

SOLO + ROUTING

Urable Express: $70

Published route creation and optimization are included in Urable's Express feature table.

JOBBER + ROUTING

Connect: $139+

Route optimization starts on Connect, with the price increasing by team-size configuration.

For a truly mobile detailing operation doing several driveway jobs each day, routing can matter more than the $21 difference between the two entry plans.

5. Automotive specialization: Urable has the clearer advantage

Urable is built around automotive care workflows. Its current pricing page includes customer vehicle data and image storage, VIN scanning into vehicle profiles, multi-vehicle jobs, vehicle drop-off scheduling and in-field route tools. Pro and Enterprise also publish Carfax-related functionality for obtaining service history and reporting performed services.

That means the customer record is not only “John Smith.” It can also carry information about the vehicle being serviced and the work performed on it.

Jobber is a broader field-service operating system. Its strength is that the same platform can support many service categories, with quoting, online booking, scheduling, invoicing, payments, reporting and a large integration marketplace.

PURE DETAILING / PPF / TINT

Urable is the more natural fit

Vehicle records, VIN-related workflows and automotive-focused scheduling reduce the amount of adaptation required.

MULTI-SERVICE BUSINESS

Jobber deserves the stronger look

Its general field-service model is useful when detailing is only one part of a broader service operation.

6. Automation and growth: the right tier matters more than the brand

Urable Express already includes automated reminder messaging, employee assignment, invoicing, inventory tracking and internal follow-up tools. Pro adds online booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up messaging, QuickBooks integration and profit analytics. Enterprise adds customizable workflows, recurring billing, video inspections, commission tracking and line-item scheduling.

Jobber Connect adds automated reminders, automatic payments, quote and invoice follow-ups, checklists, QuickBooks Online, time and expense tracking and route optimization. Grow adds richer quoting, job costing, two-way SMS and custom workflow automation. Plus adds features for more complex operations, including Jobber's AI-powered Receptionist and additional sales/operations tools.

So the correct comparison is rarely “Urable $70 versus Jobber $49.” It is the first Urable and Jobber plan that contains all of your non-negotiable workflows.

7. Annual pricing: Jobber offers the more obvious discount

Jobber publishes meaningfully lower effective monthly prices when billed annually. Core falls from $49 flexible monthly to a $29/month effective annual rate for one user. Connect, Grow and Plus also have lower annual effective rates by team size.

Urable publishes yearly prices alongside monthly prices: $840/year for Express, $1,320/year for Pro and $2,200/year for Enterprise. Those totals are essentially the same as paying the published monthly rates across a year rather than a large annual discount.

If you are comfortable paying annually, compare the annual economics separately from the flexible-monthly baseline used by DetailerFit's calculator.

8. Free trial: Jobber publishes clearer terms

Jobber publishes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. The trial provides access to Grow features, and Jobber says nothing is deleted if the account expires before billing information is added.

Urable's pricing page uses “Get Started For Free” calls to action and other official pages mention a free trial, but the public pricing documentation reviewed by DetailerFit does not clearly spell out the same trial length and card requirement. Confirm the current trial terms during signup before relying on them.

Real-world scenarios

Solo detailer · no routing · wants online booking

Start with Jobber Core. It is $49/month with no commitment for one user and includes online booking. Urable requires Pro at $110/month for its online-booking platform.

Solo mobile detailer · routing is mandatory

Urable Express is the lower published option. Urable includes route creation and optimization at $70/month. Jobber requires Connect at $139/month for one user.

Five-person crew · routing required

Urable Express has a major headline-cost advantage at $70/month. It includes unlimited users and published route optimization. Jobber Connect is $199/month for the five-user configuration.

Five-person crew · online booking + routing required

Urable Pro is $110/month and combines unlimited users, online booking and routing. Jobber Connect is $199/month for five users and also includes routing plus Core's online-booking capability.

Detailing / PPF / tint shop that tracks vehicles deeply

Put Urable first. Its vehicle data, images, VIN tools, automotive scheduling and higher-tier Carfax functionality are much closer to the underlying objects your business actually manages.

Detailing plus several non-automotive service lines

Put Jobber high on the shortlist. Its broader field-service model and integration ecosystem may be easier to standardize across different kinds of work.

Which should a mobile detailer choose?

  • Choose Urable first if: you run a pure automotive-care business, have multiple users, need route optimization, or value vehicle-specific records and VIN workflows.
  • Choose Jobber first if: you are solo, want the lower flexible monthly entry price, need online booking cheaply, or operate a broader multi-service business.
  • Compare exact tiers if: you need automation, job costing, QuickBooks, advanced workflows or larger-team controls.
  • Do not decide from starting price alone: a routing requirement can make Urable cheaper, while a solo online-booking requirement can make Jobber cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Is Urable or Jobber cheaper for a solo mobile detailer?

Jobber Core has the lower flexible monthly entry price at $49 for one user, compared with Urable Express at $70. But if route optimization is required, Urable Express can become the cheaper published option because Urable includes route creation and optimization on Express while Jobber requires Connect or higher.

Which is better for online booking?

Jobber is cheaper if online booking is the deciding requirement for a solo operator: Core is $49/month. Urable's Virtual Shop starts on Pro at $110/month. Urable's booking system is more automotive-specific, while Jobber is a broader field-service platform.

Which is better for route optimization?

Urable has the stronger published entry price. Routing is listed on Express at $70/month. Jobber route optimization begins on Connect at $139/month for one user on flexible monthly billing.

Which is better for a detailing team with several employees?

Urable can be especially cost-effective because all published plans include unlimited users. Jobber prices Connect, Grow and Plus by team-size configuration, so the gap grows as more employees need access.

Does Urable work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Urable's pricing page publishes web, iOS and Android compatibility across its plans. Jobber is also web-based and supports iOS and Android.

Does Jobber limit the number of jobs on Core?

No. Jobber's current Core documentation says Core supports one user and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs and invoices.
