Urable vs Jobber: Which Is Better for Auto Detailers? (2026)

If you run an auto detailing business, choosing the right software can affect almost every part of your operation — from booking and quoting to customer communication, payments, scheduling, and profitability.

Two popular options are Urable and Jobber.

Both can help replace spreadsheets, paper calendars, disconnected payment tools, and manual customer follow-ups. But they are designed with different types of businesses in mind.

Urable is built specifically around automotive service businesses, including detailing, ceramic coating, tint, and paint protection. Jobber, on the other hand, is a broader field-service management platform used across many service industries.

So which one is better for a detailer?

This Urable vs Jobber comparison breaks down the differences in pricing, booking, CRM, scheduling, invoicing, automation, profitability tools, and overall fit for detailing businesses.

What Is Urable?

Urable is a business management and CRM platform built with automotive service businesses in mind.

It combines many of the tools a detail shop would otherwise need to manage separately, including customer management, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, automated communication, employee management, online booking, and business analytics.

One of Urable's biggest advantages is its automotive focus.

Instead of adapting generic service-business software to a detailing workflow, detailers can use features designed around vehicles and automotive jobs.

Urable is particularly well suited to businesses offering services such as:

  • Auto detailing
  • Ceramic coatings
  • Paint protection film
  • Window tinting
  • Vehicle appearance services
  • Automotive reconditioning

What Is Jobber?

Jobber is a field service management platform designed for small and growing service businesses.

It provides tools for managing leads, customers, quotes, jobs, scheduling, employees, invoices, payments, and customer communication from a single system.

Jobber is used across a much wider range of industries than Urable, including landscaping, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, pressure washing, lawn care, and auto detailing.

However, its core platform remains a general field-service system rather than automotive-specific software.

Urable vs Jobber Pricing

Urable Pricing

  • Express — $70/month
  • Pro — $110/month
  • Enterprise — $183/month

Express includes core functionality such as unlimited users, automated reminder messaging, employee job assignments, invoicing, Stripe and Square integrations, inventory tracking, and analytics.

Pro adds important growth features including online booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up messaging, QuickBooks integration, and profit analytics.

Enterprise adds more advanced capabilities such as recurring billing, video inspections, customizable workflows, commission tracking, automated payment reminders, and line-item scheduling.

One notable advantage is that Urable includes unlimited users across its primary plans, which can make its pricing attractive as a detailing team grows.

Jobber Pricing

Jobber uses tiered pricing with plans designed for businesses of different sizes and operational complexity.

Its pricing can vary based on the plan, billing term, promotions, and number of users.

Higher Jobber tiers unlock more advanced functionality such as job costing, automation, marketing tools, and additional team-management capabilities.

For that reason, don't compare the two platforms based only on the lowest advertised monthly price. Compare the plan that actually includes the features your detailing operation needs.

Booking and Scheduling

Booking is one of the most important parts of modern detailing software. Customers increasingly expect to be able to submit a request or book a service without calling the shop.

Urable

Urable's Pro and Enterprise plans include online booking functionality. Because the software was designed around automotive services, the booking process fits naturally into vehicle-based workflows.

Jobber

Jobber also provides strong online booking and request-management tools. Its scheduling system is particularly useful for mobile teams that need to coordinate multiple employees or jobs throughout the day.

Winner: Tie.

CRM and Customer Management

Both platforms give detailers a central place to manage customer information and service history.

Urable

Urable tracks customer interactions and connects customers with their jobs, invoices, services, and vehicles.

Jobber

Jobber offers robust client management and lead tracking. Customer records can be connected to requests, quotes, jobs, invoices, payments, and communication history.

Winner for detailers: Urable.

Quotes and Estimates

Professional estimates can make a major difference when selling higher-ticket detailing services such as ceramic coatings or paint correction.

Both Urable and Jobber let businesses create digital quotes instead of relying on informal text messages.

Winner: Jobber for general quoting experience; Urable for automotive workflow integration.

Invoicing and Payments

Both platforms can significantly improve the payment process compared with manually creating invoices.

Urable supports text and email invoicing and integrates with payment processors including Stripe and Square. Jobber provides digital invoices and integrated payment functionality through Jobber Payments.

Winner: Tie.

Automation and Follow-Ups

Automation can have an outsized impact on a detailing business. A customer who gets a ceramic coating today may need maintenance services months later, while a customer who requested a quote but never booked may simply need another reminder.

Urable includes automated reminder messaging and, on higher plans, long-term follow-up messaging. Jobber also offers automated customer communication and follow-up capabilities.

Winner: Slight edge to Urable for detailing-specific use.

Vehicle Inspections and Documentation

Detailers regularly need to document scratches, dents, wheel damage, interior condition, or other defects that were present before work began.

Urable's Enterprise plan includes video inspections, allowing businesses to capture vehicle-condition documentation as part of the workflow. Jobber allows files, notes, and job-related media to be stored, but its workflow is not designed specifically around automotive vehicle inspections.

Winner: Urable.

Profitability and Job Costing

Revenue alone does not tell you whether a detailing service is actually profitable. Labor hours, chemicals, pads, coatings, towels, commissions, and other costs can dramatically change the margin on a job.

Urable provides profitability analytics that can account for material and labor costs associated with jobs. Jobber also offers job-costing functionality on eligible plans.

Winner: Tie.

Mobile Detailing: Which Is Better?

Mobile detailers have slightly different requirements from fixed-location studios. Routing, scheduling, team coordination, customer addresses, and field operations become especially important.

Best for route-heavy mobile operations: Jobber.

Best for automotive-specific operations: Urable.

Detailing Shops: Which Is Better?

For a fixed-location detailing studio, Urable becomes particularly compelling. Features such as vehicle drop-off scheduling, automotive customer records, job workflows, profitability analytics, vehicle inspections, and detailing-oriented automation fit naturally into shop operations.

Winner for dedicated detailing shops: Urable.

Urable vs Jobber: Final Verdict

Both Urable and Jobber are capable business management platforms, and either can be a major upgrade from manually managing appointments, customers, estimates, and invoices.

But for a dedicated auto detailing business, Urable is our overall pick.

The reason is specialization.

Urable was built around automotive service workflows, which means features such as vehicle management, inspections, detailing-specific scheduling, profitability tracking, and customer follow-ups fit naturally into the way a detail shop operates.

Jobber remains an excellent option — especially for larger mobile teams or businesses that need broad field-service management functionality.

But if your business lives and breathes detailing, ceramic coatings, paint protection, tint, or automotive appearance services, Urable is the more purpose-built choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Urable better than Jobber for auto detailing?

For most dedicated auto detailing businesses, Urable has an advantage because it is specifically designed around automotive service workflows. Jobber is a broader field-service platform and may be better for route-heavy or multi-service companies.

Does Urable have online booking?

Yes. Online booking is included in Urable's Pro and Enterprise plans.

Can Jobber be used for auto detailing?

Yes. Jobber supports auto detailing businesses and provides booking, scheduling, quotes, CRM, invoicing, payments, and job management.

Does Urable support vehicle inspections?

Yes. Urable offers video inspection functionality on its Enterprise plan.

Which is better for mobile detailers?

Jobber can be particularly strong for mobile detailing companies with multiple technicians and route-heavy operations. Urable may still be the better choice if automotive-specific workflow and vehicle management are more important.

Which is better for ceramic coating shops?

Urable is generally the stronger fit because its automotive-specific workflow is well suited to higher-ticket services such as ceramic coating, paint correction, PPF, and tint.
