Urable vs Mobile Tech RX for Auto Detailers: Which Is Better? (2026)

Urable and Mobile Tech RX are both built around automotive service workflows, but they solve the detailing-business problem from different angles.

Urable behaves more like a complete operating system for a detailing company: CRM, scheduling, online booking, vehicle profiles, inspections, routing, invoicing, payments, inventory, analytics and advanced workflows. Mobile Tech RX has particularly deep roots in auto reconditioning and puts estimating, VIN-based vehicle intake, damage documentation, pricing, technician workflow and quality control near the center of the product.

Quick verdict: Urable is our overall pick for most established detailing businesses that want one system to organize the customer journey from lead and booking through scheduling, field routing, inspection, payment and follow-up. Mobile Tech RX is our pick for estimate-centric automotive workflows, especially when consistent vehicle pricing, VIN scanning, previous-damage documentation and technician QA are major priorities.

What Is Urable?

Urable is CRM and business-management software designed for service businesses with a strong automotive-care focus. Its platform connects customers and vehicles with quotes, jobs, scheduling, inspections, invoices, payments, staff, inventory, routing and analytics.

For auto detailers, important published capabilities include VIN scanning into vehicle profiles, HD inspection photos, customer signatures, vehicle drop-off scheduling, in-field route optimization, employee assignment and multi-location management.

Urable currently offers Express at $70/month, Pro at $110/month and Enterprise at $183/month. All three plans include unlimited users.

What Is Mobile Tech RX?

Mobile Tech RX is business-management software for automotive reconditioning businesses. It supports multiple automotive service modules including Detail, PDR, Paint & Body, Interior, PPF/Vinyl, Wheels, Glass and Tint.

Its detailing workflow is especially focused on professional estimates. Detailers can build service packages, account for vehicle condition and additional work, document existing damage, scan VINs, create invoices, collect payments and use checklists to standardize estimating and quality assurance.

The current U.S. plan structure starts with Getting Started at $39 per admin per month, Standard at $99 per admin per month and Pro at $199 per admin per month. Additional-user pricing varies by tier.

Pricing: Which Offers Better Value?

Urable

  • Express: $70/month
  • Pro: $110/month
  • Enterprise: $183/month

The unusual advantage is that every Urable plan includes unlimited users. That can materially change total cost for a detailing shop with several technicians.

Mobile Tech RX

  • Getting Started: $39/admin/month + $15/additional user
  • Standard: $99/admin/month + $29/additional user
  • Pro: $199/admin/month + $29/additional user

Getting Started includes one service module, scheduling, unlimited estimates and invoices, credit-card processing, before/after photos, basic reporting and a monthly text-alert allowance. Standard and Pro add capabilities for more sophisticated auto-recon businesses.

Winner for a solo operator: Mobile Tech RX. Its $39 entry price is substantially below Urable Express.

Winner as headcount grows: Urable. Unlimited users on every plan can make its economics increasingly attractive for multi-tech shops.

Estimating and Pricing

This is Mobile Tech RX's clearest strength. The platform was designed to help automotive professionals create consistent, professional estimates instead of improvising a price at the vehicle.

Its detailing tools can combine preloaded packages and labor rates with conditional factors based on the actual vehicle and required work. The result is a line-item estimate that helps explain why a job costs what it costs.

Urable also supports quotes and customer approvals, and Pro adds comparative proposals and add-ons. It is capable sales software, but estimating is less central to its product identity.

Winner: Mobile Tech RX.

VIN Scanning and Vehicle Intake

Both products understand that a detailing job is attached to a vehicle, not just a customer.

Urable can scan VINs into vehicle profiles. Pro and Enterprise add functionality to obtain service history and report services through Carfax.

Mobile Tech RX uses VIN scanning to retrieve vehicle information and paint-code data, reducing manual entry during check-in and estimating.

Winner: Tie. Both offer genuine automotive-specific VIN workflows.

Inspections and Damage Documentation

Pre-existing damage documentation matters because a scratch, stain, wheel defect or paint issue can turn into a costly customer dispute if there is no record of the vehicle's arrival condition.

Urable supports HD inspection photos across its plans and adds video inspections on Enterprise.

Mobile Tech RX emphasizes timestamped before/after photography and previous-damage documentation. Images can be tied to customer-facing documentation, while QA checklists help standardize the work before an estimate or invoice is completed.

Winner: Tie. Mobile Tech RX is excellent for photo-based damage documentation; Urable gains an edge for businesses that specifically need video inspections on its Enterprise tier.

Scheduling and Online Booking

Mobile Tech RX includes scheduling even on Getting Started. Its calendar connects appointments with customers, vehicles, estimates and text communication.

Urable has a broader published scheduling stack. It supports tentative quote scheduling, employee assignment, vehicle drop-off times, drag-and-drop rescheduling and automated customer communication. Pro and Enterprise add online booking, while Enterprise adds line-item scheduling.

Winner: Urable. Especially for a business that wants customer self-booking and deeper operational scheduling.

Mobile Detailing and Routing

Mobile operators have a second problem beyond scheduling: geography. Driving an inefficient sequence of jobs directly reduces productive hours.

Urable explicitly supports creating and optimizing routes for in-field jobs and assigning staff to those routes.

Mobile Tech RX is mobile-first in the sense that technicians can manage automotive workflows from the app, but route optimization is not as prominent in its current published detailing and pricing materials.

Winner: Urable.

Payments and Invoicing

Urable supports text and email invoicing and integrates with Stripe and Square. Higher tiers add additional payment and operational automation.

Mobile Tech RX includes unlimited estimates and invoices on Getting Started and offers integrated credit-card processing, allowing a shop to move from estimate to payment within the same operational workflow.

Winner: Tie.

QuickBooks and Accounting Workflow

Urable includes QuickBooks integration beginning with Pro at $110/month.

Mobile Tech RX includes QuickBooks integration beginning with Standard at $99/admin/month.

Winner: Mobile Tech RX for a solo admin on entry price. Team economics should still be calculated separately because Mobile Tech RX charges for additional users while Urable does not.

Workflow and Team Management

Urable can assign employees to jobs across all plans. Enterprise adds customizable workflows, commission tracking, automated pending-action follow-up and line-item scheduling.

Mobile Tech RX Pro is aimed at more sophisticated auto-recon businesses and emphasizes workflow management, technician performance, time and money tracking, analytics and collaboration across technicians.

Winner: Urable for broad business workflow; Mobile Tech RX for technician-centric auto-recon workflow.

Which Is Better for a Solo Detailer?

If the goal is to professionalize estimates, scan VINs, document vehicles, schedule jobs, invoice customers and take payments without spending heavily, Mobile Tech RX Getting Started is compelling at $39/month for one admin.

If online booking, routing and a broader CRM/operations platform matter more—and especially if additional employees will soon need accounts—Urable becomes more attractive.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX for a lean estimate-focused solo operation; Urable for a solo business building toward a larger team.

Which Is Better for a Growing Detailing Shop?

For an established shop with several employees, Urable's unlimited-user pricing, customer booking, scheduling, vehicle profiles, inspections, routing, profitability tools and advanced workflows form a particularly complete operating system.

Verdict: Urable.

Which Is Better for High-Ticket Paint Correction and Coatings?

Mobile Tech RX's structured estimating can be valuable when vehicle condition changes labor requirements and the final price. Its damage photos and QA checklists also fit high-value work well.

Urable counters with proposals, add-ons, inspections, customer approvals, profitability analytics and deeper scheduling.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX for pricing discipline; Urable for the broader customer and shop workflow.

Which Is Better for Multi-Service Auto Recon?

Mobile Tech RX has a major advantage if a company operates beyond detailing. Its supported modules include PDR, hail, paint/body, interior, detail, PPF/vinyl, wheels, glass and tint.

Verdict: Mobile Tech RX.

Mobile Tech RX is more specialized around the moment a vehicle enters the workflow. VIN scanning, structured estimating, condition-based pricing, damage documentation and QA make it particularly attractive to shops where accurately scoping and pricing automotive work is the core challenge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Urable or Mobile Tech RX better for auto detailers?

Urable is our overall pick for growing detailing businesses that want broad CRM, booking, scheduling, routing, inspections and team operations. Mobile Tech RX is particularly strong for automotive estimating, VIN intake, damage documentation and QA.

Which is cheaper?

Mobile Tech RX currently starts at $39/month for one admin on Getting Started. Urable Express is $70/month but includes unlimited users, so team size can reverse the cost advantage.

Do both support VIN scanning?

Yes. Both publish VIN-scanning capabilities designed for automotive workflows.

Which is better for vehicle inspections?

Both are strong. Mobile Tech RX emphasizes timestamped before/after and previous-damage photos. Urable supports HD inspection photos and adds video inspections on Enterprise.

Which is better for mobile detailers?

Urable has the advantage when route optimization is important. Mobile Tech RX remains compelling when the mobile operator's biggest need is professional vehicle estimating and documentation.

Which is better for several technicians?

Urable has a strong pricing advantage because all current plans include unlimited users. Mobile Tech RX charges for additional users, with the exact amount depending on plan.
