Urable vs OrbisX: Which Is Better for Auto Detailers? (2026)

Urable and OrbisX are two of the most compelling software platforms built specifically for auto detailers, tint shops, PPF installers, coating businesses, and other automotive appearance professionals.

That makes this comparison different from matching an automotive CRM against a generic field-service platform. Both products understand that your business revolves around vehicles, inspections, estimates, deposits, appointments, technicians, follow-up, and repeat customers.

The real question is not whether either product can run a detailing business. It is which operating style fits you better.

Quick verdict: We give Urable a slight overall edge for detailers who prioritize a structured automotive workflow, vehicle records, inspections, scheduling, routing, profitability, and operational control. OrbisX is the stronger choice for businesses that want an unusually broad all-in-one feature set with AI-assisted follow-up, marketing automation, flexible booking, fleet tools, and unlimited users under one plan.

What Is Urable?

Urable is business-management and CRM software designed around automotive care and other service businesses. For detailers, its workflow connects customers, vehicles, quotes, jobs, scheduling, invoices, payments, inspections, routes, employees, inventory, and analytics.

The automotive focus matters. Urable can store customer vehicle data and images, scan VINs into vehicle profiles, capture inspection photos, schedule vehicle drop-off times, manage multi-vehicle or fleet jobs, and create routes for field work.

Higher plans add tools such as online booking, long-term follow-up messaging, QuickBooks integration, Carfax-related functionality, customizable workflows, recurring billing, video inspections, commission tracking, and line-item scheduling.

What Is OrbisX?

OrbisX is an all-in-one CRM and business platform built specifically for detailers, tinters, PPF installers, coating installers, wrap businesses, and other automotive professionals.

Its product philosophy is broad: instead of splitting major features across several traditional pricing tiers, OrbisX currently promotes one monthly plan with access to its feature set.

That feature set covers appointments, online booking, estimates, invoices, payments, deposits, expenses, gift cards, staff management, commissions, reporting, inventory, VIN scanning, communication, integrations, route planning, fleet management, GPS, and a substantial collection of automation and AI tools.

Pricing: Urable vs OrbisX

Urable Pricing

  • Express: $70/month
  • Pro: $110/month
  • Enterprise: $183/month

All three Urable plans currently include unlimited users. Express covers many operational basics, including reminders, employee job assignment, Stripe and Square, invoicing, inventory, analytics, vehicle information, inspection photos, and routing.

Pro is the more natural comparison with OrbisX for many established detailers because it adds online booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up, QuickBooks integration, profit analytics, and other features.

Enterprise adds recurring billing, high-definition video inspections, customizable workflows, automated payment-request reminders, commission tracking, and line-item scheduling.

OrbisX Pricing

OrbisX currently advertises $100/month as a limited-time promotional price, with a 30-day free trial and no contract requirement.

The company states that its features are not divided into traditional pricing tiers and that user/staff accounts are unlimited. Some pay-per-use features, credits, SMS services, or third-party apps can still create additional costs.

Pricing Winner

For the lowest starting cost: Urable Express. At $70/month, it is $30 below OrbisX's current promotional price.

For broad feature access around the $100 level: OrbisX. The comparison becomes much tighter against Urable Pro at $110, because OrbisX exposes a large set of automation, booking, staff, marketing, and mobile tools without asking you to move through multiple feature tiers.

Online Booking

Online booking is a major revenue feature for detailers because it lets customers move toward an appointment while you are working on a vehicle instead of waiting for you to answer a call or message.

Urable's online booking is available on Pro and Enterprise. Its Virtual Shop can be configured for in-shop and in-field jobs, booking requests or direct bookings, deposits, scheduling buffers, service availability, estimated duration, and customer vehicles.

OrbisX offers an especially detailed automotive booking form. Published options include staff schedules, blocked dates, packages, upsells, deposits, overlap limits, vehicle-type limits, mobile-vs-shop services, direct booking or approval, waitlists, vehicle selection, and automotive-specific questions such as whether water is available at a mobile job.

Winner: OrbisX, by a narrow margin.

Both are strong, but OrbisX publishes an unusually broad range of booking-form controls and makes booking part of its all-in-one feature set rather than requiring a higher pricing tier.

Vehicle Records, VINs, and Automotive Workflow

This is where both products separate themselves from generic field-service software.

Urable stores vehicle data and images, supports VIN scanning into vehicle profiles, captures inspection photos, schedules vehicle drop-off times, supports multi-vehicle jobs, and can connect VIN data with Carfax functionality on eligible plans.

OrbisX also publishes VIN scanning and vehicle/VIN support, including automotive records tied to invoices and Carfax integration. Its booking form can distinguish vehicle types and let customers choose vehicles as part of scheduling.

Winner: Tie.

Both understand the vehicle as a first-class part of the customer record, which is one of the strongest reasons to choose either platform over a generic CRM.

Inspections and Damage Documentation

Inspection documentation can protect a detailing shop from disputes about scratches, dents, paint defects, wheel damage, interior condition, or other issues that existed before service.

Urable explicitly supports high-definition inspection photos across its plans and adds high-definition video inspections on Enterprise.

OrbisX provides vehicle-documentation and automotive workflow tools within its broad CRM. Its platform also supports staff checklists that can standardize quality-control steps before a vehicle is returned to the customer.

Winner: Urable.

Urable's published plan matrix makes its inspection workflow particularly clear, and Enterprise's dedicated video-inspection capability is a meaningful advantage for shops that prioritize liability documentation.

Estimates, Invoices, Deposits, and Payments

Both platforms are capable of replacing a fragmented sales workflow built from PDFs, text messages, payment links, and manual invoices.

Urable supports branded quotes and invoices, customer approvals, Stripe and Square payments, ACH, saved cards, financing options, multiple payments per job, and deposits through online booking on eligible plans.

OrbisX supports estimates, unlimited invoices, deposits, online payments, saved cards, recurring invoices, auto-charge, gift cards, staff commissions, discounts, taxes, QuickBooks sync, and payment integrations including Stripe, Square, and Clover.

OrbisX estimates can include online acceptance, signatures, templates, alternative options, upsells, and automated follow-up.

Winner: OrbisX, slight edge.

Both are strong, but OrbisX combines a particularly broad set of sales, invoice, payment, gift-card, and estimate features under its current single-plan model.

Automation and Customer Follow-Up

This may be the biggest philosophical difference between the two products.

Urable provides automated reminders and ETA messaging, long-term service-level follow-up on Pro and Enterprise, and customizable workflows on Enterprise. Those workflows can automate repetitive operational steps and pending customer actions.

OrbisX puts AI and automation closer to the center of its marketing. Its Luna assistant is promoted for customer rebooking, inventory management, reports, sales scripts, service descriptions, and estimate visuals.

Published OrbisX automations also include review requests, appointment reminders, upsell suggestions, referral requests, sales-cycle follow-up, no-show follow-up, overbooking prevention, and weather-related notices for mobile services.

Winner: OrbisX.

If automated reactivation and marketing are important parts of your growth strategy, OrbisX has the more aggressive all-in-one automation proposition.

Mobile Detailing: Routing, GPS, and Fleet Management

For mobile detailers, travel time is an operating cost. The software should help reduce wasted miles and keep staff and customers informed.

Urable can create and optimize routes for in-field jobs, assign staff to routes, navigate with Google Maps, and send automated "On My Way" messages with ETA.

OrbisX offers route planning, fleet management, real-time GPS tracking, mileage tracking, customer notifications, and mobile-specific functionality. It also advertises tools for finding nearby past customers when gaps appear in a route.

Winner: OrbisX for fleet visibility; Urable remains strong.

A solo mobile operator can do well with either. A business running several mobile units may find OrbisX's explicit fleet and real-time GPS focus more valuable.

Team Management and Unlimited Users

Both products take a refreshingly team-friendly approach compared with software that charges a large fee for every additional employee.

Urable lists unlimited users on Express, Pro, and Enterprise and includes employee assignment, hours, wages, tips, and other team-management functionality. Commission tracking is available on higher tiers.

OrbisX advertises unlimited user and staff accounts, permissions, scheduling, clock-in/out and break tracking, time-off management, payroll-related tracking, commissions, staff performance analytics, task management, and quality-assurance checklists.

Winner: OrbisX, slight edge for staff-management breadth.

Inventory and Profitability

Detailers can lose margin without realizing it when chemical, coating, film, labor, commission, and rework costs are not tracked.

Urable tracks product inventory and offers service- and job-level profit analysis, labor and material cost visibility, revenue analytics, and commission tracking on eligible plans.

OrbisX supports inventory tracking, low-stock functionality, POS barcode scanning, invoice-level expenses, profit-and-loss visibility, financial reports, forecasts, staff commissions, and AI-oriented inventory tools.

Winner: Tie.

Both offer enough operational depth to move a detailing business beyond simply measuring revenue.

Integrations

Urable publishes integrations with Stripe, Square, QuickBooks, Carfax, and Zapier, among others.

OrbisX publishes a broad integration ecosystem that includes Stripe, Square, Clover, QuickBooks, Carfax, Zapier, Reserve with Google, communications providers, and many other connected tools.

Winner: OrbisX for breadth.

Which Is Better for a Solo Detailer?

Urable Express is compelling if you want to start with an automotive-specific system for $70/month and do not yet need customer self-booking.

OrbisX becomes more attractive if you want online booking, automation, marketing, and its wider feature set immediately and are comfortable with the current $100 promotional price.

Verdict: Urable for lean operations; OrbisX for feature-heavy solo businesses.

Which Is Better for a Fixed Detailing Shop?

Both products are excellent candidates because both are built around automotive businesses rather than generic service calls.

We prefer Urable slightly for a process-focused shop where vehicle records, inspection documentation, scheduling, profitability, and controlled operational workflows are the priority.

Choose OrbisX instead if the shop wants a wider package of marketing, AI, staff, booking, and customer-reactivation tools without moving between plan tiers.

Verdict: Urable, slight edge.

Which Is Better for a Multi-Van Mobile Detailing Business?

Urable has robust route optimization and staff assignment, but OrbisX's published mobile toolkit goes further into fleet operations with real-time GPS and mileage tracking.

Verdict: OrbisX.

Which Is Better for Ceramic Coating, Tint, or PPF?

This is extremely close. Both companies explicitly target automotive appearance businesses beyond basic detailing.

Urable's inspection, proposal, workflow, scheduling, and line-item tools are useful for longer high-ticket installs. OrbisX combines automotive estimating and documentation with booking, AI follow-up, staff workflows, deposits, inventory, and broad marketing tools.

Verdict: Tie — demo both with your real workflow.

Urable vs OrbisX: Final Verdict

Best overall for structured detailing operations: Urable

Best for AI, marketing automation, and broad all-in-one functionality: OrbisX

Best for multi-unit mobile fleet visibility: OrbisX

Best for inspection-centric workflow: Urable

Best low-cost entry point: Urable Express

This is one of the closest comparisons in detailing software because both products were built for the industry.

Urable is our slight overall pick. Its plan structure and published feature matrix make it especially easy to understand how a shop can progress from core CRM operations into booking, profitability, advanced workflows, video inspections, recurring billing, and line-item scheduling.

But OrbisX may be the better value for a business that will actually use its broader feature set. At its current $100 promotional price, unlimited users, booking, staff tools, fleet capabilities, AI-powered follow-up, marketing automation, estimates, payments, inventory, and integrations form an unusually comprehensive package.

The best way to decide is to map your real process—from first lead to booking, vehicle arrival, inspection, work assignment, payment, review request, and rebooking—and test both platforms against it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Urable or OrbisX better for auto detailers?

Both are strong automotive-specific platforms. Urable is our slight overall pick for detailers who prioritize a structured operating workflow, inspection tools, vehicle records, scheduling, routing, and profitability. OrbisX is particularly attractive for businesses that want broad AI, marketing, booking, staff, and fleet features in one plan.

Which is cheaper, Urable or OrbisX?

Urable Express is currently $70/month. Urable Pro is $110/month and Enterprise is $183/month. OrbisX currently advertises a limited-time promotional price of $100/month. Always verify current pricing before buying.

Do Urable and OrbisX support unlimited users?

Yes. Urable currently lists unlimited users across all three plans. OrbisX also advertises unlimited user and staff accounts.

Which is better for mobile detailing?

Both are strong. Urable supports route creation and optimization, staff assignment, and ETA messaging. OrbisX adds a strong focus on fleet management, live GPS, mileage tracking, route planning, and mobile-specific automation.

Do Urable and OrbisX have online booking?

Yes. Urable provides online booking on Pro and Enterprise. OrbisX includes a highly configurable automotive booking form within its published feature set.

Do both platforms support VIN scanning?

Yes. Both publish VIN-related capabilities. Urable supports scanning VINs into vehicle profiles and Carfax functionality on eligible plans. OrbisX lists VIN scanning and vehicle/VIN support among its automotive tools.

Which is better for a growing detailing team?

Both scale well because both advertise unlimited users. Urable may be preferable for teams focused on controlled operational workflows, while OrbisX is especially compelling for teams that want staff tracking, commissions, GPS fleet visibility, marketing, and automation in one system.
