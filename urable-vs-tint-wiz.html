Urable vs Tint Wiz (2026): Which Is Better for PPF & Tint Shops?

Urable and Tint Wiz both target automotive appearance businesses, but they solve the problem from different angles. Urable is a broader operations platform for detailing, PPF, tint, ceramic coating and related services. Tint Wiz is more deeply centered on film businesses, especially window tint and PPF.

Quick verdict

Urable wins overall by a narrow margin for multi-service automotive appearance shops. Its multi-day scheduling, line-item scheduling, video/photo inspections, workflows, employee compensation tools and broader shop-management structure make it the stronger operating system as a business becomes more complex.

Tint Wiz is the better choice for tint-first and film-first shops. Its film-roll inventory, QR/roll tracking, project-level material traceability, flat-glass measurements and Good / Better / Best proposals are unusually well matched to window tint and PPF sales.

Research note: DetailerFit has not completed a hands-on test of both products. This comparison is based on current official vendor pages and help documentation checked September 7, 2026. We separate published product facts from our assessment.

Pricing: Urable Is Cheaper to Enter, Tint Wiz Is Simpler

Urable currently offers three US plans:

Urable planPriceKey additions
Express$70/moUnlimited users, reminders, employee assignment, inventory, invoicing, Stripe/Square
Pro$110/moOnline booking, custom lead forms, long-term follow-up, QuickBooks, profit analytics
Enterprise$183/moVideo inspections, workflows, commissions, line-item scheduling, recurring billing

Tint Wiz currently advertises a single plan at $150/month flat with unlimited users, unlimited projects, a 30-day free trial, no credit card required and no contract.

Which is cheaper in practice?

Urable wins for a small shop that only needs the essentials. Express starts $80/month below Tint Wiz. But the comparison changes once a PPF shop needs Urable's Enterprise-only tools such as video inspections, customizable workflows and line-item scheduling. At that point, Tint Wiz's $150 flat price is actually lower than Urable Enterprise at $183/month.

Important Tint Wiz pricing note: the current Tint Wiz homepage lists $150/month, while some older Tint Wiz pages still show a legacy $100/month price. We use $150 as the current advertised price and recommend verifying the signup amount before purchase.

Window Tint: Tint Wiz Wins

If window tint is the center of the business, Tint Wiz is the more purpose-built platform.

Tint Wiz's current product workflow includes film-roll inventory, roll check-in/check-out, remaining-film tracking by weight or length, film labels, automotive and flat-glass projects, measurements, proposals, deposits, invoices and appointment reminders.

For residential and commercial tint, users can record rooms and window dimensions, calculate square footage and build proposals around multiple film choices. That is a much more specific workflow than a generic CRM normally offers.

Urable also supports window tint and flat glass and explicitly promotes tint inventory, scheduling, reminders and workflow automation. But Tint Wiz's product design is more visibly centered on the material and quoting realities of film installers.

Winner for window tint: Tint Wiz

Choose Tint Wiz if your daily questions sound like: Which film roll did we use? How much is left? Which film option did the customer approve? What were the window measurements?

PPF: Urable Wins for Operations, Tint Wiz Wins for Film Traceability

This is the closest part of the comparison because both vendors explicitly support PPF.

Why Urable is stronger for complex PPF operations

Urable's official PPF documentation highlights multi-day scheduling, automated workflows, video/photo inspections, employee earnings, online booking and line-item scheduling.

Line-item scheduling is particularly useful for a full PPF installation. A shop can break a complex job into components, assign personnel and resources, estimate duration and manage the calendar more precisely. Urable also lets a business assign resources such as a specific bay to PPF or tint work.

Its inspection workflow is also valuable before high-value film installation because pre-existing vehicle damage can be documented with photos or video and retained with the job record.

Why Tint Wiz is stronger for film material traceability

Tint Wiz can assign a specific film roll to a project, track inventory usage and place the film type and roll code on the invoice record. Its current Help Center explicitly describes this as useful for warranty and reference purposes.

That creates a strong chain from:

film roll → project → invoice → warranty reference

For a PPF shop that cares deeply about exactly which roll was used on a vehicle, that is a major advantage.

PPF verdict

Urable wins for project management and team execution. Tint Wiz wins for film-roll traceability. A one- or two-installer film shop may prefer Tint Wiz; a larger operation coordinating complex, multi-day PPF jobs is more likely to benefit from Urable Enterprise.

Film Inventory: Tint Wiz Wins

Both platforms publish inventory capabilities, but Tint Wiz exposes more film-specific detail.

Tint Wiz lets shops:

  • Add individual film rolls
  • Record film type, width and length
  • Track rolls by weight when configured
  • Print QR-coded roll labels
  • Check rolls in and out
  • Assign a specific roll to a project
  • Track remaining inventory
  • Keep the roll code tied to the job/invoice for future warranty reference

Urable says its PPF inventory tools track material levels, low-stock alerts, usage rates and inventory data connected with job costing and invoicing.

Our assessment: Urable's inventory is useful operationally, but Tint Wiz provides the clearer film-roll workflow for tint and PPF businesses.

Quoting & Proposals: Tint Wiz Wins for Sales Presentation

Urable supports branded, interactive quotes and invoices. Its PPF workflow lets customers review quotes, add services and continue into scheduling.

Tint Wiz goes further on proposal presentation. Its current product page emphasizes Good / Better / Best options, upsells and photos. Proposals can be sent through SMS and email and tracked when opened, viewed and approved.

This is well suited to premium services where the goal is not just to quote a single price but to move a customer from an entry package to a higher-value film, wider coverage or an additional ceramic coating service.

Winner for proposals: Tint Wiz

If your sales process depends on showing customers multiple film or coverage options, Tint Wiz has the clearer edge.

Scheduling & Workflow: Urable Wins

Both platforms schedule jobs and keep the team organized, but Urable offers more advanced project scheduling for complex installations.

Urable's Enterprise plan includes customizable workflows and line-item scheduling. Its official documentation describes splitting a complex job into components, assigning personnel and resources and using estimated durations to build the overall schedule.

This matters when a job has multiple stages such as:

  • Vehicle intake and inspection
  • Wash/decontamination
  • Paint correction
  • Disassembly
  • PPF installation by panel or installer
  • Ceramic coating
  • Final inspection and delivery

Tint Wiz has appointment scheduling, projects, notes, tasks and employee assignment, plus automatic appointment confirmations and reminders. For a straightforward tint schedule, that may be more than enough. But Urable provides the better framework when the job itself needs to be broken into multiple coordinated tasks.

Inspections & Liability Protection: Urable Wins

Urable's Enterprise plan includes video inspections, and its PPF documentation explicitly frames inspections as a way to record pre-existing damage before film installation and protect the shop in disputes.

Tint Wiz can store photos, videos, documents and notes inside projects, which is useful for before-and-after documentation. It also supports signed documents such as agreements, warranties and care instructions.

Our assessment: both can preserve useful evidence, but Urable's purpose-built inspection workflow gives it the edge for standardized vehicle intake.

Online Booking & Lead Capture: Urable Has the Clearer Published Self-Booking Workflow

Urable Pro adds custom lead forms and online booking. Urable's PPF page also promotes 24/7 online booking after a quote has been provided.

Tint Wiz has customizable lead capture forms and robust internal scheduling, but its current homepage focuses more on managing appointments and reminders than on customer self-booking as a headline feature.

If automated customer booking is a major requirement, Urable Pro or Enterprise has the clearer published fit.

Customer Communication: Both Are Strong

Urable includes automated reminder messaging on Express, long-term follow-up messaging on Pro and customizable workflows on Enterprise.

Tint Wiz automatically sends appointment confirmation plus 24-hour and 1-hour reminders through email and SMS. It also supports proposal follow-up, review requests and customer communication around projects.

Verdict: Tint Wiz is excellent out of the box for the classic tint sales-and-appointment cycle. Urable is more extensible when a business wants to build a broader automated customer journey.

Team Management: Urable Wins

Both platforms support unlimited users under their current pricing models.

Urable goes further into workforce operations with employee assignment, time tracking, hourly wages, role/service-based commissions, line-item scheduling and resource assignment.

Tint Wiz supports team accounts, notes, tasks and co-worker assignment, but its public product positioning is less focused on payroll/commission management.

For a shop growing from owner-operator to installers, sales staff and front-office employees, Urable has the stronger management layer.

QuickBooks & Integrations

Urable

QuickBooks integration begins on the Pro plan. Urable also publishes integrations with payment providers including Stripe and Square, plus other automotive-business tools.

Tint Wiz

Tint Wiz supports QuickBooks Online and Zapier. Its current product page advertises connectivity through Zapier to more than 6,000 apps.

Tint Wiz's QuickBooks integration should not be treated as a complete two-way mirror of every record type. Its documentation focuses on invoices and payments, and does not currently describe syncing every estimate, inventory or payroll record.

Verdict: neither platform wins automatically here. Tint Wiz makes QuickBooks available within its one-plan structure, while Urable places QuickBooks on Pro and above.

Which Is Better for a Solo Operator?

Urable Express is the better value for most solo operators if the business mainly needs customer management, reminders, inventory, invoicing and job organization. At $70/month with unlimited users, it leaves substantial room below Tint Wiz's $150 price.

But a solo window tinter who actively uses film roll tracking, proposal options, flat-glass measurements and project-level material traceability may still get more practical value from Tint Wiz despite the higher price.

Which Is Better for a Growing Team?

Urable is our pick for a growing multi-service team. The reason is not user limits — both are generous there. It is the operational layer: commissions, line-item scheduling, inspections, workflows, employee assignment and resource planning.

A larger tint-specific company can still make a strong case for Tint Wiz because its flat price does not increase as more users are added.

Which Is Better for a Window Tint Shop?

Winner: Tint Wiz

Tint Wiz is the better fit when window film is the identity of the business. Its flat-glass measurements, film-roll inventory, proposals and tint-specific project structure are hard to ignore.

Which Is Better for a PPF Shop?

Winner: Urable — by a narrow margin

For a PPF shop with multiple employees or complex multi-day installations, Urable's inspections, workflows and line-item scheduling are more valuable. Tint Wiz remains a serious alternative when film-roll traceability and proposal selling matter more than advanced labor scheduling.

Which Is Better for a Detail + PPF + Ceramic Coating Shop?

Winner: Urable

Urable is broader by design and is a better operating system when detailing, PPF, tint, wrap and ceramic coating all need to live under the same process. Its feature set scales more naturally into multi-service shop management.

Final Verdict: Urable Wins Overall, Tint Wiz Wins the Film Specialist Battle

Our overall pick: Urable

Urable is the better all-around platform for most growing automotive appearance businesses. It starts cheaper, supports unlimited users on every plan and offers a deeper operational stack as a shop grows into inspections, complex scheduling, automated workflows and employee compensation.

But Tint Wiz is not the weaker product — it is the more specialized product. For a business built around window tint, PPF and film inventory, Tint Wiz may be the better fit because it understands rolls, measurements, project material traceability and proposal selling at a deeper level.

Choose Urable if your business is becoming a multi-service automotive shop and you need a system to run the team.

Choose Tint Wiz if your business is fundamentally a film shop and the software needs to understand the material as well as the customer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Urable cheaper than Tint Wiz?

Yes at the entry level. Urable Express is $70/month and Pro is $110/month, compared with Tint Wiz's current $150/month flat price. Urable Enterprise is more expensive at $183/month.

Which is better for window tint: Urable or Tint Wiz?

Tint Wiz is our pick for a tint-first business because it combines film-roll inventory, flat-glass measurements, proposal options, project records and tint-specific workflows in one system.

Which is better for PPF: Urable or Tint Wiz?

Urable wins narrowly for complex PPF operations because Enterprise includes video inspections, workflows and line-item scheduling. Tint Wiz is stronger for film-roll traceability and film-focused proposal management.

Do both Urable and Tint Wiz support unlimited users?

Yes. Urable currently lists unlimited users on Express, Pro and Enterprise. Tint Wiz currently advertises unlimited users under its $150/month flat subscription.

Does Tint Wiz track individual film rolls?

Yes. Tint Wiz can track individual rolls, width, length, optional weight-based inventory, QR-coded labels and the specific roll assigned to a project. Its Help Center also describes keeping the roll code connected to invoices for warranty reference.

Does Urable have online booking?

Yes. Online booking is listed on Urable Pro and Enterprise, and Urable promotes a 24/7 booking workflow for PPF shops.

Does Urable have vehicle inspections?

Yes. Video inspections are currently listed on the Enterprise plan. Urable's PPF documentation specifically positions photo/video inspections as a way to document pre-existing vehicle damage.
